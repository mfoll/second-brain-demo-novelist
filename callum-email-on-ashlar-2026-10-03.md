# Callum Email On Ashlar 2026-10-03

[[callum-pryce]]'s reply, on Saturday 3 October 2026, to my forwarding of the Ashlar offer. Summarised; the email is in the agency folder.

## Summary

He advises against. Three reasons:

1. The third draft is the version that will be published, and it is due with Imogen on 29 January. Every week of translation before then comes out of it.
2. Delivery has moved once. Larkspur & Venn agreed without fuss; they would not agree a second time without asking why, and "a translation" is the wrong answer.
3. The option on the next novel runs from acceptance of The Salt Choir. A late Salt Choir delays the third book's deal, which matters more over five years than 9,500 GBP now.

He adds that he understands the cash problem and would rather I applied for every grant going than took the commission. He does not represent me for translation work and would not take commission on it.

## Why It Matters

The strongest case against in [[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]]. His objection is mostly to timing: when I mentioned on 2026-10-20 that a co-translated half could start in April, after delivery, he said "that is a different question" and did not object outright.
