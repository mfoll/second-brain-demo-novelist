# Scrivener (software)

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-07-13, the planning day for the third draft, when I took draft 2 apart and needed to remind myself what the program could and could not export.

## Summary

Describes Scrivener as a word processor and outliner for writers that manages documents, notes, research and metadata in one project, with templates for fiction, non-fiction and screenplays, and export to standard word processors, screenwriting and publishing tools, or TeX for final formatting.

## Why It Matters

Scrivener holds every draft of The Salt Choir. The binder of separate scenes is what made re-sorting the braid into chronology a three-hour job on [[third-draft-planning-day-2026-07-13|13 July]]. Compile and export settings for sending are in [[send-a-draft-to-imogen]]; the live project is [[salt-choir-third-draft-scrivener-project]].

## Caveats

An encyclopedia overview, not the manual. Menu names change between versions; check in the program itself.
