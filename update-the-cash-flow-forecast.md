# Update The Cash-Flow Forecast

Monthly update of [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]], on the first working day of the month, and again whenever a contract date, statement or decision changes. About forty minutes.

## Inputs

- Last month's business account statement (paper file; no account details in the vault).
- Remittance advices from the agency and the centre.
- Any new contract, variation, statement or decision.

## Steps

1. Replace last month's forecast line with actuals. Note any difference over 100 GBP in the comment column.
2. Check every future income line against its trigger. Move any tranche whose trigger has moved; then add one month ([[advances-arrive-late]]).
3. Reserve releases, grants and commissions not yet signed stay on scenario tabs, never in the base case.
4. Recalculate the base case and each scenario. Record the lowest balance and its month.
5. If the base case falls below the 2,000 GBP floor within six months, write the response in [[manage-writing-finances]] Current State: what I will do and by when.
6. Save a dated copy only when the structure of the year changes (as in August 2026). Ordinary monthly updates overwrite.

## Outputs

- Updated spreadsheet.
- One line in the Maintenance Log of [[manage-writing-finances]] if the low point moved by more than 500 GBP.

## Verification

The opening balance of the month matches the bank statement to the pound. If it does not, stop and find out why before forecasting anything.
