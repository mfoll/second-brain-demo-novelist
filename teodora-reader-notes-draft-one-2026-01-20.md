# Teodora Reader Notes On Draft One 2026-01-20

[[teodora-vlasic|Teodora]]'s two-page letter on the first draft, with pencil marks on the paper copy.

## Summary

- Nell is "lonely in a crowded town": we meet dozens of people only through her, so none of them have weight.
- Her attention dropped in chapters 14 to 24 (1925 to 1926), marked with pencil ticks on almost every page.
- The storm chapters are "the best writing you have done and they arrive when I had nearly stopped reading".
- She wanted the choir to matter earlier.

## Why it matters

The same diagnosis as Imogen's ten days later, in reader's terms. Her pencil ticks were the first map of where the book sagged.
