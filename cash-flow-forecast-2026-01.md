# Cash-Flow Forecast 2026 (January Version)

Superseded on 2026-08-28 by [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]], when the delivery date moved. Kept because it shows what the year looked like before the rewrite, and why the gap came as a surprise.

## Summary

Built on 2026-01-12, after the 2025 accounts were drafted and before Imogen's editorial letter. It assumed delivery of The Salt Choir on 2026-09-30, acceptance in October and the 8,000 GBP delivery tranche (6,800 GBP net) in November 2026. On that basis the account never fell below 3,400 GBP in the year, and I treated 2026 as a year without money worries.

## Key Details

- Delivery tranche: November 2026, 6,800 GBP net.
- Lantern Hours royalties: nothing assumed until the April 2027 statement. I did not expect the paperback to earn out the advance in its first six weeks.
- Teaching: three terms at 800 GBP.
- No grant, no translation.

## Caveats

It assumed the first draft would need a revision, not a rebuild. Two weeks later the editorial letter of 2026-01-30 made that assumption wrong, though the forecast was not rebuilt until the variation was signed. The lesson is in [[advances-arrive-late]].
