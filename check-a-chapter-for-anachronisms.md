# Check A Chapter For Anachronisms

How I check a rewritten chapter of The Salt Choir for period errors before it counts as done. About two hours for a 3,500-word chapter.

## Inputs

- The chapter, printed, with its date from [[third-draft-chapter-map]].
- [[saltmarrow-town-bible]], [[saltmarrow-chronology]] and [[character-bible]].
- The open entries of the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]].

## Steps

1. **Facts first** ([[evidence-before-atmosphere]]). Underline every object, technology, institution, price, unit and date. For each, ask whether it existed and was ordinary in a small town in the chapter's year. Check against the town bible first, then the research folder.
2. **Words.** Circle every word or idiom that might be later than the chapter's year. Check doubtful ones in a historical dictionary. The usual offenders: "okay", "teenager", "stress", anything ending in "-wise".
3. **Attitudes.** Read the interior passages only, looking for abstract nouns and modern framings ([[anachronism-of-attitude]]).
4. **Ages and dates.** Every character's age against the character bible; every date against the chronology.
5. **Sea and boats.** Anything maritime is marked for [[ronan-maddox]] to read; I do not pass it myself.
6. **Texture test.** For each surviving period detail, apply [[period-texture-vs-period-trivia]]; cut trivia.
7. **Log.** Every error found gets a line in the log with date, chapter, problem and fix. Errors I cannot settle stay open with a next step.

## Output

A clean chapter, the log updated, and a tick against the chapter in the chapter map.

## Verification

Before a draft goes to Imogen, the log has no open entries for the chapters sent, or each open one is named in the covering note.
