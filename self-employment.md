# Self-Employment

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-07-10, read on 2026-08-04 while working out what a year without an advance means.

## Summary

Self-employment is working for oneself rather than an employer. Tax authorities generally treat someone as self-employed if they say so or if they earn income that needs a tax return; the practical test is whether the activity makes a profit. Hobby activity without profit is usually ignored. Self-employed people are commonly sole proprietors, independent contractors or partners.

## Why It Matters

The profit test is the uncomfortable point for 2026: without a tranche, writing and teaching together come to about 4,000 GBP gross ([[writing-income-overview]]), against costs that do not shrink.
