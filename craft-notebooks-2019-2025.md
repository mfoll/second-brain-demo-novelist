# Craft Notebooks 2019 To 2025

The eleven paper notebooks transcribed in [[craft-notebook-digitisation]]: A5, lined, the same brand since 2016, about 170 pages each.

## Summary

Translation working notes, reading notes, overheard speech and the first notes for Lantern Hours and The Salt Choir. Personal entries are interleaved and stay on paper.

## Why It Matters

They are the record; the vault holds the index ([[craft-notebook-index]]) and the craft notes drawn from them.

## Source Location

Study shelf, second from the top, numbered on the spine. Notebook twelve (live) is in my bag.
