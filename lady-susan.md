# Lady Susan

Jane Austen's epistolary novella, written around 1794 and first published in 1871, in the public domain. Cited from its English Wikipedia article (last edited 2026-10-15), checked on 2026-10-17 before reusing the epistolary handout.

## Summary

An early, complete short work in letters that Austen never sent to a publisher. The letters trace the schemes of its title character.

## Why It Matters

The short example I describe in a minute in class ([[epistolary-voice-handout-2026-01-27]], [[class-session-2026-01-27]]): a whole story in letters, short enough that students can read it in an evening, and a clear case of a letter-writer performing for her correspondents. It is the opposite of what Constance does. She writes to the one person she does not perform for ([[epistolary-voice]]), and the contrast is the most useful thing I can say about letters in a two-hour class.

## Caveats

Described, never quoted in handouts. The article is about the work's history; my reading of the voice is my own.
