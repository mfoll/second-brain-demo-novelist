# Title Call 2026-10-01

Thirty-minute call with [[imogen-lacey|Imogen Lacey]] on Thursday 1 October, set up by her to raise the title before it hardens.

## What she said

- [[martin-okello|Martin Okello]] (sales) read the one-page synopsis ([[salt-choir-synopsis-2026]]) for the 2028 planning meeting and thinks "Choir" tells booksellers this is a gentle, devotional book. He suggested something with the sea or the town in it.
- Imogen herself is neutral. She likes the title but thinks it is "a title for people who have read the book".
- No decision is needed until the autumn 2027 catalogue copy deadline, 2027-04-30 ([[larkspur-catalogue-schedule-2027]]).

## What I said

That I like it, that I know I am attached to it after two years, and that I will put up a proper shortlist rather than defend it by reflex.

## Outcome

[[final-title-decision]] opened as an Idea-stage project. Longlist in [[salt-choir-title-longlist]]. Shortlist of three to Imogen by 2026-12-18.
