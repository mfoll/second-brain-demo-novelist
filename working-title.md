# Working title

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-10-02, the day after the [[title-call-2026-10-01|title call]].

## Summary

Defines a working title as a provisional name for a project, common in film, television, games, music and publishing, sometimes marked "(wt)" in trade press and also called a production or tentative title.

## Why It Matters

A reminder of what "The Salt Choir" has always been on paper: provisional, in the contract and in every email. My attachment to it is two years of use, not a decision. That is the point I need to hold while cutting the [[salt-choir-title-longlist]] to three.
