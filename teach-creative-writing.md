# Teach Creative Writing

My Tuesday evening creative-writing class at the [[gwen-attah|Carrowmede Adult Education Centre]], 19:00 to 21:00, ten sessions a term. It started as income after the Lantern Hours advance had gone and has turned into the place where I find out what I actually know about craft. No end date: as long as the centre runs the class and I can mark the evenings out of the writing week, I teach it.

## Scope

- The class itself: course outlines, handouts, session plans, end-of-term readings, feedback to students.
- My relationship with the centre: the tutor agreement, timesheets, room bookings, Gwen Attah as coordinator, sharing the Tuesday slot with [[kiran-sethi]]'s poetry group.
- What I learn there that feeds the novel. The craft lives in [[point-of-view-and-voice]] and the book notes; this area only records where an idea came from.
- Not here: the fees as money. They are invoiced from here but tracked in [[manage-writing-finances]].
- Student privacy follows [[student-writing-privacy-rules]]: names, broad projects and what they contributed in class. No quoted writing beyond a short phrase with consent, no grades, no circumstances, no contact details.
