# Paperback Cover Option A

> [!warning]
> Rejected on 2026-02-04 in favour of route B (type on a dark ground). Kept as the record of the option and why it lost; see [[paperback-cover-brief-2026]].

## The option

[[felipe-araujo|Felipe Araujo]] redrew the hardback lantern illustration in two colours, full bleed, with the title set small inside the lantern's glow. Lovely, and continuous with the hardback.

## Why it lost

- On a table of paperbacks it read as a children's book at two metres. Martin Okello's sales team tested both on a mock shelf.
- The title was hard to read at thumbnail size online.
- Continuity with the hardback matters less than it seems: most paperback buyers never saw the hardback.

## What I liked and lost

The glow. Route B keeps a small lantern device, which is a fair compromise.
