# Salt Choir Third Draft

The draft that should become the delivered book: chronological from 1924 to 1929, three viewpoints, Constance's chapters as letters, and a four-page storm prologue.

## Outcome

A chronological three-viewpoint third draft with a four-page storm prologue, sent to [[imogen-lacey|Imogen Lacey]] by 2027-01-29. That leaves room for one round of notes before delivery on 2027-03-31 ([[delivery-date-extension]]).

## Current State

- 66,900 words at 2026-10-21 against a target of 105,000 ([[salt-choir-word-count-log]]).
- Part one (1924 to 1926, chapters 1 to 12, about 38,600 words) finished at Ferrow House on the evening of 2026-08-20 ([[writing-retreat-2026]], [[ferrow-house-residency-2026-08-10]]). [[teodora-vlasic]] read it and reported on 2026-09-09 ([[teodora-reads-part-one-2026-09-09]]).
- Part two (1927, chapters 13 to 25) drafted to chapter 19. The storm chapters (21 to 23) are next and are the hardest; the boat is a ten-oar pulling and sailing lifeboat ([[would-saltmarrows-lifeboat-have-had-an-engine-in-1927]]).
- Prologue drafted at four pages (about 1,100 words); [[callum-pryce]] still against it ([[callum-prologue-call-2026-09-17]]).
- On schedule at the [[imogen-progress-call-2026-10-15|progress call of 15 October]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Drafting and revising the third draft. The contract and cash consequences of the timetable are in [[delivery-date-extension]] and [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]]. Title work is separate ([[final-title-decision]]).

## Working Context

The plan agreed after the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|review of 8 July]] is in [[third-draft-plan-2026-07-13]]; the chapter-by-chapter plan is [[third-draft-chapter-map]]. Rules I set for this draft:

- Strict chronology inside the novel; the only displacement is the prologue.
- Nell and Tobias alternate in close third; a Constance letter closes each run of two or three chapters. Nine letters in all.
- Tobias's war arrives in two remembered scenes, not scattered fragments ([[how-much-of-tobiass-war-goes-on-the-page]]).
- Nell sees only what a telegraph clerk could see ([[what-can-nell-know-from-the-telegraph-office]]).
- Target 105,000 words, so roughly 13,000 fewer than draft 2 ([[how-long-should-the-third-draft-be]]).
