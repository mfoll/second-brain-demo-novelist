# Email Interviews Over Recorded Ones

## Stance

Where I have the choice, I give interviews by email. Recorded interviews get a hard stop at sixty minutes and a quote check.

## Scope

Print and online interviews. Not radio or festival events, which are live and are their own discipline.

## Why I Think This

- I write better than I talk, and a translator's habit is to revise. Email lets me say exactly what I mean once.
- The [[long-margin-interview-2026-07-15|Long Margin misquote]] came from the last half hour of a ninety-minute conversation, when I was tired and the interviewer's notes filled the gaps ([[long-margin-interview-recording-2026-06-03]]).
- Priyanka agrees, with the caveat that some editors find email answers flat.

## What Would Change My Mind

If an email interview comes out stiff enough that the magazine cuts it, or if editors start declining email interviews. Priyanka would hear first.

## Implications

- Default answer to interview requests: "happy to, by email".
- If recorded: agree the time limit, ask for quote checking at the start, keep book two to one sentence ([[what-i-say-in-public-about-book-two]]).
