# Vellacott Fund Development Award Guidelines 2026

The Vellacott Fund for Writers' guidelines for the autumn 2026 round of Development Awards, downloaded from the fund's website on 3 August 2026.

## Summary

Awards of up to 6,000 GBP for published writers of fiction, poetry or narrative non-fiction, to support "the development of new work". One round a year, closing 18 October 2026, decisions 11 December 2026. Payment in one sum within 30 days of acceptance.

## Key Details

- Eligibility: at least one book published by a trade publisher; no award from the fund in the previous three years.
- Application: statement up to about 1,000 words (the form accepts more), writing sample up to 5,000 words, budget, publication evidence, one or two references sent directly by referees.
- Budget may cover writing time, research costs or both. Applicants must declare other funding for the project, including advances.
- Panel of three writers and a trustee; the grants officer is secretary without a vote.
