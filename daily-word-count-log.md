# Daily Word Count Log

The spreadsheet where I log each working day: date, draft, chapter, words new or rewritten, first hour kept (yes or no), and a one-word note.

## Summary

Kept since 2025-02-03, the start of the first draft. Figures as of 2026-10-21:

- First draft: about 112,000 words over 46 weeks.
- Draft 2: rebuilt from the first draft between late February and early June 2026.
- Third draft since 2026-07-13: average about 760 words on non-class drafting days; 1,900 a day at Ferrow House.
- First hour kept on 181 of 204 working days in 2026.

## Why It Matters

Honest numbers for the delivery plan and for the [[writing-routine]]. It is also the evidence for [[residencies-are-for-finishing-not-starting]].

## Caveats

"Rewritten" is generous: a lightly revised paragraph counts. Treat the third-draft numbers as progress, not output.

Spreadsheet "Word counts" in the writing folder; one tab per draft.
