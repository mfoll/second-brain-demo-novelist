# Chapel Hymnal, 1926 Printing

A second-hand chapel hymnal, words and tunes, in a 1926 printing, bought for 8 GBP from a market stall on 2025-10-04. Its denomination and place of printing do not matter to the book and are not recorded here.

## Summary

About 800 hymns, arranged by season and occasion, with a section for funerals and memorials and one "for those at sea". Tunes in four-part harmony. A previous owner's pencil marks: alto line underlined in about thirty hymns, dates written beside some, one hymn crossed out.

## Why It Matters

The check for which hymns Constance's choir could have sung in the 1920s, and the physical object behind her alto jokes. The pencilled alto lines are why Constance's first choir letter is about the altos.

## Caveats

One book from one tradition. If a hymn in the novel is quoted, I also check that the words and tune are out of copyright everywhere.
