# Translation Rights Matter More Than Audio For Lantern Hours

## Stance

For Lantern Hours, effort spent on translation sales is worth more than effort spent on the audiobook, in money and in what the book is for.

## Scope

Lantern Hours only. The Salt Choir has a voice-driven structure (letters, three narrators) that might make a better audiobook.

## Why I Think This

- The Italian advance alone (1,200 GBP to me) is most of the audio advance on offer (2,000 GBP gross, against which Callum's 15% applies) ([[casa-velluto-rights-agreement-2026-03]], [[audiobook-offer-email-2025-11]]).
- A novel about translation in translation is its own argument. Sigrun's pitch rests on that ([[lantern-hours-rights-guide-2025]]).
- Audio sales for literary debuts are small; Callum says so himself.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the second translation sale does not come within a year, or if Hana's 2027 list comes with better terms.

## Implications

- I do not push the audiobook while it is paused.
- I answer translators' queries promptly; that is my part in the rights effort.
