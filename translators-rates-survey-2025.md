# Translators' Rates Survey 2025

The annual rates survey of [[aurelio-santangelo]]'s translators' collective (eleven members), 2025 edition, which he sends me though I am no longer a member. Reread on 5 October 2026 for the Ashlar offer.

## Key Details

- Fiction from French into English, 2025: members' fees ranged from 85 to 135 GBP per 1,000 words; median 105.
- Small presses at the lower end; payment usually half on signature, half on delivery.
- Royalty share after earn-out: 1% common; in practice rarely paid.
- Translation grants to publishers often fix the delivery date, as with Ashlar.

## Why It Matters

Ashlar's 9,500 GBP for 88,000 words is about 108 GBP per 1,000 words, at the median. A fair offer, not a generous one ([[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]]).

## Caveats

Eleven translators is a small sample, self-reported, and the collective's members work mostly with small presses.
