# In medias res

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-02-16 while preparing options for the [[editorial-call-2026-02-19|editorial call]].

## Summary

Defines starting a narrative in the chronological middle of the action, with exposition deferred and filled in later through dialogue, flashback or description. The article's example is Hamlet, which opens after the king's death.

## Why It Matters

The braid opened in the middle of things, on the storm night, and filled in 1924 to 1927 afterwards. The article's point that exposition is deferred, not removed, is exactly what the braid did badly with Tobias's war: the deferred material came in too many pieces. See [[where-should-the-salt-choir-begin]].
