# Callum Email On Prologue 2026-07-10

[[callum-pryce|Callum]]'s written dissent on the storm prologue, sent two days after the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|draft two review]] "so it's on the record".

## Summary

Three paragraphs. A prologue showing the lifeboat launching in a storm tells the reader the lifeboat will be lost. Chronological order with that prologue "keeps the spoiler and loses the design". He would rather the book open in 1924 and trust the sea scenes in part one to make the reader afraid for the crew.
