# Epistolary novel

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-01-22 while writing the handout for the spring class session of 27 January on epistolary voice.

## Summary

Describes the epistolary novel as one told through letters between characters, and notes that the term is often stretched to cover novels built from other documents such as diaries and newspaper cuttings, and lately emails and recordings.

## Why It Matters

Background for the three bullet points on the epistolary tradition in the [[epistolary-voice-handout-2026-01-27|class handout]] and for the session itself ([[class-session-2026-01-27]], [[epistolary-voice]]). Reread on 2026-02-02 for The Salt Choir: the book is not an epistolary novel, since one character of three writes letters to one correspondent and nobody replies. The article helped me name what I am doing instead ([[letter-chapters]]) and decide against replies ([[should-constances-sister-write-back]]).

## Caveats

General reference. The handout uses its own short example, [[lady-susan]], rather than the article's list.
