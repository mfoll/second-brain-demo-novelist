# Tariq Mansour Continuity Notes 2026-06-30

A two-page continuity check of draft 2 by [[tariq-mansour|Tariq Mansour]], Imogen's editorial assistant, dated 30 June and attached to her letter of 3 July.

## Summary

Fourteen items. Four concern Tobias: his age in 1924 given as 30 and 31; his enlistment year as 1915 and 1914; a reference to a 1918 leave that clashes with his service elsewhere; and the year his father gave up the coxswain's place. Others: a hymn sung in two different keys, Nell's office hours, one character's name spelled two ways.

## Why it matters

Hard evidence that the braid made Tobias's time hard even for me to keep straight. All fourteen fixed in the third draft and the [[character-bible]] dates checked against them.
