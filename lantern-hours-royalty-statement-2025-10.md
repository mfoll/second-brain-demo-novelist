# Lantern Hours Royalty Statement To 2025-06-30

The first royalty statement for Lantern Hours, covering 1 January to 30 June 2025, received on 2 October 2025. The hardback launch period.

## Key Details

| Line | Basis | Units | Royalty (GBP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hardback, home | 10% of RRP 16.99 | 2,450 | 4,162.55 |
| Ebook | 25% of net receipts (3,269.80) | 1,190 | 817.45 |
| **Earned in period** | | | **4,980.00** |
| Advance paid | | | -12,000.00 |
| **Balance carried forward** | | | **-7,020.00** |

## Why It Matters

Showed the advance about 40% earned after four months of hardback sales. On this rate, earning out would have taken until 2027. It is the baseline against which the paperback's effect is measured in [[lantern-hours-earn-out-check]]. The launch itself is in [[hardback-launch-2025]].

## Caveats

No reserve on hardback lines in this period, which Bethan later said is unusual and lucky.
