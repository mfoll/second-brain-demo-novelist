# Maya Okonjo Blog Review 2025-03

[[maya-okonjo|Maya Okonjo]]'s 2,400-word review of Lantern Hours on her blog, published 2025-03-11. Saved as a PDF on 2025-03-12.

## Summary

- Reads the novel as a book about fidelity: to a text, to a dead teacher, to one's own voice.
- Singles out the dictionary scene and the narrator's refusal to "improve" her mentor's draft.
- Criticises the middle, and the mentor's letters as too explanatory.
- Recommends it to reading groups, which Priyanka quoted on the paperback.

## Why It Matters

The phrase Priyanka used on the paperback cover came from here, with Maya's agreement. Her criticism of the mentor's letters was in my head when I wrote Constance's letters for The Salt Choir ([[constance-leal]]): a letter has to want something from its reader, not explain.
