# Vellacott Eligibility Call 2026-09-08

Twenty-minute call with [[ana-lucia-ferreira]], grants officer at the Vellacott Fund, booked through the fund's website.

## My Question

The Salt Choir is under contract with an advance of 24,000 GBP. The [[vellacott-development-award-guidelines-2026|guidelines]] say awards support "the development of new work", and do not say whether contracted work counts.

## Her Answer

Contracted books are eligible. The panel wants to see the full advance and its payment schedule, and a budget that shows what the award pays for that the advance does not. A writer whose tranche has moved and who has a gap in the writing period is, in her words, "exactly what Development Awards are for". Recorded as the answer to [[does-the-vellacott-award-fund-a-contracted-book]].

## Other Advice

- Keep the statement under 1,000 words even though the form allows more.
- One reference is enough if it is the right one; the editor of the book is the right one.
- Do not apply for time and research costs together unless both are essential.

All three taken.
