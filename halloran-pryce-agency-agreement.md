# Halloran Pryce Agency Agreement

My representation agreement with the Halloran Pryce Agency, signed in January 2023 when [[callum-pryce|Callum Pryce]] took on Lantern Hours.

## Key Details

- Commission: 15% on home-market income, 20% on translation and other foreign income, 15% on audio.
- All publisher payments go to the agency's client account and are paid on within ten working days, with a statement from [[bethan-okafor|Bethan Okafor]].
- Either side may end it on three months' notice; the agency continues to collect on contracts it negotiated.
- Covers my own writing only. Translation commissions I take as a translator are outside it, which matters for [[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]]: Callum advises on it as a favour, not as my agent for that deal.

## Why It Matters

Every advance figure in the vault is gross. What reaches me is 85% of home income. The [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]] uses net figures.
