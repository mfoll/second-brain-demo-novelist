# Tobias Arden

Viewpoint character of The Salt Choir from draft 2: the lifeboat coxswain, back from the war with shell shock. A character of the novel; ages and household are in [[character-bible]].

## Who he is

- Thirty-one in 1924. Fisherman's son; served in the infantry from 1915 to 1918; came home in 1919 and went back to the boats.
- Takes the coxswain's place in April 1924, the post his father held. The crew elected him; half the committee did not want him because of his "nerves".
- Good with a boat, good with men, bad with silence and with sudden noise. Has good weeks and bad weeks, not a constant state ([[femi-adewale]]'s correction).

## Role

He is the sea and the crew. In November 1927 he takes the ten-oar pulling and sailing lifeboat out to a drifter on the bar ([[would-saltmarrows-lifeboat-have-had-an-engine-in-1927]]); the boat capsizes on the return and five of the crew are lost. He survives ([[should-tobias-survive-the-storm]]). Part three is his inquiry, the town's judgement, and the motor boat promised for 1928 that he will not command.

## The war on the page

Two remembered scenes only in the third draft, chapters 7 and 17, each complete and placed in a dated present that triggers it ([[how-much-of-tobiass-war-goes-on-the-page]]). In draft 2 the war came in fragments across three timelines and readers lost track of it ([[draft-two-review-2026-07-08]]).

## Voice

Close third, but with more [[narrative-distance]] than Nell, and the distance widens when the war takes him. Locals say "nerves", never "shell shock"; he does not name it at all ([[period-diction]], [[shell-shock]]).
