# Electrical telegraph

## Summary

Electrical telegraphy sent text messages as electric signals over wire, point to point, and was the main system from the 1840s until late in the twentieth century. The article presents it as the first electrical telecommunications system, faster than carrying messages by hand.

## Why It Matters

Background for [[nell-garrick]]'s work at the Saltmarrow telegraph office. Read in February 2025, before her first chapter, together with [[could-a-woman-work-as-a-telegraph-clerk-in-1924]]. In February 2026 it caught an error in draft 2: a "teleprinter" in a small 1920s office, replaced by a sounder and key ([[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]]).

## Key Details

- Point-to-point messaging over wire, in wide use from the 1840s.
- Still in ordinary use in the 1920s and long after.

## Caveats

A broad history across countries and decades. What equipment a small office had in 1924 is my judgement, not the article's.
