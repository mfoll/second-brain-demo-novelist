# Lantern Hours Royalty Statement To 2026-06-30

Larkspur & Venn's royalty statement for Lantern Hours covering 1 January to 30 June 2026, sent through the Halloran Pryce Agency and received on 2 October 2026. The statement on which the book earned out.

## Summary

The first statement to include the paperback (published 2026-05-14) and the Italian rights advance. It closes the 4,610 GBP unearned balance carried from the previous statement and shows 1,140.00 GBP payable on 2026-10-31, after a reserve against returns.

- No audio line: the audiobook is paused ([[audiobook-edition]]).

## Why It Matters

This is the answer to "has Lantern Hours earned out?" Yes, on this statement. It closes [[lantern-hours-earn-out-check]] and puts 969 GBP into November in the [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]], the only real income in the first month of the gap. It also means every future sale pays me directly, which changes how I think about the bookshop readings in [[bookshop-reading-tour]]: they now earn a little as well as sell.

## Caveats

- The reserve is held, not lost. [[bethan-okafor]] expects most of it released over the next two statements, depending on returns from the paperback's first shipment ([[how-big-a-reserve-against-returns-is-reasonable]]).
- The Italian rights income is the full Casa Velluto advance less the publisher's 20%. If the Italian edition earns out its own advance, further income will come through later statements, not soon.
- I reconciled it against the agency's remittance advice and the sales figures [[priyanka-desai]] quoted in July, using [[reconcile-a-royalty-statement]]. Paperback units match within 40 copies; the gap is timing of a late-June shipment.
