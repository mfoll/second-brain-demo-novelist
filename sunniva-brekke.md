# Sunniva Brekke

Poet, member of the monthly writing group. Two collections; she teaches poetry workshops in schools.

## How we work together

She reads for sound and for excess. In the group she reads chapters aloud before commenting, which is unsettling and useful: anything sentimental or slack is obvious once spoken.

## What she contributed

- Notes on the memorial chapter in September ([[sunniva-brekke-notes-on-memorial-chapter-2026-09]]): cut every adjective near a hymn, and let the hymn words do the work. She marked eleven cuts; I took nine.
- She is the reason I read Constance's letters aloud before I count them finished.
