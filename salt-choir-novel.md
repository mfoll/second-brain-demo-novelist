# The Salt Choir Novel

The umbrella for my second novel: literary historical fiction set in Saltmarrow, an invented herring port and chapel town, between 1924 and 1929, told through Nell Garrick, Tobias Arden and Constance Leal, with the November 1927 storm and the loss of the lifeboat at its centre. Working title throughout; see [[final-title-decision]].

## Outcome

An accepted manuscript of The Salt Choir delivered to Larkspur & Venn by 2027-03-31 (moved from 2026-09-30 by the contract variation of 2026-08-28). Target length 105,000 words.

## Working Context

The book in one paragraph, as I keep it for myself: Saltmarrow, 1924. Nell Garrick, nineteen, leaves the gutting crews for a clerk's stool at the telegraph office. Tobias Arden, back from the war and not right since, takes the lifeboat coxswain's place his father held. Constance Leal, a vicar's widow, runs the Bethel chapel choir and writes to her sister. In November 1927 the lifeboat goes out to a drifter on the bar and five of its crew are lost; Tobias survives. The choir sings at the memorial. The last part is the town after: the inquiry, the promised motor boat, the herring failing.
