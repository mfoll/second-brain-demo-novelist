# Kipper

## Summary

A kipper is a whole herring split from tail to head along the back, gutted, salted or pickled, and cold-smoked over smouldering wood chips. The article covers the method, its history and where kippers are eaten.

## Why It Matters

Settled a small argument with myself in February 2025: Saltmarrow's yards salt and barrel their herring for export; they do not smoke. A smokehouse would need different buildings, different smells and different workers. I gave the town one small smokehouse for local sale at the back of Hallett's yard, mentioned once, and left it there. Recorded in the [[saltmarrow-town-bible]].

## Key Details

- Splitting, gutting, salting and cold smoking are separate steps.
- Smoked fish and salted barrelled fish are different trades.

## Caveats

Background only; no claim about any particular town or firm.
