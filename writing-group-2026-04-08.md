# Writing Group 2026-04-08

Monthly writing group. The group read chapter 1 of the braided draft 2: the storm night from the telegraph office, Nell at the desk, about 4,800 words.

## Responses

- [[marcus-ellery|Marcus]] liked it best of anything I had shown them: an opening that "starts the clock". His written note ([[marcus-ellery-notes-on-storm-chapter-2026-04-08]]) added the line I keep returning to: once the reader knows who dies, every later chapter has to make them want to know how.
- [[teodora-vlasic|Teodora]] liked it but asked who the coxswain was and when he had come back from the war; the chapter mentions both, in passing, in two different years.
- [[sunniva-brekke|Sunniva]] and one other member could not place Tobias in time from the chapter.

## What I took then

Marcus's line as encouragement. The confusion about Tobias as a matter of clarity, fixed with dates in two places.

## What I see now

Both were early warnings of what the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|July review]] found: the storm-first opening spends the suspense, and Tobias's time is hard to follow in a braid. See [[salt-choir-structure-history]].
