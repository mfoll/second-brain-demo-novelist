# Ingrid Solheim

Director of the community choir I joined in September 2025 to hear four-part hymn singing from inside rather than from a recording. Rehearsals on Wednesday evenings, about forty singers, no audition.

## How we work together

- I asked in August 2025 whether I could join as a research singer; she said nobody in her choir is a research singer, and put me in the altos. See [[joined-the-wednesday-choir-2025-09-10]].
- She teaches parts by ear, line by line, then puts them together. Watching her build a hymn from four separate lines is where Constance's rehearsal scenes come from, and where the altos in Constance's first letter come from.
- She answered a set of questions in October 2025 about how a 1920s chapel choir might have been run (who chose the hymns, who paid for music, how a memorial programme was agreed). Her answers were from experience and general knowledge, not research, and are marked that way in [[wednesday-choir-rehearsal-notes]].
- I sang in the [[remembrance-concert-2025-11-09]].

## Current threads

- I am still singing, Wednesdays, through the autumn 2026 term, missing the odd week for readings.
- She has asked to read Constance's letters when the book is out. Not before.

## Boundaries

A choir director and, by now, a friendly acquaintance. The choir's members are not named in the vault.
