# Does The Salt Choir need a 1929 coda?

## Question

The third draft ends with Constance's last letter in December 1929 (chapter 33). Should there be a short coda after it, in Nell's or Tobias's present, to close the frame that the prologue opens?

## Current Answer

Deferred until part three exists. Imogen suggested it on the [[imogen-progress-call-2026-10-15|call of 15 October]] as an answer to Callum: if the prologue is the launch, a coda could be the new motor boat launching in 1929, mirroring it. I am wary. It risks symmetry for its own sake ([[structure-serves-the-reader-not-the-writer]]), and Constance's letter is a better last voice.

## Revisit when

Part three is drafted (target 2027-01-15). Decide during the [[full-draft-read-through]].
