# Final Title Decision

Choosing the published title of the book that has been called The Salt Choir since January 2025.

## Outcome

A final title agreed with Larkspur & Venn before the autumn 2027 catalogue copy deadline, which [[imogen-lacey|Imogen]] gives as 2027-04-30 ([[larkspur-catalogue-schedule-2027]]).

## Current State

- Not started in earnest. Opened after the [[title-call-2026-10-01|call of 1 October]], when Imogen passed on [[martin-okello|Martin Okello]]'s view from sales that "Choir" reads as a quiet, devotional book and may shelve it wrongly.
- Twenty-four candidates on the [[salt-choir-title-longlist]]. My preference is still The Salt Choir. Imogen is neutral; Callum likes it.
- No public use of any title: the [[long-margin-interview-2026-07-15|Long Margin interview]] printed the working title before the publisher had announced it, which is part of why it now feels fixed in my head and not in anyone else's.

## Scope and Boundaries

Title only. Cover, jacket copy and the catalogue paragraph come after acceptance and belong to Larkspur & Venn with me consulted. Not to be decided before the third draft exists: the title should name the book I actually deliver.

## Next Actions

- [ ] Cut the longlist to a shortlist of three, with one line each on what the title promises, before 2026-12-18.
- [ ] Ask Teodora and the writing group to rank the shortlist blind in January.
- [ ] Agree the title with Imogen after acceptance, and in any case by 2027-04-30.
