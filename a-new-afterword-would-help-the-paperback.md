# A New Afterword Would Help The Paperback

> [!warning]
> Superseded on 2026-02-02 by the decision to write a reading group guide with a short translation note instead ([[reading-group-guide]]). Kept because the reasoning came back in the guide.

## Stance (as held in January 2026)

The paperback should carry a 3,000-word afterword on translating a dead friend's work, to give hardback readers a reason to buy again.

## Why I dropped it

- Priyanka: hardback readers rarely rebuy; the paperback's readers are new, and groups want questions, not an essay.
- Imogen: an afterword would add 12 pages and push the price.
- Me, on reflection: an afterword explains the novel, and the reviews already said the mentor's letters explained too much ([[maya-okonjo-blog-review-2025-03]]).

## What survived

The 600-word note in the guide ([[reading-group-guide-final-2026]]) is the afterword's best paragraph, cut to size.
