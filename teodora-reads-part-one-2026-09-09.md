# Teodora Reads Part One 2026-09-09

[[teodora-vlasic|Teodora Vlasic]] reported on part one of the third draft (prologue and chapters 1 to 12, about 38,600 words) after the writing group on Wednesday 9 September. Her written notes: [[teodora-reader-notes-part-one-2026-09-09]].

## What she said

- The chronology works. She always knew where and when she was, which she could not say of draft 2.
- Constance's letters are the best thing in it; chapter 8 (Christmas, the altos) made her laugh aloud.
- Tobias's first war scene (chapter 7) lands because it is whole.
- She read the whole part "waiting for the boat". Every launch scene made her tense. She meant it as praise.
- Chapter 11 (the wireless at the Institute) is the weakest; it is there for period colour.

## What I did with it

- Chapter 11 marked for cutting by a third in revision.
- Sent her "waiting for the boat" to Imogen and Callum. Imogen read it as the prologue working. Callum read it as the prologue giving the game away, and said so on the [[callum-prologue-call-2026-09-17|call of 17 September]]. Logged under [[should-the-salt-choir-keep-the-storm-prologue]].
