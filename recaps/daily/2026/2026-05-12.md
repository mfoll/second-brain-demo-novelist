# Tuesday 12 May 2026

## The engine has to go

A reading-room day at the Merrin with [[eluned-parry]] ([[merrin-archive-visit-2026-05-12|visit note]]). Three small stations' service records, all still launching pulling and sailing boats in the late 1920s; one district inspector's letter recommending a motor boat "when funds permit". Ronan was right in March. I did not decide at the table; I wanted two days to make sure I was not just relieved to have an answer.

## What it costs

If the boat has no engine, both storm chapters of draft 2 need rewriting a month before the draft goes to Imogen on 12 June. The alternative, sending them with a note, I rejected over lunch on the harbour wall: Imogen reads a storm, not a note.

## Small things
