# Wednesday 8 July 2026

## Draft two review: the braid partly reversed

Two hours with Imogen and Callum on draft 2. Imogen's reading, and mine since late June: the storm told first is spent by the time it returns, and readers lose Tobias's war across three timelines. Decided: back to chronological order, 1924 to 1929; keep the three viewpoints and Constance's letters; keep a storm prologue of four pages at most, no names, no outcome. This partly reverses what we decided on the [[editorial-call-2026-02-19|editorial call of 19 February]]. Full notes in [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|the review]].

## Callum dissents

Callum argued for twenty minutes that any storm prologue gives the ending away and that the compromise keeps the braid's spoiler without its design. Imogen called it "the right worry" but sided with keeping the prologue. Not settled: [[should-the-salt-choir-keep-the-storm-prologue]].

## The date
