# Wednesday 23 April 2025

## Interview with Dorothy Pengelly

The [[dorothy-pengelly-interview-2025-04-23|oral history interview]] with [[dorothy-pengelly]] took the whole afternoon: consent read aloud and signed first, then an hour and fifty minutes on her mother's gutting crew, hands, singing and the season. She was sharper than I was by the end. She asked me to switch the recorder off twice; nothing from those minutes is in my notes.

## What I decided on the way home

The crew of three and the singing go into Nell's first chapter as texture, unattributed. No direct quotation, as agreed in the [[dorothy-pengelly-consent-form|consent form]]. Her mother's name and the firms stay out of the vault and the book.

## What still nags

How much of a living person's family memory a novel may absorb before it becomes her story told without her. Opened as [[how-much-of-dorothys-story-can-the-novel-use]]. Morning draft: 820 words on chapter 4 before I left, which felt like nothing next to the afternoon.
