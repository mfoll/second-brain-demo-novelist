# February 2026

## The Salt Choir becomes a braid

The month's decision. Imogen's letter of 30 January on the first draft ([[imogen-editorial-letter-2026-01-30]]) said the storm came too late and Nell alone could not carry the town. On the [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-02-19|19 February call]] I proposed rebuilding the book as a braid of three viewpoints opening on the storm night, and Imogen accepted. The braid was reversed in July ([[draft-two-review-2026-07-08]]); anyone asking why should read [[salt-choir-structure-history]] rather than this month alone.
