# Salt Choir First Draft

The first complete draft of The Salt Choir: linear, 1924 to 1929, told entirely from [[nell-garrick|Nell Garrick]]'s point of view in close third person.

## Outcome

A complete first draft (about 112,000 words, linear, Nell's point of view only) sent to [[imogen-lacey|Imogen Lacey]] on 2025-12-19. Reached.

## Current State

- Closed. The manuscript is kept as a pointer in [[salt-choir-first-draft-manuscript]]; nothing in it is current except the scenes carried into later drafts.
- Imogen's response is the [[imogen-editorial-letter-2026-01-30|editorial letter of 30 January 2026]]: the storm arrives too late (chapter 31 of 41, about 74% through), and Nell alone cannot carry the town.
- [[callum-pryce]] and [[teodora-vlasic]] had both read it in January 2026 and said versions of the same thing, more kindly ([[callum-notes-on-draft-one-2026-01-16]], [[teodora-reader-notes-draft-one-2026-01-20]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Drafting only. Research ran in parallel under [[saltmarrow-research]], characters under [[character-bible]] (finished 2025-06-27, partly in response to what this draft needed).

## Working Context

How it went, briefly:

- Started 2025-02-03 from a 3,000-word opening I had written in late 2024 and the pitch paragraph ([[salt-choir-pitch-paragraph-2025-03]]).
- Halfway point at 56,000 words on 2025-07-04 ([[first-draft-halfway-2025-07-04]]), behind my own plan by about three weeks because the [[hardback-launch-2025]] readings ate the spring.
- [[wendy-farrant]] read chapters 1 to 20 in September and told me over lunch on 2025-10-08 that Nell was "listening at keyholes" to report a town she could not see ([[wendy-farrant-lunch-2025-10-08]]). I noted it and kept going. She was right.
- The writing group read the opening chapter on 2025-11-12 ([[writing-group-2025-11-12]]).
- Finished 2025-12-17; sent 2025-12-19 after a fast read-through, which I would not do again ([[full-draft-read-through]]).

What survives into draft three: Nell's telegraph office chapters almost whole, the curing yard scenes, the chapel rehearsals, and about a third of the storm night. What went: Nell's eavesdropping on the lifeboat crew, her improbable presence at the inquiry, and 14 chapters that existed to move her into rooms she had no reason to be in. Details in [[salt-choir-cut-list]].
