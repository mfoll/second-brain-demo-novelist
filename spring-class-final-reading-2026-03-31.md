# Spring Class Final Reading 2026-03-31

Last session of the [[evening-class-spring-2026|spring 2026 term]]. Fourteen students read for about seven minutes each; it ran to 21:20. By custom I read last, one page.

## What I Read

One page of the new storm opening of The Salt Choir, written in March for the [[braided-structure-rewrite]]: the lifeboat going out, seen from the harbour wall. I chose it because it was the newest thing I had and I wanted to hear it aloud.

## The Engine

Afterwards, over the chairs, [[ronan-maddox]] asked who had started the engine. In draft 2 the Saltmarrow lifeboat was a motor boat. His view, from forty years as a harbour pilot and a lot of reading, was that a small station in the late 1920s would much more likely still have had a pulling and sailing boat, with oars and a lug sail, and that motor lifeboats came to the larger stations first.

I wrote it on the back of my page and put a query in the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]] the next morning. It was settled at the archive in May: see [[would-saltmarrows-lifeboat-have-had-an-engine-in-1927]]. The storm chapter is now written with a ten-oar boat, which is a better chapter, because the crew are rowing.
