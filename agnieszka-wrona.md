# Agnieszka Wrona

Student in the spring 2026 term only. A poet moving into prose, who came across the corridor from [[kiran-sethi]]'s poetry group.

## In Class

Agnieszka had the best ear in the spring room for rhythm and the least patience with plot, which she said was the reason she came. Her project is a short novel, or a long story, told in numbered sections. In the session on one scene in three viewpoints she wrote the same kitchen in three lengths of sentence rather than three people, which was not the exercise and was better than most attempts at it. I used that afterwards, with her agreement and without her text, as an example of voice carried by syntax.

She found the second-person evening pointless and told me so on the feedback form; she was right ([[spring-2026-class-feedback-summary]]).

## Since

She did not enrol for autumn 2026 because she had no draft of 3,000 words yet, which the revision term required. She is back in Kiran's group and has said she may return for the spring 2027 term on dialogue and scene.
