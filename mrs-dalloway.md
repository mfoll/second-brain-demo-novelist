# Mrs Dalloway

## Summary

Virginia Woolf's novel, published on 14 May 1925, follows one June day in the life of Clarissa Dalloway in post-war London as she prepares a party, and interleaves it with the day of Septimus Warren Smith, a veteran damaged by the war. The Wikipedia article gives the publication date and setting; I reread the novel itself (my own copy) in May 2025 and used the article for dates and context.

## Why It Matters

Two things for The Salt Choir. First, Septimus: a portrait of war damage from 1925 written without the vocabulary we now use, which is the register I want for [[tobias-arden]] and the town's word "nerves". Second, the way the narration passes from one mind to the next in the street, which I used in class as the clearest demonstration of [[free-indirect-style]]. It also sits behind [[the-single-day-novel]], a structure I considered for the storm chapters and rejected.

## Key Details

- Published 14 May 1925; set on a single day in June 1923.
- Two centres of consciousness who never meet; the link is thematic and spatial.
- The novel is in the public domain in most of the world; my copy is a 1990s paperback.

## Caveats

The Wikipedia summary is background; the craft observations are mine, from the novel. Its London is nothing like a herring port, and its class register is not my characters'.
