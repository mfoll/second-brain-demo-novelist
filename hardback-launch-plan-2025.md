# Hardback Launch Plan 2025

[[priyanka-desai|Priyanka Desai]]'s publicity plan for the Lantern Hours hardback, three pages, received 2025-01-09.

## Summary

- Proofs to forty reviewers, bloggers and booksellers in November 2024; finished copies to twenty more in January.
- Launch at Quire & Candle on publication day, 2025-02-27.
- Five bookshop readings and two library talks in March and April; one regional radio interview.
- A pitch to translated-fiction readers and translators' networks rather than to general fiction media, because "a translator's novel" is the hook.
- No festival bookings in 2025: the summer programmes were already full when the book was announced.

## Why It Matters

The template for the [[paperback-campaign-plan-2026|paperback plan]]. It was right about the translators' networks: the first 500 copies went largely to people who knew my name from title pages.
