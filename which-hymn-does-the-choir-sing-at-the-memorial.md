# Which Hymn Does The Choir Sing At The Memorial?

Chapter 25 of the third draft is the December 1927 memorial for the five lost lifeboatmen, where Constance's choir sings. Which hymn, and can I name it?

## Constraints

- It must be in the public domain, words and tune, if any line is quoted. A few words only in any case.
- It must have been in chapel hymnals by the mid-1920s. My [[chapel-hymnal-1926]] is the check.
- The choice should be argued over by the committee, as the [[remembrance-concert-2025-11-09]] taught me: Constance wants one hymn, the committee another.
- It should not be the obvious sea hymn every reader expects.

## Options so far

1. A well-known sea hymn: the committee's choice, rejected by Constance in her letter as "what the newspapers will expect".
2. An evening hymn from the hymnal that Constance has been teaching the choir in four parts since 1925, so the memorial is the first time the town hears what she built.
3. No named hymn at all, only its first line paraphrased.

## Leaning

Option 2, with option 1 as the committee's lost argument. Decide by the end of part two; check with [[ingrid-solheim]] whether the four-part arrangement is plausible for a town choir.
