# Tidewrack Programme Meeting 2026-03-05

Forty-minute video call with [[orla-fenwick|Orla Fenwick]] to shape the panel she had invited me to on 2026-03-02.

## Agreed

- Title "Other people's voices: translation and historical voice". Three panellists, a chair, sixty minutes.
- Chair: [[kwabena-osei|Kwabena Osei]]. Orla's choice; I was glad.
- Fee 250 GBP plus standard-class rail ([[tidewrack-speaker-agreement-2026]]). Festival shop to stock the paperback, which would be five weeks old.
- I would not talk about The Salt Choir beyond a sentence. Orla was fine with that.

## Rejected

- A solo event on Lantern Hours. Orla offered it; I declined because a paperback debut alone draws a small room, and a panel built around a question draws the festival's regulars.
