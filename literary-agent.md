# Literary Agent

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2026-04-18), read on 2025-01-20.

## Summary

A literary agent represents writers and their work to publishers and to theatre and film producers, and helps sell and negotiate the deals. Most represent novelists, screenwriters and non-fiction writers. The article covers how agents are paid (a commission on the author's income) and their role between author and publisher.

## Why It Matters

Twenty-two years as a translator, I was paid flat fees by publishers with no agent between us. Read in January 2025 when Callum and I were preparing the Salt Choir proposal, to understand what I was actually paying 15% for ([[halloran-pryce-agency-agreement]]). The answer turned out to be the parts I could not do: the option clause struck back, audio reserved, the 80% translation share. It also explains why the Ashlar commission sits outside his remit ([[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]]).

## Caveats
