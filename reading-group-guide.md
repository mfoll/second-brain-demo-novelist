# Reading Group Guide

A reading group guide for Lantern Hours, printed in the back of the paperback and posted on the Larkspur & Venn site.

## Outcome

Guide printed in the paperback and posted online. Completed 2026-04-24.

## Current State

- Final text: ten discussion questions, a 600-word note on translation, and a short list of further reading ([[reading-group-guide-final-2026]]). Four pages in the paperback.
- Signed off by [[tariq-mansour|Tariq Mansour]] on 2026-04-24, in time for the printer.
- Used by at least nine groups through [[rosa-mbatha|Rosa Mbatha]]'s library network; their comments are in [[reading-group-feedback-2026]].

## Scope and Boundaries

The guide for Lantern Hours. Any guide for The Salt Choir belongs to that book's publication, years away.

## Working Context

I tested a first draft of the questions on Rosa's library group on 2026-02-26. Two lessons: questions that ask readers to judge Ines (the narrator) start better conversations than questions about the mentor, and nobody wants a question about the title. I cut three questions and rewrote two. The translation note was the part Tariq expected readers to skip and the part the groups talked about most. I took out a question on the ending because it gave the ending away to readers who start with the guide.
