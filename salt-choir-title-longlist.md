# Salt Choir Title Longlist

Every candidate title for the book so far, with my one-line verdict. Started 2025-02; reopened after the [[title-call-2026-10-01|title call of 1 October 2026]]. Ranking happens in [[final-title-decision]], not here.

## Still in

- **The Salt Choir**: the working title since January 2025. A town, a choir, the sea. Sales think it sounds quiet.
- **Saltmarrow**: a place name as title. Clean, but it says nothing about people.
- **The Bar**: the sandbar where the boat goes over. Too short; reads as a pub.
- **A Hymn for the Launchers**: the women on the ropes. Too long.
- **What the Wire Said**: Nell's book, not the whole book.
- **Ten Oars**: the boat itself. Plain and physical. Teodora likes it.
- **The November Business**: Constance's phrase. Ironic in the right way.
- **Night of the Maroons**: sounds like a thriller.

## Out, and why

- Anything with "storm", "tide" or "sea" as the first noun: the shelf is full of them.
- **The Widows' Choir**: tells the ending.
- **Bethel**: reads as religious fiction.
- **The Lifeboat**: a children's book.
- Twelve others scored out in the notebook, not worth retyping.

## Criteria

A title should name the book I deliver, survive translation (I tried each in French: [[back-translation-test]]), not give away the storm's outcome, and not have been used for a novel in the last twenty years.
