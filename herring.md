# Herring

## Summary

Wikipedia's general article on herring, the forage fish of the order Clupeiformes and its several species. I read the lead and the sections on fisheries and preservation; my notes below are limited to what I needed.

## Why It Matters

The first thing I read for The Salt Choir, in January 2025, to understand why a whole town's year turns on a fish that comes and goes in shoals. It gave me the season's shape for the [[saltmarrow-town-bible]]: the fleet following the shoals, the curing yards working flat out while the fish are in, and the slack winter. The open anachronism entry on herring prices on the wire (chapter 16) points back here.

## Key Details

- Herring are a group of species, not one fish.
- The article is a natural-history and fisheries overview; the season and trade of a single port are not its subject.

## Caveats

General natural history and fisheries background; it says nothing about any particular port, and Saltmarrow's season is my invention built on the general pattern.
