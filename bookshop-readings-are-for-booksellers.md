# Bookshop Readings Are For Booksellers

## Stance

A bookshop reading is worth doing for what the bookseller does afterwards, not for the copies sold on the night. Judge it by reorders, not attendance.

## Scope

Independent shops with a bookseller who reads. Not chains, not online events, not festivals, where the audience is the point.

## Why I Think This

- Event sales on the paperback tour were 9 to 24 copies a night ([[lantern-hours-publicity-ledger]]). Barely worth the train.
- Two of four shops reordered within a fortnight; Driftline reordered twelve ([[driftline-books-reading-2026-09-24]]). A bookseller who has met the author hand-sells for months; Rhiannon did it for the hardback until Easter 2025.
- Martin Okello says independent shops' sell-through on Lantern Hours is ahead of the chains in proportion to stock.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the shops that hosted readings show no better sell-through over six months than shops that did not. Martin could tell me; I have not asked for that breakdown.

## Implications

- Choose shops for their booksellers, not their rooms.
- Thank the bookseller in writing afterwards. It costs nothing.
- For The Salt Choir, fewer readings, all in shops that will hand-sell.
