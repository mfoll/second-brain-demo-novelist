# Earning Out

A book earns out when the royalties credited to it equal the advance already paid. Until then the royalty account shows an unearned balance and nothing more is paid; after it, every royalty is paid to the author on the next statement.

## The Mechanics

- The advance is a payment against future royalties, not a fee. It is never paid back if the book does not earn out.
- Each statement credits royalties for the period and carries the balance forward. A negative balance is "unearned".
- Rights income that the publisher controls (translation, audio, serial) is credited to the same account, less the publisher's share. That is how the Casa Velluto advance helped Lantern Hours over the line.
- A [[reserve-against-returns]] is held back from the first statements after a big shipment, so the first payable sum after earning out is smaller than the balance.

## Lantern Hours

Unearned 7,020 GBP at 2025-06-30, 4,610 GBP at 2025-12-31, earned out on the statement to 2026-06-30 with 1,140 GBP payable. The paperback did it in six weeks of sales. Full path in [[lantern-hours-earn-out-check]].

## Why It Matters

Earning out changes nothing about the advance already spent, but it changes the next book's conversation: an author who earns out is a smaller risk. [[callum-pryce]] will use it when the option on book three is exercised ([[third-book-option]]).
