# Lifeboat Crew Roll

The crew of the *Providence*, Saltmarrow's ten-oar pulling and sailing lifeboat, on the night of the November 1927 storm. Fixed on 2026-05-14, when the engine went and the crew became thirteen ([[lifeboat-question-answered-2026-05-14]]). All names are invented and checked against nothing real; if a name turns out to belong to a real lifeboat loss, it changes.

## Notes for the draft

- Five lost: Kit Harker, the two Vennings (father and son), Albert Coyle and Harry Treen.
- The Vennings are why the fund chapter in part three is about one widow who lost a husband and a son.
- Only Tobias, Daniel and Kit are principal characters ([[character-bible]]); the others are names and one detail each, no more.
- On the page, the crew are counted, not listed. The prologue names nobody.
