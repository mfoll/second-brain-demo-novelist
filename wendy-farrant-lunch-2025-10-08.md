# Wendy Farrant Lunch 2025-10-08

Lunch with [[wendy-farrant|Wendy Farrant]]. She had read chapters 1 to 20 of the first draft in September at my request.

## What she said

- The prose is the best I have written, and the town is alive in the details.
- Nell is "listening at keyholes": she reports a town she cannot see, so I move her into rooms she has no reason to be in. Wendy counted six such scenes in twenty chapters.
- A second novel is harder than the first because the first was written with no audience in mind. She thought I was writing Nell for Imogen's approval of Lantern Hours' narrator.
- Her advice: finish the draft first, then decide how many people the town needs to be seen through.

She followed up with a short letter two days later ([[wendy-farrant-letter-2025-10-10]]).

## What I decided

To finish the draft as planned and come back to the keyholes in revision. I wrote "keyholes: who else?" on the inside cover of the notebook. The answer came four months later, at the [[editorial-call-2026-02-19|editorial call]]: Tobias and Constance.
