# Modernist Women Writers

Exploration cluster: rereading the women modernists for what they do with interior time, with the inside of a mind moving through a day, and with letters and short forms. Not yet a project; it may become a teaching unit.

## What lives here

- [[mrs-dalloway]] and [[to-the-lighthouse]], reread in May and June 2025.
- [[the-garden-party-and-other-stories]], for Mansfield's way of entering and leaving a character in one sentence.
- [[literary-modernism]] and [[stream-of-consciousness]] as the background articles.
- [[pilgrimage-novel]], the long one I have still not finished.
- Concepts that came out of the reading: [[the-single-day-novel]], [[free-indirect-style]].

## Why it is here and not under the novel

These books taught me technique, not period facts, and the period they describe is not my characters' class or place. When I use them in class they go through [[teach-creative-writing]]; when a technique lands in the draft it is recorded in the craft notes. The cluster itself stays exploratory.

## Reading so far

- May 2025: [[literary-modernism]] as orientation, then [[mrs-dalloway]], read slowly over a fortnight with a pencil.
- June 2025: [[to-the-lighthouse]], mostly for "Time Passes", which taught me how to move years in a page without a narrator stepping forward.
- Autumn 2025: the Mansfield stories, a few at a time between drafting mornings.
- Stalled: [[pilgrimage-novel]], volume two.

## What might come of it

Two possible outcomes, neither yet a project. A teaching unit for the spring 2027 class on interior time and free indirect style, built on Woolf and Mansfield; or a short talk, since the Tidewrack panel showed me that audiences like hearing a translator talk about other people's sentences. If either gains a date, it becomes a project under [[teach-creative-writing]] or [[publicise-lantern-hours]] and this Topic moves with it.
