# Ifeoma Clarke

Curator at the [[merrin-maritime-collection]], the regional maritime museum and archive I use for the Saltmarrow research. She runs the galleries, the photograph collection and the museum's oral history programme; [[eluned-parry]] runs the archive and reading room.

## How we work together

- Met at the [[oral-history-workshop-2025-03-29|oral history workshop]] she ran at the Merrin in March 2025. Afterwards she put me in touch with [[dorothy-pengelly]], one of the museum's past interviewees, with Dorothy's agreement.
- Showed me the photograph collection on my [[merrin-first-visit-2025-06-10|first visit]]: curing-yard photographs from the 1920s, which fixed the layout of the yards in the [[saltmarrow-town-bible]].
- Generous with time, careful about rights. Photographs may be looked at and described; reproduction needs a licence, and I have not asked for one because I do not need images in the book.
- Prefers email, answers within a week; never phones.

## Current threads

- The Merrin would like a reading when The Salt Choir comes out. I said yes in principle, without a date, and noted it for the publicity plan in 2027.
- She asked whether Dorothy's interview could be deposited with the museum's oral history collection. That is Dorothy's decision; Dorothy said yes in August 2025 and the copy went via Ifeoma. See [[dorothy-pengelly-interview-recording]].

## Boundaries

A professional contact through the museum. No Merrin material is reproduced in the vault beyond my own notes and short catalogue references.
