# Word Counts Are For Contracts Not Days

## Stance

I do not set daily word targets. I count words weekly, to know whether the draft will meet the date in the contract, and I work daily by scenes: one scene drafted or one scene fixed is a day.

## Scope

Drafting and revising novels. Translation was different: there a daily page count was the honest measure, because the text already existed.

## Why I think this

In the first two months of draft 1 I set 1,000 words a day, hit it, and cut most of it. Scene days produce less and keep more. The weekly log ([[salt-choir-word-count-log]]) is enough to see the trend against the third-draft schedule.

## What would change my mind

Falling behind the 29 January date by more than two weeks with no structural reason. Then a daily floor for a month.

## Implications

Friday word count only. When asked "how many words a day", say none.
