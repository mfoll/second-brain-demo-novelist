# List Of Literary Festivals

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2026-10-01), read on 2026-03-03.

## Summary

A list article whose lead defines a literary festival: a regular, usually annual gathering of writers and readers, with readings, talks and other events over several days, aimed at promoting authors' books and encouraging reading. The body lists festivals by country.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after Orla Fenwick's invitation, mainly for the lead's definition. A festival exists to promote books and reading in general; a panel slot serves the festival's question first and my book second. That is why a panel built around "other people's voices" was better for a paperback debut than the solo event Orla offered ([[tidewrack-programme-meeting-2026-03-05]]).

## Caveats

Only the lead was useful. The list says nothing about how panels are chosen or what they sell.
