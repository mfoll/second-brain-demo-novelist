# Epistolary Voice Handout 2026-01-27

Two-page handout for session 3 of the spring 2026 term, written 25 January 2026.

## Contents

- Page one: what a letter has that narration does not (an addressee, a date, a reason to be written, a gap between event and outcome). Three bullet points on the epistolary tradition, from my reading of [[epistolary-novel]], and a one-paragraph description of [[lady-susan]] as a short example.
- Page two: my exercise, a letter a character would keep and one they would burn. Second edition (from 3 February) adds [[maureen-tighe-letter-exercise]] as the main exercise, with her name.
