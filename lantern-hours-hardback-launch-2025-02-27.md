# Lantern Hours Hardback Launch 2025-02-27

Publication-day launch of Lantern Hours at Quire & Candle, 19:00 to 20:30.

## What happened

- About 70 people in a shop that seats 50. [[rhiannon-teague|Rhiannon]] opened the back room.
- Imogen introduced the book; I read for eleven minutes (chapter 3, the mentor's desk) and answered questions for twenty.
- 61 copies sold on the night, 20 more signed for stock ([[quire-and-candle-event-sheet-2025-02]]).
- [[priyanka-desai|Priyanka]] counted four former translation clients in the room and two editors I had worked for. The questions were nearly all about translation.

## What I kept from it

- Read less than you think. The room was still listening at eleven minutes and would not have been at fifteen.
- Answer the translation question first; it is always coming.
- Signing takes as long as the reading. Plan the evening for it.
