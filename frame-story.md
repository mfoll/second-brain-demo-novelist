# Frame story

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-07-06 with [[prologue]], to be sure of the difference before the July review.

## Summary

Explains the frame story as an opening narrative that leads into a second, more detailed narrative or a set of tales, the inner story carrying most of the weight.

## Why It Matters

Lantern Hours used a frame: the translation in progress holding the mentor's story. The Salt Choir's prologue is not one, because nothing returns to it. Imogen's suggestion of a 1929 coda would make it a frame, which is the main reason I am wary of the coda ([[does-the-salt-choir-need-a-1929-coda]]).

## Caveats

Terminology only.
