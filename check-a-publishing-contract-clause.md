# Check A Publishing Contract Clause

How I read a contract, variation or licence when it arrives, before I agree to anything. I am not a lawyer and the agency negotiates; this is so I understand what I am signing and record what matters.

## Inputs

- The document from Callum or Bethan, and their covering note.
- The existing contract note it changes ([[salt-choir-contract]], [[lantern-hours-contract]]).

## Steps

1. **Read the covering note first.** Callum always says what changed. Then read the whole document, not only the changed clause.
2. **For each money term:** amount, trigger (signature, delivery, acceptance, publication), and the date that trigger realistically falls. Triggers on acceptance are later than they look.
3. **For each date:** what moves it, and what moves with it. The 2026 variation moved delivery and the delivery tranche together ([[delivery-date-extension]]).
4. **For each right:** who holds it, for how long, and what share I keep. Update [[rights-and-licences-register]].
5. **Options and exclusivity:** what triggers the window, how long, what must be submitted ([[option-clause]]).
6. **Questions** go to Callum in one email, numbered. Bethan answers the arithmetic ones.
7. **After signature:** update the contract note, the register and [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]] if any payment date moved. File the signed PDF; never store it in the vault.

## Verification

Bethan confirms the agency's payment schedule matches the note.
