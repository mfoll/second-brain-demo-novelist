# Student Writing Privacy Rules

What this vault may hold about my students, and what it may not. Written in September 2025 from the centre's policy ([[carrowmede-learner-data-policy]]) and tightened in September 2026 after the memoir question.

## May Record

- Names, and which terms they attended.
- What they are working on, in broad terms ("a crime novel", "a memoir of working life").
- What they contributed in class: a question, an exercise, a comment, a structural idea.
- A short phrase of theirs, only with their agreement, and with that agreement noted beside it.

## Must Not Record

- Any of their writing beyond that phrase.
- Grades or judgements of ability. The class is not graded and my opinions of their prose stay in the room.
- Personal circumstances, health, family, work beyond what they mention as context for their writing.
- Contact details. Contact goes through the centre's system.
- Anything from memoir content ([[how-do-i-give-feedback-on-memoir-about-real-people]]).

## Credit

When a student's idea feeds my work, I record it, tell them, and ask how they want to be credited. Maureen Tighe agreed to be thanked in the acknowledgements ([[maureen-tighe]]); Ronan Maddox prefers "a harbour pilot" ([[ronan-maddox]]).

## Applies To

Person notes for students, class session Events, term projects and handouts. [[run-a-class-session]] checks it after every session.
