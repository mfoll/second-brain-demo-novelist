# Joined The Wednesday Choir

First rehearsal with [[ingrid-solheim]]'s community choir on 2025-09-10. I joined to hear four-part hymn singing from inside the sound rather than from a recording, for Constance's choir in The Salt Choir. Ingrid put me in the altos and told me there was no such thing as a research singer.

## First evening

- About forty singers; tea at the break; subscription 6 GBP a week, paid termly.
- Ingrid taught a hymn tune in parts by ear: sopranos first, then altos, then the men, then all four together. The altos have the dullest line and hold everything up. That evening is where Constance's altos come from.
- I could not hold my line once the tenors came in. By week four I could.

## Why it matters

- Constance's rehearsal scenes, and the choir's progress from congregational unison to four-part harmony by 1927, are built on what I heard there ([[four-part-harmony]], [[choral-traditions]]).
- It is also the only part of the research that is not reading, and it has kept me in a room of people once a week through the long drafting months.
