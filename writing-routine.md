# Writing Routine

How the working week runs during the third draft of The Salt Choir, autumn 2026. Rewritten when the phase changes; the last version (first draft, 2025) is in Git.

## The week

| Day | Morning | Afternoon | Evening |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Draft 07:00-11:00 | Revision; admin after 16:00 | |
| Tuesday | Draft 07:00-10:00 | Class preparation | Class at Carrowmede 19:00-21:00 |
| Wednesday | Draft 07:00-11:00 | Research or reading | Choir |
| Thursday | Draft 07:00-11:00 | Revision | Writing group, first Thursday |
| Friday | Draft 07:00-11:00 | Money, email, publicity | |

Weekends are free unless a reading falls on one.

## Rules

- The [[first-hour-rule]]: the first hour goes to the draft, whatever else happens.
- Target 1,000 new or rewritten words on a drafting morning, counted in the [[daily-word-count-log]]. The average since 2026-07-13 is about 760; on residency it was about 1,900.
- No research in the morning. Questions that come up while drafting go in the margin as "[CHECK]" and into the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]] at the end of the week.
- Email after 11:00 only, except on reading days.
- Notebook in the bag always; transcription monthly ([[transcribe-a-notebook-into-the-vault]]).

## What breaks it

Readings and travel (the [[bookshop-reading-tour]] took nine mornings between May and October), the class's marking weeks, and anything to do with contracts. I plan for the breaks rather than pretend they will not happen.
