# Should Tobias survive the storm?

## Question

When the lifeboat capsizes on the bar in November 1927 and five of the crew are lost, should the coxswain be one of them?

## Current Answer

No. Tobias survives. Decided on 2025-05-16 while building the [[character-bible]], and held through every draft.

## Why

- A dead coxswain makes the storm the end of his story; a surviving one carries it into the inquiry and the town's judgement, which is part three.
- Survivor's guilt layered on shell shock is harder to write and more honest than a heroic death.
- It keeps the book from being about one man's sacrifice. The five dead belong to the town.

## Caveats

His survival is one of the things the prologue must not reveal ([[storm-prologue]]).
