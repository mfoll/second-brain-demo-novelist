# Casa Velluto Rights Agreement 2026-03

Licence of Italian-language rights in Lantern Hours from Larkspur & Venn to Casa Velluto, signed 2026-03-09. Sigrun sent me a summary and a copy for my file on 2026-03-10.

## Key Details

- Advance 1,500 GBP, payable half on signature and half on publication. Larkspur & Venn keep 20%; my 80% (1,200 GBP) goes to the royalty account.
- Royalties to the licensor: 7% rising to 9% after 5,000 copies, on the Italian cover price.
- Term: seven years from first Italian publication. Publication within 24 months of signature.
- Translator to be credited on the cover. Sigrun asked for this; Casa Velluto do it as standard anyway.

## Why It Matters

The first translation sale, and 1,200 GBP that helped close the earn-out gap ([[lantern-hours-royalty-statement-2026-10]]). The cover credit for the translator was a condition I would have argued for myself.

## Caveats

The whole advance is credited at signature in the publisher's accounts even though half is paid later. That is Larkspur & Venn's practice, not mine to argue.
