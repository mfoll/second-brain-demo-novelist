# Read And Explore

The designated home for reading that does not yet belong to a project. A translator's habit I have kept: read widely, write down what a book does with its sentences, and only later find out what it was for. Some of it ends up nowhere, which is fine.

## Scope

- Exploration clusters, each a Topic under this Responsibility: [[modernist-women-writers]], [[oral-history-methods]] and [[choral-traditions]].
- The running list of what I read and why: [[reading-log-2025-2026]].
- When a cluster gains an outcome or a deadline, it becomes a project somewhere else and the Topic moves with it. The oral history reading, for example, fed the [[dorothy-pengelly-interview-2025-04-23|interview with Dorothy Pengelly]] but stays here because the method interests me beyond this book.
- Stays elsewhere: research that answers a question for The Salt Choir belongs to [[saltmarrow-research]], even when it started here.
