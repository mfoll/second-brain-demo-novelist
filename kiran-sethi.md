# Kiran Sethi

Poetry tutor at Carrowmede. Teaches the Tuesday poetry group in the room across the corridor, same hours as my class, and has done for about eight years.

## How We Work Together

We share a kettle, a photocopier code and the 20:00 break. Kiran is the only other tutor I see regularly, and the conversations at the break have become a small staffroom of two: what to do with the student who talks over everyone, whether to set homework, how to end a term.

Practical arrangements:

- Students move between the groups. [[agnieszka-wrona]] came to me from Kiran's group in spring 2026 to try prose; two autumn 2026 applicants without a draft went the other way.
- Kiran's group and mine held a joint reading in March 2026, which worked for the poets and less well for the prose writers, who needed more time. Not repeated.
- When the photocopier fails, Kiran prints for both of us at home. I owe several.

## What I Have Learned From Kiran

Kiran sets one exercise a week and never reads it back in the session it is written. Writing in the room, reading the next week. I adopted it for autumn 2026 and it cut the read-back time by half ([[run-a-class-session]]).

Kiran also told me early on that adult students forgive a dull exercise but not a tutor who talks about their own book. I have kept to that, with the exception of writing alongside the class ([[teaching-feeds-the-writing]]).
