# Reading Group Feedback 2026

Comments from nine library reading groups that used the guide between May and September 2026, collected and anonymised by [[rosa-mbatha|Rosa Mbatha]], received 2026-09-18.

## Summary

- The most discussed question: whether Ines should have finished the mentor's translation at all. Seven of nine groups split on it.
- The translation note was read by every group and quoted back in four sets of comments.
- Least useful: the question about the town, which two groups skipped.
- Recurring request: a list of other translator novels. Rosa compiled one for her groups.
- Two groups asked when the next book is out. Rosa told them she did not know, which is accurate.

## Why It Matters

Confirms the guide's design. Also a reminder that readers want to argue with a character, which is something the Constance letters may give them ([[constance-leal]]).
