# Dorothy Pengelly Consent Form

The signed consent form for the oral history interview of 2025-04-23 with [[dorothy-pengelly]], adapted from the Merrin Maritime Collection's template after the [[oral-history-workshop-2025-03-29|workshop]].

## Summary of the terms

- The interview is recorded for background research for a novel.
- No direct quotation of her words in the novel or in publicity without asking her again.
- Her name appears only in the acknowledgements, and only if she agrees when the book is accepted.
- She receives a copy of the recording.
- She may choose to deposit the recording with the Merrin's oral history collection (she chose to, in August 2025).
- She may withdraw consent for future use at any time by letter.
- Anything said with the recorder off is not used.

## Why It Matters

These terms bound everything I take from the interview; see [[how-much-of-dorothys-story-can-the-novel-use]].
