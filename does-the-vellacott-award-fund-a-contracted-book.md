# Does The Vellacott Award Fund A Contracted Book?

Can a Vellacott Development Award support writing time on a novel that is already under contract with an advance?

## Why It Mattered

The Salt Choir has a 24,000 GBP advance. If contracted work were excluded, the [[vellacott-grant-application]] was pointless and the only answer to the cash gap was the Ashlar commission. The guidelines speak of "new work" and are silent on contracts.

## Current Answer

Yes. Confirmed by [[ana-lucia-ferreira]] on the [[vellacott-eligibility-call-2026-09-08|call of 8 September 2026]]: contracted books are eligible if the full advance and its payment schedule are declared and the budget shows what the award pays for that the advance does not. A moved tranche that leaves a gap in the writing period is a recognised case.

## How It Was Applied

The [[vellacott-budget-2026]] declares all three tranches with dates (signature 2025, delivery and acceptance now May 2027, publication) and asks for 6,000 GBP as six months of writing time, November 2026 to April 2027, the months the advance no longer covers.

## Caveat

Eligible is not funded. The panel may still prefer writers without advances. Decision 2026-12-11.
