# Creative Writing

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-10-08, read on 2026-10-10 while starting the spring 2027 outline.

## Summary

Defines creative writing as writing outside the usual professional, journalistic, academic and technical forms, marked by attention to craft: structure, character, tropes, genre and poetics. It covers fiction and non-fiction, from novels and stories to biography, poetry and some journalism; screen and stage writing are often taught separately.

## Why It Matters

Useful for the brochure, not for the room. Gwen asked for a sentence on what the class is for, and "craft and technique" in a mixed group of memoir, crime, fantasy and literary fiction is the honest answer. The breadth of the definition is also my case for keeping memoir in the class rather than splitting it off ([[how-do-i-give-feedback-on-memoir-about-real-people]]).

## Caveats

A definition article. Says nothing about how to teach any of it.
