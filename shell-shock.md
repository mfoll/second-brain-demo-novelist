# Shell shock

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-03-20, before sending the Tobias chapters of draft 2 to [[femi-adewale]].

## Summary

Explains shell shock as a First World War term for symptoms now associated with combat stress reaction and post-traumatic stress disorder, used colloquially before PTSD was recognised, and lists typical symptoms such as dread, panic, and inability to reason, sleep, walk or talk.

## Why It Matters

Background for [[tobias-arden]]. The article's own framing, which maps the term onto later diagnoses, is exactly the vocabulary Femi warned me not to let into Tobias's head ([[femi-adewale-notes-on-tobias-2026-03-24]], [[period-diction]]). In Saltmarrow people say "nerves"; nobody in the novel uses the article's title, Tobias least of all.
