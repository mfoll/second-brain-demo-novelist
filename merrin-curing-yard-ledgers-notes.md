# Merrin Curing-Yard Ledger Notes

My notes on one curing firm's wage book and barrel book for the 1926 season, read at the Merrin Maritime Collection on 2025-06-10.

## Summary

- Crews of three paid together by the barrel, with engagement money at the start of the season and lodging deductions.
- Season totals per crew varied by about a third between the best and worst crews.
- The barrel book shows the coopers' weekly output and the seasonal peak.

## Why It Matters

The pay structure of Ma Garrick's crew in the [[character-bible]] and the scale of Rendell's yard in the [[saltmarrow-town-bible]]. Figures went into the [[interwar-prices-and-wages-table]] as ranges, not copied numbers.

## Caveats

One firm, one season. The names in the ledger are real people's and are not in my notes.
