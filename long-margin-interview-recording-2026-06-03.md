# Long Margin Interview Recording 2026-06-03

Recorded the interview with [[jasper-meade|Jasper Meade]] for The Long Margin, 14:00 to 15:30, in a hired room above a café; he recorded on his phone and took notes.

## How it went

- The first hour followed his questions ([[long-margin-interview-questions-2026-05]]): translation, the twenty-two years, Lantern Hours as a translator's novel, coming to fiction at 54. Good questions, and I gave careful answers.
- The last thirty minutes drifted to book two. I described the lifeboat, the choir and the 1920s, and gave the working title after asking whether we were still recording. I heard "we're nearly done" and took it as no.
- I did not ask to check quotes. The magazine offers it on request.

## In hindsight

Everything that went wrong in the [[long-margin-interview-2026-07-15|published version]] came from the last half hour. Recorded interviews over 60 minutes now get a hard stop at 60, and I check quotes ([[email-interviews-over-recorded-ones]], [[on-the-record]]).
