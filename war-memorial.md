# War memorial

## Summary

The article covers any building, monument or statue raised to mark a war or a victory, or to remember the people killed or wounded in it.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after the [[remembrance-concert-2025-11-09]]. Saltmarrow's memorial, unveiled in 1922 on the green by St Anne's with 61 names ([[saltmarrow-town-bible]]), is where the town already knows how to grieve in public when the lifeboat is lost in 1927. The question for the memorial chapter is how a town that has done this once does it again for five men lost at sea.

## Key Details

- Memorials commemorate the dead and the injured, not only victory.
- The form varies widely: buildings, monuments, statues and other structures.

## Caveats

General and international; nothing in it concerns Saltmarrow, which is invented.
