# Imogen Editorial Letter 2026-07-03

[[imogen-lacey|Imogen]]'s letter on draft 2 (the braid), six pages, received Friday 3 July 2026, five days before the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|review call]].

## Summary

The town is now there and the three viewpoints are right. The braid is not: the storm told first is spent by the time it returns, and readers lose Tobias's war. Constance's letters are the best thing in the book.

## Key details

- Two colleagues at Larkspur & Venn read the draft; both gave up trying to place Tobias's memories in time.
- She counts five storm-night returns before part three and says a reader "can list the dead by chapter 16".
- She does not propose chronology outright; she asks whether the storm "needs to be first, or only needs to be felt early".
- She attaches [[tariq-mansour]]'s continuity notes ([[tariq-mansour-continuity-notes-2026-06-30]]).
- Line notes are in the marked-up PDF ([[imogen-draft-two-margin-notes]]).
