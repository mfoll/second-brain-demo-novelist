# When Does The Option Clause Start And End?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> It starts on acceptance of The Salt Choir, not on delivery or publication, and runs for 60 days of exclusive consideration on an outline and a sample chapter. With delivery on 2027-03-31, acceptance and the window could open in May 2027.

## Evidence

- Clause read in the signed copy on 2026-09-15 after Callum's call ([[third-book-option-call-2026-09-15]]); summary in [[salt-choir-contract]].
- Bethan Okafor confirmed the 2026-08-28 variation did not change it.

## Caveats

- Acceptance could come later than 60 days after delivery if Imogen sends an editorial report instead. The window moves with it.
- Whether the 60 days run from the publisher receiving the submission or from acceptance is ambiguous in the wording; Callum reads it as from submission, if submission comes after acceptance. Good enough.
