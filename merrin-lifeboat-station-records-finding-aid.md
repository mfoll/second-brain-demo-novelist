# Merrin Lifeboat Station Records Finding Aid

The Merrin Maritime Collection's printed finding aid for its lifeboat station records, sent by [[eluned-parry]] as a PDF on 2026-04-28 when I booked the May visit.

## Summary

Lists the records of eleven stations by series: service record books (launch by launch, with crew present and outcome), crew lists, quarterly returns, correspondence with the district inspectors, and committee minutes where they survive. Dates run roughly 1890 to 1960, with gaps. Each item has a reference and a note on condition; fragile items are produced as copies.

## Why It Matters

I chose the ten items for the [[merrin-archive-visit-2026-05-12]] from it: service books and inspector correspondence for three small stations, 1924 to 1931. Those items settled [[would-saltmarrows-lifeboat-have-had-an-engine-in-1927]].

## Caveats

Committee minutes survive for only four stations, none of them for 1928, which may answer my open request about the promised motor boat before Eluned does.
