# Femi Adewale Reading Call 2026-03-26

Call with [[femi-adewale|Femi Adewale]], medical historian, about the Tobias chapters of draft 2. He had sent written notes two days before ([[femi-adewale-notes-on-tobias-2026-03-24]]). Forty minutes.

## What he said

- The symptoms are plausible, but continuous. A man who ran a lifeboat crew for years would have had long good stretches, with episodes brought on by particular sounds, smells, anniversaries, exhaustion.
- Tobias's inner voice uses a later vocabulary of trauma. People around him would have said "nerves", and the committee might say "not steady". He suggested what he would have heard from a local doctor in 1924, which was very little.
- Fragmented memory is a modern literary convention. Accounts from the period describe whole, vivid, repeated scenes.
- He sent a short list of background reading ([[femi-adewale-reading-list-2026-03]]).

## What I changed

- Tobias's bad periods are episodes now, not a constant state ([[tobias-arden]]).
- Vocabulary audit of his chapters, logged under [[period-diction]].
- His point about whole scenes was the seed of [[how-much-of-tobiass-war-goes-on-the-page]], decided in July: two complete war scenes.

## Boundaries agreed

No fee, an acknowledgement with his agreement, not to be named as a consultant. He reads the third-draft Tobias chapters before delivery.
