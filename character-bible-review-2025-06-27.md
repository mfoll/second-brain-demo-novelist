# Character Bible Review, 27 June 2025

A full day reading first-draft chapters 1 to 18 against the new [[character-bible]], which closed the project. The point was to find every contradiction the bible was meant to stop.

## Found

- Nell's age wrong in four places (she is nineteen in March 1924, born 1905).
- Walter Pascoe's son killed in two different years.
- Hugh Rendell both chairman of the lifeboat committee and "never on a committee in his life".
- Kit Harker gassed in one chapter and wounded by shrapnel in another; gassed won.
- Constance's cottage on Fore Street in chapter 3 and on the Brae in chapter 11; the Brae won, see [[saltmarrow-town-bible]].

## Decided

- The bible is the reference; the draft is corrected to it, never the other way round without an entry in the bible's change list.
- Fourteen principal characters and no more without a reason written down.
- The bible lives in the Scrivener research folder ([[character-bible-document]]), with the table summarised in the project note.

Corrections went into the [[salt-choir-first-draft]] over the following week.
