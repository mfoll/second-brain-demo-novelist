# Lifeboat Question Answered

On 2026-05-14, two days after the [[merrin-archive-visit-2026-05-12]], I wrote up my archive notes and settled the lifeboat: no engine. Recorded as that day's entry in the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]] and as the answer to [[would-saltmarrows-lifeboat-have-had-an-engine-in-1927]].

## Decided

- The *Providence* is a ten-oar pulling and sailing lifeboat, crew of thirteen, launched by hand and horse team down the beach slipway.
- The district committee promises a motor boat for 1928; it arrives in September 1928 and becomes a part three thread.
- Five of the thirteen are lost in the storm; the names are fixed in [[lifeboat-crew-roll]].
