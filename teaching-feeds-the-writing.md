# Teaching Feeds The Writing

## Stance

One evening a week of teaching makes my writing better, not just my income. Two evenings would not.

## Scope

The Tuesday class as it is: ten sessions a term, students who are writing their own books, and me writing alongside them occasionally. Not festival workshops, which are performances.

## Why I Think This

- Constance's letters came from writing alongside the class on [[class-session-2026-01-27|27 January 2026]], using [[maureen-tighe]]'s exercise.
- [[ronan-maddox]] caught the motor lifeboat before the archive did ([[spring-class-final-reading-2026-03-31]]).
- Explaining [[free-indirect-style]] to adults who have never heard the term forced me to say what I do with Nell and Tobias.
- The [[reverse-outline]] I taught in October found a repetition in my own part one.

## What Would Change My Mind

- A term where preparation runs over three hours a week. Then it is eating the writing.
- A second class: I expect the returns to stop at one evening, but have not tested it ([[should-i-teach-a-second-weekly-class]]).

## Implications

- Write alongside the class in about one session in three, never on my current book's pages as a workshop text.
- Keep my own book out of the room except for short examples (Kiran's rule, see [[kiran-sethi]]).
