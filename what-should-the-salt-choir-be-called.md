# What should The Salt Choir be called?

## Question

Is "The Salt Choir" the right published title, or only a good working title?

## Current Answer

Open. I still prefer The Salt Choir. Larkspur & Venn's sales director, [[martin-okello]], thinks "Choir" signals a quiet, devotional book and may cost the book readers who would like it ([[title-call-2026-10-01]]). Imogen is neutral; Callum likes the title.

## What would settle it

- A shortlist of three by 2026-12-18 ([[salt-choir-title-longlist]]).
- Blind ranking by the writing group and Teodora in January.
- Imogen and sales after acceptance, and in any case by the catalogue copy deadline of 2027-04-30 ([[larkspur-catalogue-schedule-2027]]).

## Caveats

The working title appeared in print in the [[long-margin-interview-2026-07-15|Long Margin interview]] before the publisher announced it. Changing it now costs nothing commercially, but it is the name I have written under for two years; I should discount my attachment.
