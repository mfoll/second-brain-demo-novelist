# Spring 2026 Course Outline

"Voice and Point of View", the outline for the spring 2026 term, sent to [[gwen-attah]] on 17 November 2025.

## Summary

Ten sessions: first person; close third and [[free-indirect-style]]; epistolary voice; second person; the unreliable narrator; dialect and period voice; narrative distance; one scene in three viewpoints; revision for voice; reading. The term's main exercise is the same short scene written from three points of view.

## Why It Matters

Session 3 on epistolary voice is the one that changed The Salt Choir ([[class-session-2026-01-27]]). The outline planned it as a minor session about letters as documents; its plan was replaced in the room by [[maureen-tighe]]'s exercise.

Second person should go next time ([[spring-2026-class-feedback-summary]]).
