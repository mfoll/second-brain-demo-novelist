# Interwar Coastal Life

Everything about how people lived and worked in a small fishing and chapel town between the wars, as background for The Salt Choir. Saltmarrow is invented; this Topic gathers the real background and my notes on what I took from it.

## What lives here

- **Work**: the herring season and the curing yards ([[herring]], [[kipper]], [[cooper-profession]]), gutting crews and their rhythm from [[dorothy-pengelly-interview-2025-04-23|Dorothy Pengelly's interview]].
- **News and wires**: the telegraph office where Nell works ([[electrical-telegraph]], [[telegram]]) and the first wireless sets ([[history-of-radio]], [[should-saltmarrow-have-a-wireless-set-by-1927]]).
- **After the war**: [[war-memorial]]s, [[shell-shock]], the influenza years ([[spanish-flu]]) and the [[interwar-period]] as a whole.
- **Chapel**: [[hymn]] singing and the choir, more in [[choral-traditions]].
- **Money and the end of the decade**: [[how-would-saltmarrow-hear-of-the-wall-street-crash]].

## How I use it

Background only. The rule I hold to is in [[period-texture-vs-period-trivia]]: a fact earns its place if a character would notice it, not because I found it. Period mistakes go to the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]].

## Where the research stands

The town itself is stable: the [[saltmarrow-town-bible]] has not changed shape since May 2026, and the [[saltmarrow-chronology]] carries the town's years against the background. What remains is the end of the decade. Part three of the third draft (1928 and 1929) needs the slow decline: the herring trade falling off, the telegraph office cut back, families leaving. I have read less about the late 1920s than about the early years, and it shows in the seven open entries of the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]], five of which are in part three.
