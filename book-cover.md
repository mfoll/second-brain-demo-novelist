# Book Cover

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2026-08-18), read on 2026-01-29.

## Summary

Mostly about the physical cover: boards and cloth for hardbacks, card wrappers for paperbacks, dust jackets, and older or hand-bound forms. It notes that "bookcover" is also used for the cover image in library systems.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after seeing [[felipe-araujo|Felipe Araujo]]'s two paperback routes ([[paperback-cover-brief-2026]]). Less useful than I hoped for the design choice; useful for one point. The hardback's jacket and the paperback's printed card are different objects, and a design made for a jacket does not necessarily survive on a glossy card at thumbnail size. That was part of why [[paperback-cover-option-a]] lost.

## Caveats

Little on cover design or marketing; that came from Felipe and the sales team.
