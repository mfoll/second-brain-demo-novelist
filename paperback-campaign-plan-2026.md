# Paperback Campaign Plan 2026

[[priyanka-desai|Priyanka Desai]]'s plan for the Lantern Hours paperback, sent after our call of 2026-01-12 and saved on 2026-01-14.

## Summary

- Premise: paperbacks of literary debuts live on reading groups, bookshop tables and word of mouth, not reviews. Spend effort there.
- Components: a reading group guide in the back of the book; one festival; one long magazine interview; six bookshop or library readings between May and November.
- Publication 2026-05-14 at 9.99 GBP with a new cover.
- Out of scope: radio, online events, anything abroad.
- Author time budget: one evening a month plus the festival weekend.

## Why It Matters

The plan held. Every component happened ([[tidewrack-festival-talk]], [[long-margin-interview]], [[bookshop-reading-tour]], [[reading-group-guide]]), and the time budget was roughly kept until the third draft started in July, when I asked her to book nothing new.

## Caveats

It assumed the interview would be low risk. See [[long-margin-interview-2026-07-15]].
