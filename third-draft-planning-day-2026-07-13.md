# Third Draft Planning Day 2026-07-13

A full Monday at the desk to plan the third draft of The Salt Choir, starting from [[imogen-lacey|Imogen]]'s written summary of the July review ([[imogen-third-draft-brief-2026-07-09]]). The output is [[third-draft-plan-2026-07-13]] and the first version of the [[third-draft-chapter-map]].

## What I did

- Took the draft 2 Scrivener project apart into a new project, every scene tagged by year and viewpoint, then sorted by date. The braid unravelled into a chronology in about three hours.
- Counted what survives: about 61,000 words of draft 2 usable with revision, mostly Nell's chapters and the letters.
- Planned three parts and 33 chapters, with the prologue cut from draft 2's opening.
- Picked the two war scenes for Tobias and moved the rest of the war material to the Cuts folder ([[salt-choir-cut-list]]).
- Set a schedule to finish by the end of January 2027, assuming the delivery date would move. Callum called the next day to start that ([[delivery-date-extension]]).

Part one is to be finished at Ferrow House in August ([[writing-retreat-2026]]). It was, on the evening of 2026-08-20.
