# Where should The Salt Choir begin?

## Question

Where does the book open: in 1924 with Nell leaving the curing yards, or on the storm night of November 1927?

## Current Answer

In 1924, with a four-page storm prologue before chapter 1. Answered at the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|draft two review]] and reflected in the [[third-draft-chapter-map]].

## History

- Draft 1 began in March 1924; the storm came at chapter 31 of 41. Too late ([[imogen-editorial-letter-2026-01-30]]).
- Draft 2 began [[in-medias-res|in the middle of things]], on the storm night ([[editorial-call-2026-02-19]]). It spent the storm early.
- Draft 3 combines them: 1924, with the storm held up front as a question.

## Caveats

The prologue half of the answer is still disputed: [[should-the-salt-choir-keep-the-storm-prologue]]. If it goes, the answer becomes simply 1924, with the sea brought into chapter 2.
