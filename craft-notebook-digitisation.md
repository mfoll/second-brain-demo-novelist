# Craft Notebook Digitisation

Eleven paper notebooks from 2019 to 2025, the last three years of translating and the first draft of Lantern Hours, read through and transcribed into the vault as craft notes linked to topics. The paper stays the record; the vault holds what I would otherwise never find again.

## Outcome

Eleven paper notebooks (2019-2025) transcribed into craft notes and linked to topics. Reached on 2025-08-29.

## Current State

- Completed 2025-08-29 ([[notebook-digitisation-finished-2025-08-29]]).
- Index of all eleven notebooks, with date ranges and what each is mostly about: [[craft-notebook-index]]. The paper originals are on the study shelf: [[craft-notebooks-2019-2025]].
- About 1,900 handwritten pages read; roughly 260 entries marked worth keeping; those became 23 vault notes (Concepts, Opinions and a few Sources) rather than 260 fragments.
- The method became [[transcribe-a-notebook-into-the-vault]], now used monthly for notebook twelve.

## Scope and Boundaries

Craft only: technique, translation problems, reading notes, things overheard that taught me something about speech. Personal entries stayed on paper and are not summarised here. Lantern Hours drafting notes were transcribed only where they generalise.

## Working Context

I tried photographing pages and running them through handwriting recognition in April 2025. It read my hand at about 70% and turned French and Portuguese quotations into nonsense, so I stopped after notebook one and typed instead. Typing was slower, about 90 pages an evening, and better, because I rewrote while I typed and dropped what had not aged.

What came out of it that I still use:

- [[research-iceberg]] and [[period-texture-vs-period-trivia]], both from notebook nine (2023), originally about translating a 1950s Lisbon novel.
- [[anachronism-of-attitude]], from notebook six.
- [[notebooks-beat-apps-for-first-capture]], which is the lesson of the project itself.
