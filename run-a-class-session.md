# Run A Class Session

How I prepare, run and follow up one Tuesday session at Carrowmede, 19:00 to 21:00. Built from the first term ([[first-class-session-2025-09-16]]) and revised each term since.

## Inputs

- The term outline (for example [[autumn-2026-course-outline]]).
- Last week's exercise, which students bring back to read.
- Handout for this week, two pages at most.

## Before (Sunday or Monday, about two hours)

1. Reread the outline line for the session and last week's notes.
2. Write or revise the handout. Two pages: one idea, two or three short examples (public domain or my own invented lines), one exercise. Print 16 copies; upload a PDF to the centre's system for absentees.
3. Do the exercise myself once, so I know how long it takes.
4. Choose the three read-backs from last week, rotating through the register.

## During

1. 19:00 to 19:20: read-backs, three students, five minutes each, one comment each from the room.
2. 19:20 to 19:45: the idea, with the handout. Talk for no more than fifteen minutes.
3. 19:45 to 20:00: start the exercise in the room.
4. 20:00 to 20:15: break.
5. 20:15 to 20:50: exercise continues, then discussion of what it showed. No reading of work written tonight (Kiran's rule); it is read next week.
6. 20:50 to 21:00: next week's subject; anything for the centre.

## After (Tuesday night or Wednesday, thirty minutes)

1. Two lines in the term's project note on what worked and what did not.
2. Craft points worth keeping go to the relevant concept note, not here.
3. Nothing about students beyond what [[student-writing-privacy-rules]] allows. No student writing in the vault.
4. Timesheet line for the session.

## Verification

At the end of term: ten timesheet lines, ten handouts in the term folder, feedback forms summarised (as in [[spring-2026-class-feedback-summary]]).
