# Story Timeline 1924-1929

A spreadsheet of every dated event in the novel, one row per event, with columns for the chapter, viewpoint, characters present and ages that year. Rebuilt on 2026-07-13 for the third draft from draft 2 and Tariq's continuity notes.

## Why it matters

The chronology of the third draft depends on it. Ages are computed from the [[character-bible]] birth years, so the errors Tariq found in draft 2 cannot recur.
