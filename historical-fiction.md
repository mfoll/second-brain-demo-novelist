# Historical fiction

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2025-02-10, the second week of the first draft, when I was writing the "what kind of book is this" page for my own notebook.

## Summary

Defines historical fiction as a genre in which an invented plot is set among particular real historical events, and notes that the label applies across media, from novels to theatre, film and games.

## Why It Matters

The Salt Choir is historical fiction with an invented town, which sits slightly to one side of the article's definition: the real events of the 1920s (the war's aftermath, the herring trade's decline, the coming of the wireless and the motor lifeboat) are background, and nothing real happens in Saltmarrow. I keep that boundary deliberately ([[saltmarrow-town-bible]]). The genre's familiar trap, characters who think like us in costume, is what [[period-diction]] is for.

## Caveats

A genre overview; no guidance on invented settings, which is the choice I care about.
