# Foreshadowing

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-09-18, the day after [[callum-prologue-call-2026-09-17|Callum's call]] about the prologue.

## Summary

Describes foreshadowing as planting hints or warnings of events to come, often early in a story, to develop or to subvert the audience's expectations.

## Why It Matters

Callum's case is that the prologue is not foreshadowing but disclosure. The article's "develop or subvert" is useful: if the prologue stops at the harbour mouth, the third draft can still subvert expectation about who is lost and that Tobias survives. Notes on keeping the planted hints honest are in [[dramatic-irony]].

## Caveats

Definition only.
