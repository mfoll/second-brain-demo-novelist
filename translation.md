# Translation

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-10-16, read on 2026-10-18 while thinking about the Ashlar offer.

## Summary

Defines translation as conveying the meaning of a source-language text through an equivalent text in a target language. Notes that English separates translating, for written texts, from interpreting, for spoken or signed communication.

## Why It Matters

I went looking for a neutral account of the trade I spent twenty-two years in, to set against my own feelings about going back to it ([[does-translating-again-damage-my-own-voice]]). The word that stays with me is "equivalent": a translator's job is to make an equivalent, and a novelist's is to make something that has no original. That is the whole of my worry about translating while drafting, in one word. Notes gathered under [[translation-and-craft]].

## Caveats

Very general. It is not about literary translation in particular, its economics or its practice, which I know better from the inside and from [[translators-rates-survey-2025]].
