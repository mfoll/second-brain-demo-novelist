Priyanka's plan ([[paperback-campaign-plan-2026]]) assumed the paperback would live on reading groups and bookshop tables rather than reviews, which turned out right: the review coverage was two short notices, while the guide and the readings moved copies. Joan Whitlock's proof pass ([[paperback-proof-corrections-2026]]) fixed 23 hardback errors, two of them in the Portuguese.
