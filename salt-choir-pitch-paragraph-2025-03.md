# Salt Choir Pitch Paragraph 2025-03

The one paragraph Callum used to sell The Salt Choir to Larkspur & Venn in early March 2025, before the [[salt-choir-contract|contract of 18 March]].

## Summary

About 120 words: a herring town in the 1920s, a girl at the telegraph office who hears everything, a lifeboat that will not come home, and a choir that sings for it. "From the translator-turned-novelist of Lantern Hours."

## Why it matters

A record of what the book was promised to be. It survived three drafts better than the synopsis did: the town, the wire, the boat and the choir are all still there.
