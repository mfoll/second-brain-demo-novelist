# Reading Group Guide Final 2026

The signed-off text of the Lantern Hours reading group guide, four pages in the back of the paperback, final version of 2026-04-24.

## Key Details

- Ten discussion questions. Four about Ines and her choices, two about the mentor, two about the dictionary and the work of translation, one about the town, one asking readers which sentence they would translate first.
- A 600-word note, "On translating someone you loved", in my own voice.
- Further reading: three novels in translation and one book about translation, all chosen so a library could supply them.
- Cut from the draft: a question on the ending (spoiler for readers who start with the guide), a question on the title, and a long author biography.

## Why It Matters

The guide is part of why the paperback sold to groups ([[lantern-hours-paperback-sales-report-2026-09]]). If The Salt Choir gets one, the template is here: judge the characters, not the author.
