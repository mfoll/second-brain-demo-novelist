# Halloran Pryce Remittance Advices 2025

The twelve remittance advices the Halloran Pryce Agency sent me in 2025, one per payment passed on, collected on 8 January 2026 to build the commission line of the [[accounts-2025|2025 accounts]].

## Key Details

- Each advice shows the payer, the gross received into the agency's client account, the commission deducted and the net paid on, under the [[halloran-pryce-agency-agreement]].
- 2025 gross through the agency: 12,000 GBP (the Lantern Hours publication tranche of 4,000 and the Salt Choir signature tranche of 8,000). Commission at 15%: 1,800 GBP.
- The other ten advices are nil or small: festival fees the agency happened to collect, and two statements with nothing payable.
- Lag between the publisher's payment and mine: six to nine working days.

## Why It Matters

Commission is the largest expense line every year a tranche lands, and these are its evidence. [[neil-gathercole]] asked for a single annual statement instead; [[bethan-okafor]] has agreed to send one for 2026 in January 2027.

One point confirmed with Bethan in October 2026: on a royalty statement where translation income sold by the publisher is netted into the payable balance (as with Casa Velluto on [[lantern-hours-royalty-statement-2026-10]]), the agency charges its home rate of 15% on the balance paid, because the agency did not sell those rights. Hence 969 GBP net of the 1,140 GBP due.
