# Narration

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-01-08 while planning the spring 2026 class on voice and point of view.

## Summary

Covers narration as the commentary through which a story reaches its audience, delivered by a narrator who may be a character or an unspecified voice, and required in written fiction in a way it is not in film or theatre. The article goes on to narrative point of view and narrative voice.

## Why It Matters

The overview I point students to before the session on point of view. For The Salt Choir it was a check on vocabulary when I started thinking about three viewpoints in January 2026: close third for Nell and Tobias, first person in letters for Constance ([[point-of-view-and-voice]]).

## Caveats

A broad article; the parts on film and games are irrelevant to me.
