# Lantern Hours Royalty Statement To 2025-12-31

Larkspur & Venn's statement for 1 July to 31 December 2025, received through the agency on 2 April 2026. Hardback only; the paperback was still to come.

## Key Details

| Line | Basis | Units | Royalty (GBP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hardback, home | 10% of RRP 16.99 | 980 | 1,665.02 |
| Hardback, high discount (book club) | 10% of net receipts (3,060.00) | 600 | 306.00 |
| Ebook | 25% of net receipts (1,755.92) | 640 | 438.98 |
| **Earned in period** | | | **2,410.00** |
| Balance brought forward | | | -7,020.00 |
| **Balance carried forward** | | | **-4,610.00** |

Nothing payable.

## Why It Matters

I expected the unearned balance nearer 3,900 GBP. The difference was the book club sale: 600 copies at a discount above 52%, which under clause 9 of the Lantern Hours contract pays 10% of net receipts instead of 10% of RRP. At full rate it would have earned about 713 GBP more. Correct, and a lesson: read the high-discount clause before celebrating a bulk sale. This statement is why [[reconcile-a-royalty-statement]] exists.
