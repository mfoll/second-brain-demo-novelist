# Catalogue Announcement

The moment a publisher first makes a book public: its entry in the seasonal catalogue sent to booksellers, reps and rights buyers, with title, description, format, price and date. Everything said publicly before it is either the publisher's choice or a leak.

## Why it matters to me

- Larkspur & Venn announce titles in their catalogue, about nine to twelve months before publication. For The Salt Choir that means 2027 at the earliest.
- The catalogue copy is written by the publisher with the author's approval, and it becomes the book's public description for the sales cycle. Getting there with the wrong description already in circulation is the risk [[long-margin-interview-2026-07-15|the Long Margin misquote]] created.
- The title has to be settled before the copy deadline ([[final-title-decision]]).

## Seen in practice

The paperback entry for Lantern Hours ([[paperback-catalogue-copy-2025-11]]) was fixed six months before publication, and one word of mine changed in it.
