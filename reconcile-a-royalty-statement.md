# Reconcile A Royalty Statement

What I do with each royalty statement when it arrives, usually early April and early October. Written after the April 2026 statement surprised me ([[lantern-hours-royalty-statement-2026-04]]).

## Inputs

- The statement (PDF from the agency).
- The previous statement.
- The contract's royalty clauses, especially the high-discount clause and the reserve.
- Any sales figures from the publisher or agent in the period.

## Steps

1. Check the balance brought forward equals the last statement's balance carried forward.
2. For each line, check the royalty basis against the contract: percentage of RRP or of net receipts, and which discount triggers which.
3. Recompute each line: units times rate. Differences over 1 GBP get a query.
4. Check rights income: gross deal, publisher's share, credited amount.
5. Check the reserve: what percentage, on what lines, and whether any earlier reserve has been released.
6. Compare units with any figures given in the period. Differences of a few dozen copies are timing; more is a query.
7. Record the result in the statement's Source note: table, payable sum, net after commission, payment date.
8. Put the net payment into [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]] in the month after the payment date.
9. Queries go to [[bethan-okafor]] by email with line references.

## Verification

Payable figure on the statement equals my recomputation within a pound, or every difference has a written explanation.
