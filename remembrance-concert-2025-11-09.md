# Remembrance Concert, November 2025

The Wednesday choir sang at a remembrance concert on Sunday 2025-11-09, my first performance with them. Five hymns, one long silence, a reading of names by the organisers.

## What I noticed

- Choosing the programme had taken three weeks and a quarrel over one hymn that some thought too cheerful. A memorial programme is committee work, and the committee is never neutral. That went straight into Constance's memorial letter (third draft chapter 24) and the memorial chapter.
- Singing through the reading of names is impossible. We stopped, and Ingrid had planned for it.
- The audience sang the last hymn with us. The sound of a hall joining a choir it has not rehearsed with is different from the choir alone: rougher, louder, more moving.

## For the book

- The Saltmarrow memorial in December 1927 should be argued over beforehand, not simply sung.
- Restraint: what moved the room was the silence and the names, not the music. A note for [[is-the-memorial-chapter-too-sentimental]].
