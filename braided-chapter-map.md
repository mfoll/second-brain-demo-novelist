# Braided Chapter Map

> [!warning]
> Archived on 2026-07-13. This was the chapter plan for draft 2, the braid, abandoned at the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|review of 8 July 2026]]. The current plan is [[third-draft-chapter-map]].

## Shape

44 chapters in four parts, planned 2026-02-23 to 2026-03-20 after the [[editorial-call-2026-02-19|editorial call]].

- **Part one, "The Night"** (chapters 1 to 4): the storm night from the telegraph office, the slipway and the chapel, cut off before the boat returns.
- **Part two, "Before"** (chapters 5 to 28): three strands from 1924, rotating N, T, C. Every fourth chapter a short storm-night section (S) advancing the night by an hour.
- **Part three, "The Bar"** (chapters 29 to 36): the storm night in full, all three viewpoints.
- **Part four, "After"** (chapters 37 to 44): memorial, inquiry, 1928 to 1929.

## What went wrong, chapter by chapter

- The S sections (8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28) gave away the crew list by chapter 16. Readers were counting.
- Tobias's strand in part two jumped between 1924 to 1927 and memories of 1916 to 1918, inside chapters already displaced from the frame. [[tariq-mansour-continuity-notes-2026-06-30|Tariq's notes]] found four date conflicts.
- Part three repeated part one from other angles; it was the longest part and the slowest.

## What was salvaged

The Constance letters, nearly all of the Nell telegraph chapters, the rewritten storm sequence (with the pulling and sailing boat after May), and chapters 1 to 2 cut down to the [[storm-prologue]].
