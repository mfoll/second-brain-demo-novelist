# Paperback Cover Brief 2026

The Larkspur & Venn cover brief for the paperback, and [[felipe-araujo|Felipe Araujo]]'s two routes, seen on 2026-01-28.

## Key Details

- Brief: make it look like a reading-group book, not a prize hardback. Keep the lantern as a recognition device. Room for one review line.
- Route A: the hardback illustration redrawn in two colours, full bleed ([[paperback-cover-option-a]]).
- Route B: title in large type on a dark blue ground, small lantern device above the author name, Maya Okonjo's line at the foot.
- Chosen: B, on 2026-02-04. Sales preferred it on a shelf of paperbacks in a shop; I preferred it because it puts the words first.
