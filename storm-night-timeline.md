# Storm Night Timeline

An hour-by-hour spreadsheet of the storm night in the novel, from the first gale warning in the afternoon to first light: weather, tide, where each of the three viewpoint characters is, what messages pass through the telegraph office, the launch, the time to the drifter, the capsize on the return.

## Summary

Rebuilt on 2026-05-22 for the pulling and sailing lifeboat: the row to the bar takes about 50 minutes instead of 20 under engine, which moves the capsize later and puts it on the falling tide. Every storm-chapter fact must agree with this sheet.

## Why it matters

Chapters 21 to 25 and the prologue use it; Constance's storm letter (24) must match it as reported, a few days later and partly wrong. Research behind it lives in [[saltmarrow-research]].
