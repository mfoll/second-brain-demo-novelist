# Salt Choir Delivery Schedule (Original)

Superseded on 2026-08-28 by the [[salt-choir-contract-variation-2026-08-28|contract variation]] and the third draft's schedule in [[salt-choir-third-draft]]. Kept because it shows the plan the rewrite broke, and because the cash forecast of January was built on it ([[cash-flow-forecast-2026-01]]).

## The Plan, As Written In January 2026

- 2025-12-19: first draft to Imogen (done).
- January 2026: editorial letter; revision plan by mid-February.
- February to June: revision of the first draft, about 4,000 words of change a week. Structure unchanged.
- 2026-06-30: revised draft to Imogen.
- July and August: line edit.
- 2026-09-30: delivery, per the [[salt-choir-contract]].
- October: acceptance. November: tranche.

## What Happened Instead

The editorial letter of 2026-01-30 asked for more than a revision. The braided rebuild started on 2026-02-23, draft 2 went in on 2026-06-12, and the review of 2026-07-08 partly reversed it ([[salt-choir-structure-history]]). The plan above assumed a book whose structure was settled. It was not.
