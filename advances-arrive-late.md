# Advances Arrive Late

## Stance

Plan cash on the date money is likely to arrive, not the date a contract names. Every advance tranche, royalty payment and reserve release goes into the forecast at least one month after its trigger, and the trigger itself is assumed to slip.

## Scope

Publishing income: advance tranches, royalties, rights income, reserve releases. Not teaching fees, which Carrowmede has paid within three weeks of a timesheet every term.

## Why I Think This

- The January 2026 forecast put the Salt Choir delivery tranche in November 2026. It is now in May 2027 ([[cash-flow-forecast-2026-01]], [[delivery-date-extension]]).
- Signature tranches took five and six weeks to reach me on both contracts.
- "Delivery and acceptance" is two events, and the second is not mine.
- A [[reserve-against-returns]] is money owed, not cash.

## What Would Change My Mind

A run of two contracts where tranches arrive on their contract dates. Unlikely to happen before I stop caring.

## Implications

- The May 2027 tranche is in the forecast as May; [[neil-gathercole]] would put it in June, and the June case is on its own tab.
- Grants are not in the base case until awarded ([[vellacott-grant-application]]).
- When a decision depends on a payment, ask what happens if it is two months late.
