# Writing Retreat 2026

Two weeks at Ferrow House, a rural writers' residency, to get part one of the third draft of The Salt Choir finished away from the class, the inbox and the publicity for the paperback.

## Outcome

A two-week residency at Ferrow House (2026-08-10 to 2026-08-21) in which part one of the third draft was finished. Reached: part one (chapters 1 to 12, 38,600 words) was finished on 2026-08-20.

## Current State

- Completed 2026-08-21. Account of the fortnight in [[ferrow-house-residency-2026-08-10]] and the [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-08-21|last-day recap]].
