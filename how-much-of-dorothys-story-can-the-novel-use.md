# How Much Of Dorothy's Story Can The Novel Use?

Dorothy Pengelly gave me her mother's working life on the curing yards. The consent form allows background use and rules out direct quotation without asking again ([[dorothy-pengelly-consent-form]]). The question is the grey zone between the two: when does texture become her story retold?

## Current Answer

Provisional. Texture yes, story no. I use what any gutter of the period would have known (the crew of three, cotton-bound fingers, singing to keep pace) because the photographs and ledgers at the Merrin corroborate it. I do not use anything that only her family would know: a particular injury, a particular quarrel, the story she told after the recorder was off.

## Tests I am using

1. Could I have found it somewhere else? If yes, it is texture.
2. Would Dorothy recognise it as hers if she read the chapter? If yes, ask her.
3. Does it carry a plot? Her material may colour a scene, never drive one.
