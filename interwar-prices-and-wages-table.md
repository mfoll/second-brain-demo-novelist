# Interwar Prices And Wages Table

A small spreadsheet of what things cost and what people earned in a town like Saltmarrow in the 1920s, as ranges, with the source of each line.

## Summary

About 60 lines: gutters' season earnings, a clerk's weekly wage, a coxswain's retainer and launch payments, rent for a cottage on the Brae, a hymnal, a wireless set, a stamp and a telegram. Each line carries its source (ledger notes, background reading, or "guess, check") and the year.

## Why It Matters

Stops the draft inventing prices. Nell's wage at the telegraph office and the cost of the Institute's wireless set both come from it. Lines marked "guess, check" are open entries waiting in the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]].

## Caveats

Ranges, not figures. National averages do not describe a poor herring town, and the novel never states a price it does not need.
