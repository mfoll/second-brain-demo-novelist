# Sunniva Brekke Notes On Memorial Chapter 2026-09

[[sunniva-brekke|Sunniva]]'s pencil notes on the draft 2 memorial chapter, which I gave her in August as a test for the third-draft rewrite.

## Summary

Eleven cuts marked, every one an adjective or adverb within a sentence of the hymn: "mournful", "swelling", "trembling" and so on. Her note at the end: let the hymn's words and the parts do it; the reader already knows who is missing. I took nine of the eleven.

## Why it matters

The working rules for the rewrite of chapter 25: [[is-the-memorial-chapter-too-sentimental]].
