# Adult Education

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-10-20, read on 2026-10-21 after Gwen's offer of a Thursday class.

## Summary

Adult education covers systematic learning by adults beyond their initial schooling, from basic literacy to lifelong learning for its own sake. The lead section rests on the assumption that adults can and want to learn, take responsibility for their learning, and expect it to respond to their needs; what they learn and how is shaped by circumstances, globalisation and technology.

## Why It Matters

It put words to something the class taught me: my students choose what they bring and what they want from it, and a session works when it serves their drafts rather than my syllabus. That is the reason for the enrolment rule of the revision term ([[evening-class-autumn-2026]]) and for keeping my own book out of the room ([[run-a-class-session]]). It also made me ask what a "general fiction" Thursday group would want, which I do not yet know ([[should-i-teach-a-second-weekly-class]]).

## Caveats

General and mostly about policy and literacy. Nothing on creative-writing classes specifically.
