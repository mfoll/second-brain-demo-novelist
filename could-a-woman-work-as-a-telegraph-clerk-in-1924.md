# Could A Woman Work As A Telegraph Clerk In 1924?

Nell leaves the curing yards in March 1924 for a clerk's place at the Saltmarrow telegraph office. Was that plausible, or would the post have gone to a man as a matter of course?

## Current Answer

Plausible, and better than plausible: women had worked as telegraph operators since the nineteenth century, and a small post office employing a young woman as counter and telegraph clerk is ordinary for the period. What is unusual is a gutter getting the place, which is the point of the chapter. Answered 2025-02-11, before the first chapter was written.

## Consequence

Nell got the job by being quicker at sums than the postmaster's nephew. The [[character-bible]] gives Walter Pascoe a reason to prefer her: his own son, killed in 1916, would have had the place.
