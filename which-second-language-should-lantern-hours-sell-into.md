# Which Second Language Should Lantern Hours Sell Into?

Not really my decision; Sigrun sells to whoever offers. The question is where I put my own effort: which reader's report I read closely, whose queries I answer first.

## Current Answer

Provisional: Portuguese, because I can judge the reader's report and the translator's sample myself, and because a Portuguese edition of a book by a translator from Portuguese has a story Sigrun can sell. German is the larger market and the likelier money.

## Evidence

- Portuguese: reader's report due in November.
- Korean: unsolicited enquiry, early.
- French: two declines in 2026.

## What would settle it
