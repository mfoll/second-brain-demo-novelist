# Lifeboat (rescue)

## Summary

A rescue lifeboat goes out to a vessel in distress, or to its survivors, to save crew and passengers. The article notes that such boats can be hand pulled, sail powered or engine powered, and rigid, inflatable or a combination.

## Why It Matters

First read in March 2025, when it made me move the lifeboat house out of the harbour ([[saltmarrow-map-redrawn-2025-03-14]]). Reread in May 2026 alongside the archive records: the article's plain statement that lifeboats were pulled, sailed or powered was the general frame; the station records at the [[merrin-archive-visit-2026-05-12]] gave the evidence that small stations still had pulling and sailing boats in the late 1920s. Answer in [[would-saltmarrows-lifeboat-have-had-an-engine-in-1927]].

## Key Details

- Three means of propulsion: oars, sail, engine.
- The article is about the type of craft, not about any service or station.

## Caveats

It does not date the change from pulling to motor boats for small stations; that came from the archive.
