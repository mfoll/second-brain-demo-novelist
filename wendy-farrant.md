# Wendy Farrant

Novelist in her seventies, eleven novels, and my informal mentor since I moved from translation to fiction. Not in the writing group; we meet for lunch every two or three months.

## How we work together

She reads selectively, when I ask, and answers in one sentence I keep thinking about for months. She does not give line notes and does not want my thanks in acknowledgements, which I will ignore.

## What she contributed

- At lunch on 2025-10-08 she said Nell was "listening at keyholes" to report a town she could not see ([[wendy-farrant-lunch-2025-10-08]], with her follow-up letter [[wendy-farrant-letter-2025-10-10]]). I noted it and finished the first draft anyway. Imogen's editorial letter said the same thing three months later.
- She was the first person to tell me a second novel is usually harder than the first, and why: the first one is written without an audience.

## Current threads

- Lunch planned for early December, after part two is done. She has asked to read nothing until the whole third draft exists.
