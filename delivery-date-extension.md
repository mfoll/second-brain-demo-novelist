# Delivery Date Extension

Getting The Salt Choir's delivery date moved from 30 September 2026 to 31 March 2027 by a variation to the [[salt-choir-contract]], and understanding what that did to the money.

## Outcome

A contract variation signed on 2026-08-28 moving delivery to 2027-03-31, and the delivery tranche with it. Reached.

## Current State

- Signed by both parties on 2026-08-28 ([[salt-choir-contract-variation-2026-08-28]], [[contract-variation-signed-2026-08-28]]).
- New delivery date 2027-03-31. The third draft goes to [[imogen-lacey]] by 2027-01-29 so that one round of notes fits before delivery ([[salt-choir-third-draft]]).
- The second tranche of the 24,000 GBP advance (8,000 GBP, payable on delivery and acceptance) moved with the date. Forecast receipt: May 2027, net 6,800 GBP after commission ([[advances-arrive-late]]).
- Nothing else in the contract changed: same advance, same option clause, same royalty rates. Publication moves from spring 2027 to spring 2028, which is Larkspur & Venn's decision, not part of the variation.
- Consequence: a six-month cash gap, November 2026 to April 2027, now handled in [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]], [[vellacott-grant-application]] and [[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]].

## Why It Was Needed

The braided rewrite took from February to July and was then partly reversed at the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|review of 8 July]]. That cost about five months. A third draft starting 13 July could not be delivered, revised and accepted by 30 September. The structural story is in [[salt-choir-structure-history]]; this project is only the contractual and cash side.

## Options Considered

1. **Deliver draft 3 unfinished on 30 September** and revise after. Rejected by Callum and by me: acceptance would not follow, so the tranche would not either, and Imogen would read a book I knew was not ready.
2. **Move delivery by three months to 2026-12-31.** My first ask. Rejected after the call of 14 July: Imogen's notes on draft 3 could not come back before Christmas, and acceptance would slip into March anyway.
3. **Split the delivery tranche**, half on a partial delivery of part one, half on full acceptance. Callum asked; Larkspur & Venn declined on 2026-08-06, as their contracts do not pay on partial manuscripts ([[should-the-delivery-tranche-move-or-be-split]]).
4. **Move delivery six months to 2027-03-31.** Chosen.

## Working Context

[[callum-pryce]] negotiated; [[bethan-okafor]] drafted the variation; Imogen agreed it in principle on 2026-07-24, before her August leave. The original writing schedule to the September date is kept as [[salt-choir-delivery-schedule-original]] (archived). The cash picture before the move is preserved in [[cash-flow-forecast-2026-01]] (archived), which assumed the delivery tranche in November 2026.
