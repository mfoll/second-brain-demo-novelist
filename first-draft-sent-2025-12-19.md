# First Draft Sent 2025-12-19

Sent the first complete draft of The Salt Choir to [[imogen-lacey|Imogen Lacey]] and [[callum-pryce|Callum Pryce]] on Friday 19 December 2025. About 112,000 words, 41 chapters, linear 1924 to 1929, Nell's viewpoint only ([[salt-choir-first-draft-manuscript]]). Daily context in [[recaps/daily/2025/2025-12-19|the recap for the day]].

## What went with it

A cover note saying what I knew was wrong: the storm comes late, at chapter 31; the last part (1928 to 1929) is thin; Constance is a sketch. I asked Imogen two questions: does the town come alive, and does the storm justify the wait?

## What I did wrong

- Finished on 17 December and sent two days later after a fast skim rather than a real read. The [[full-draft-read-through]] Procedure exists partly because of this.
- Sent on a Friday before Christmas, then spent the holiday rereading it in my head.

- Teodora's on 2026-01-20 ([[teodora-reader-notes-draft-one-2026-01-20]]).
- Imogen's editorial letter on 2026-01-30 ([[imogen-editorial-letter-2026-01-30]]), answering both questions: the town, partly; the storm, no.
