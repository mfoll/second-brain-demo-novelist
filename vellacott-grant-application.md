# Vellacott Grant Application

An application to the Vellacott Fund for Writers for a Development Award of 6,000 GBP, to cover writing time on the third draft of The Salt Choir through the cash gap that opened when delivery moved.

## Outcome

A Development Award of 6,000 GBP from the Vellacott Fund. Application submitted 2026-10-16; decision expected 2026-12-11.

## Current State

- Submitted on 2026-10-16, two days before the deadline ([[vellacott-application-submitted-2026-10-16]]). Acknowledgement received the same afternoon from [[ana-lucia-ferreira]].
- Decision expected 2026-12-11. Awards are paid in one sum within 30 days of acceptance, so a yes would land in early January 2027, just as the [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]] drops below my 2,000 GBP floor.
- Eligibility settled before I started writing: a contracted book can be funded if the advance and its schedule are declared ([[does-the-vellacott-award-fund-a-contracted-book]], [[vellacott-eligibility-call-2026-09-08]]).
- Reference from [[imogen-lacey]] sent directly to the fund on 2026-10-12 ([[imogen-lacey-reference-vellacott-2026]]).
- Nothing to do until December except not counting the money.

## Scope and Boundaries

The application, its budget and the decision. What I do with the award, if it comes, is a forecast change and belongs in [[update-the-cash-flow-forecast]]. The commission question is separate, though the two are linked: if the award is refused, the case for Ashlar gets stronger ([[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]]).

## What Went In

From [[vellacott-application-2026]] and [[vellacott-development-award-guidelines-2026]]:

- Statement of 980 words (limit 1,000): what the third draft is, why the structure changed, what six months of protected time buys.
- Writing sample of 4,800 words (limit 5,000): the opening of part one from draft 3, after the storm prologue. I left the prologue out, as Callum would have wanted anyway.
- Budget ([[vellacott-budget-2026]]): 6,000 GBP as writing time, 1,000 GBP a month for six months, November 2026 to April 2027, with the advance schedule declared: 8,000 GBP received on signature in 2025, 8,000 GBP on delivery and acceptance now expected May 2027, 8,000 GBP on hardback publication.
- Publication evidence: Lantern Hours hardback and paperback.
- One reference: Imogen.

## Rejected Approaches

- Asking for research costs (a second archive trip, the community choir's music) as well as time. The fund prefers one clear use, and the research is done.
- Using [[wendy-farrant]] as referee. She offered; the guidelines prefer someone who knows the current project, which means the editor.
- Applying in the spring round. Too late for the gap.
