# Imogen Lacey

Fiction publishing director at Larkspur & Venn and my editor on both novels. She acquired Lantern Hours in 2023 and The Salt Choir in 2025.

## Role

Editor: structural notes by letter, then line edits on the accepted text. She commissions the copy-edit ([[joan-whitlock]]) and works with sales ([[martin-okello]]) and publicity ([[priyanka-desai]]) on how the book is presented. [[tariq-mansour]] assists her and does continuity checks.

## How we work together

- Big notes arrive as a letter of six to ten pages, then a video call a few weeks later once I have had time to disagree with it. I read the letter twice, wait three days, then write down what I think the problem is before reading her solutions again ([[editors-diagnose-better-than-they-prescribe]]).
- She diagnoses precisely and prescribes lightly. On the [[editorial-call-2026-02-19|February call]] the braid was my proposal, not hers; she said yes to it, and on the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|July review]] she was the first to say it had not worked.
- She does not like being surprised by public statements about a book, reasonably.
