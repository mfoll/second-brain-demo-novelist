# Rights Strategy Call 2025-09-04

Video call with [[sigrun-aas|Sigrun Aas]] and [[callum-pryce|Callum Pryce]] to plan translation sales for Lantern Hours ahead of the autumn book fairs.

## Decisions

- Pitch the book on its translation theme, not as "debut literary fiction". Sigrun to rewrite the entry in [[lantern-hours-rights-guide-2025]].
- Priority languages: Italian and Portuguese (my own languages give the pitch credibility), German as a stretch.
- My role: answer translators' and editors' questions when asked; never comment on a sample or a translator's choices unless the buying publisher asks. I proposed this myself. Twenty-two years on the other side taught me what an author looking over your shoulder costs.

## Rejected

- Callum suggested I approach two Portuguese editors I know from translating. Rejected: it would bypass Sigrun, and those editors buy from agents they trust, not from former translators.

## Follow-up

- Sigrun's guide entry went out in October. The Italian deal followed in March ([[casa-velluto-italian-rights-deal-2026-03-09]]).
