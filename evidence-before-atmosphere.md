# Evidence Before Atmosphere

## Stance

In research, read the dull documents before the beautiful ones. Ledgers, crew lists and service records first; photographs, memoirs and interviews after. Atmosphere is easy to absorb and hard to correct; evidence is the other way round.

## Scope

Archive days and research reading for The Salt Choir. Not reading for pleasure, and not the oral history, which is atmosphere by nature and handled under its own rules.

## Why I Think This

- [[merrin-first-visit-2025-06-10]]: four hours on photographs, forty minutes on ledgers, and the ledgers turned out to matter more.
- [[merrin-archive-visit-2026-05-12]]: done the other way round, and the day answered the question I went with.
- The engine in the storm chapter survived fourteen months because the atmosphere of the scene felt right.

## What Would Change My Mind

An archive day where the photographs answered the question and the documents did not. Possible for clothing and buildings.

## Implications

Book documents for the morning and images for the afternoon. The same order in [[check-a-chapter-for-anachronisms]]: check facts first, voice second.
