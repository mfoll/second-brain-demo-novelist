# Wednesday Choir Rehearsal Notes

My notes from rehearsals with [[ingrid-solheim]]'s community choir since 2025-09-10, transcribed monthly from notebook twelve. Not a diary of the choir: only what bears on how a choir works.

## Summary

- How Ingrid teaches a part: line by line, by ear, each section alone, then pairs, then all four. Altos and tenors last because their lines make least sense alone.
- The sound of a choir that has just found a chord, and the sound of one pretending. Useful for Constance's letters.
- Committee and programme: who chooses, who objects, who pays for music.
- Her answers to my questions of October 2025 about a 1920s chapel choir, kept separately in [[ingrid-solheim-choir-questions-2025-10]].

## Why It Matters

Most of Constance's rehearsal detail in the letters comes from here, translated back a century and checked against the [[chapel-hymnal-1926]].

## Caveats

A modern community choir is not a 1920s chapel choir: mixed beliefs, no minister, sheet music for everyone. I note the differences as carefully as the likenesses.
