# Carrowmede Tutor Agreement 2025

Sessional tutor agreement between me and the Carrowmede Adult Education Centre, signed 20 August 2025. Rolling, renewed by email each term.

## Key Details

- Role: tutor for the Tuesday evening creative-writing class, 19:00 to 21:00, ten sessions a term.
- Rate: 40 GBP an hour of contact time, 80 GBP a session, 800 GBP a term. Preparation is included in the rate.
- Payment: on a termly timesheet signed by the coordinator, paid within 30 days (in practice about three weeks).
- Self-employed; I invoice through the timesheet.
- Class size: maximum 14. Minimum 8 for the class to run.
- Learner data handled under the centre's policy ([[carrowmede-learner-data-policy]]).
- Either side may end the agreement at the end of a term with four weeks' notice.

## Why It Matters

The fee basis for every class project and the forecast's most reliable line. The minimum of eight is why [[gwen-attah]] moved the class up the brochure in July 2026.
