# History of radio

## Summary

The history of radio runs from the scientific discovery of electromagnetic radiation in the late nineteenth century through the development of better transmitting and receiving equipment. The article follows the technology and then broadcasting as it grew in the twentieth century.

## Why It Matters

Read at Ferrow House on 2026-08-14, when the anachronism check on part one reached chapter 11 and the wireless set at the chapel social. It supported the answer in [[should-saltmarrow-have-a-wireless-set-by-1927]]: a set was possible by 1926 but not common in a poor town, so one set, bought by the Institute, is right. Also background for [[how-would-saltmarrow-hear-of-the-wall-street-crash]].

## Key Details

- Broadcasting followed decades of technical development of transmitters and receivers.
- Uptake of receiving sets was gradual, shaped by cost and reception.

## Caveats

A broad international history. How many sets a small coastal town had in 1926 is my judgement.
