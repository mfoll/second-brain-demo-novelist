# Nonlinear narrative

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-02-16 with [[in-medias-res]], while writing out the three options for the [[editorial-call-2026-02-19|editorial call]].

## Summary

Defines nonlinear (also disjointed or disrupted) narrative as telling events out of chronological order or otherwise outside their direct causal sequence, including parallel plot lines and stories nested inside the main one. Notes the technique is common in hypertext fiction and long established in print.

## Why It Matters

The braid of draft 2 was a nonlinear narrative of the parallel-lines kind, with a frame on the storm night. The article's list of forms helped me name what I was proposing; it did not warn me what it would cost a reader to track three strands in different years. That lesson is in [[braided-narrative]] and [[salt-choir-structure-history]].

## Caveats

Descriptive, not evaluative.
