# Notebooks Beat Apps For First Capture

## Stance

First capture goes on paper, in a notebook I carry. The vault is for what survives a month on paper. Typing straight into an app captures more and keeps less.

## Scope

Craft notes, overheard speech, ideas for scenes, reading notes. Not research references (those go straight to the research folder with a citation) and not anything with a date attached (the calendar).

## Why I Think This

- Twenty-two years of translation notebooks. The ones I can still use are the ones I wrote by hand, because writing by hand made me choose.
- The [[craft-notebook-digitisation]] project: about 260 of perhaps 4,000 entries were worth keeping. The monthly transcription is the filter, and the filter is the point.
- A notebook on the table in an archive or a class is polite in a way a screen is not.
- The [[commonplace-book]] tradition, which I knew from translation history before I knew I was keeping one.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the monthly transcription slipped for three months running and I lost something I needed. It slipped once, in June 2026 during the paperback tour, and I lost nothing.

## Implications

- Keep the transcription monthly ([[transcribe-a-notebook-into-the-vault]]).
- Do not recommend this to students as a rule; several of them write better on phones. I say it is what works for me.
