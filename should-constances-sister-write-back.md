# Should Constance's sister write back?

## Question

Constance's chapters are letters to her sister Agnes. Should Agnes's replies appear on the page?

## Current Answer

No. Agnes never appears. Constance sometimes answers a question Agnes has evidently asked, which is all the reader gets. Decided in March 2026 while planning the letters in draft 2, and kept in the third draft.

## Why

- Replies would make it a correspondence novel and give Agnes a voice competing with three viewpoint characters.
- One-way letters keep Constance's account partial; the reader has only her version ([[unreliable-narrator]]).
- I tried two replies in March. They were the worst pages of the draft: Agnes only existed to prompt Constance.

## Caveats

Imogen asked once whether a single reply at the very end might land. Not now; maybe as part of [[does-the-salt-choir-need-a-1929-coda]].
