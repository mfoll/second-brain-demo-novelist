# Expense Categories

The categories I use for business expenses, agreed with [[neil-gathercole]] in January 2026. One tab per category in the accounts workbook.

| Category | What goes in | 2025 total (GBP) |
|---|---|---|
| Agency commission | 15% home, 20% translation rights | 1,800 |
| Research travel | Archive visits, the choir, interviews; mileage logged | 640 |
| Books and research materials | Research books, archive copying fees, maps | 410 |
| Subscriptions and software | Scrivener, the spreadsheet, a newspaper archive, a writers' society membership | 180 |
| Accountant | Neil's fixed fee | 420 |
| Use of home as office | Flat rate on hours worked at home | 312 |
| Events | Travel to readings, festival costs not reimbursed | 0 in 2025 (all reimbursed) |
| Residencies | Ferrow House fees and travel | New for 2026 |

## Rules

- Research books yes, novels read for pleasure no, even when I insist ([[accounts-meeting-2026-01-21]]).
- Travel logged at the time in [[research-travel-log-2026]].
- Receipts scanned monthly into the year's folder ([[expense-receipts-2025]]).
- Nothing personal, nothing household.
