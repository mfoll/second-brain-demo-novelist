# Storm Chapter Versions

A folder holding every version of the storm material across all three drafts, so I can compare without digging through Scrivener snapshots.

## Contents

- Draft 1, chapters 31 to 33 (2025): Nell's view only; motor lifeboat.
- Draft 2, chapters 1 to 2 and the storm-night interludes (March 2026): motor lifeboat.
- Draft 2 storm sequence rewritten 2026-05-15 to 2026-05-22 for a ten-oar pulling and sailing boat, after the archive visit and the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log|anachronism log entry of 14 May]].
- Prologue versions 1 to 3 (2026-07-15, 2026-07-22, 2026-08-21); see [[storm-prologue]].
- Third-draft chapters 21 to 23: not yet written.

## Why it matters

The storm is the book's centre and has been rewritten more than anything else. The oar-powered version changes the physical story: fatigue, time to the bar, the crew's positions at the oars.
