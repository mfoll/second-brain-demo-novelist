# Paperback

English Wikipedia article on the paperback format, read on 2026-01-13, the day after the first paperback planning call with Priyanka. (The live article has since been edited; last edit seen 2026-10-22.)

## Summary

Defines the paperback by its binding (a paper or card cover, usually glued) against the hardback's boards, and covers the history of cheap paper-covered editions, the split between mass-market and larger trade formats, and the economics that let publishers reissue a hardback cheaply once its first sales are done.

## Why It Matters

Priyanka's plan treats the paperback as a second publication for a different readership rather than a cheaper copy of the first ([[paperback-campaign-plan-2026]]). The article's account of why publishers wait before issuing a paperback helped me see why the year between hardback and paperback was not wasted time, and why a new cover ([[paperback-cover-brief-2026]]) and a reading group guide made sense.

## Caveats

Format history and terminology only. Nothing on royalty rates; those come from the [[lantern-hours-contract]].
