# Salt Choir Synopsis 2026

One-page synopsis of the third-draft book, written 2026-09-24 at Imogen's request for Larkspur & Venn's 2028 planning meeting. Replaces the archived [[salt-choir-synopsis-2025]].

## Summary

Three voices in Saltmarrow, 1924 to 1929: a gutting girl turned telegraph clerk, a lifeboat coxswain back from the war, and a vicar's widow who leads the chapel choir in letters to her sister. A short opening on the night of the storm; then five years, in order, towards it and away from it. Does not say who dies or whether Tobias survives.

## Why it matters

The only description of the book circulating inside the publisher. [[martin-okello]] read it and raised the title ([[title-call-2026-10-01]]).
