# Carrowmede Learner Data Policy

The centre's learner data and safeguarding policy for sessional tutors, issued with the tutor agreement on 20 August 2025.

## Key Details

- Learners' contact details are held by the centre. Tutors contact learners through the centre's system, not personal email or phone.
- Tutors may keep attendance and teaching notes for the course, but not personal information beyond what teaching needs.
- Learners' work belongs to the learners. Tutors may not publish, share or reuse it without written or recorded agreement.
- Disclosures of a safeguarding nature go to the coordinator the same day.

## Why It Matters

The rules behind [[student-writing-privacy-rules]], which are slightly stricter: I keep no notes on memoir content at all.
