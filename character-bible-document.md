# Character Bible Document

The working character bible for The Salt Choir: one Scrivener character sheet per principal character, fourteen in all, plus a change list. Last amended 2026-05-14.

## Summary

Each sheet: name, birth year, household, street, work in 1924 and 1929, war (served, lost, avoided), religion and chapel or church, speech habits, what they want, and what they would never say. The change list records every amendment with date and reason, for example Agnes named on 2026-01-27 and the crew fixed on 2026-05-14.

## Why It Matters

The project note [[character-bible]] summarises the table; the sheets hold the detail the draft is checked against.

Scrivener project, Research binder, folder "Characters"; exported as PDF to the research folder ([[salt-choir-research-dossier]]) after each amendment.
