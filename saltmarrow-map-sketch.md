# Saltmarrow Map Sketch

Version 2 of the hand-drawn map of Saltmarrow, drawn on 2025-03-14 and updated on 2025-06-12 after the [[merrin-first-visit-2025-06-10|first Merrin visit]].

## Summary

A bay facing north-east with the harbour at its east end, the bar across the mouth, and the shingle beach to the west. Three bands of streets: the Strand on the water, Fore Street behind it, the Brae climbing above. The lifeboat house alone at the west end of the Strand with its slipway to the beach. Bethel chapel at the top of Chapel Brae, St Anne's on its green to the west. The three curing yards and the cooperage on the Strand, added in June.

## Why It Matters

The [[saltmarrow-town-bible]] describes the town; the map stops me walking characters uphill to the harbour. Supersedes [[saltmarrow-map-sketch-v1]].
