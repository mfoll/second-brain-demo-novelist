# Does Translating Again Damage My Own Voice?

If I translate another writer's novel while revising my own, does their voice leak into mine, or does the discipline help?

## Why It Matters

It is the craft half of [[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]], and the part Callum is really worried about when he says the third draft is the book's last chance.

## What I Know From Before

- In 2016 and 2017, co-translating with [[aurelio-santangelo]], I wrote nothing of my own. I thought at the time that translating used the same muscle.
- Lantern Hours was written in 2021 and 2022, as the last two translations finished. Its early chapters are, by Imogen's word, "careful". The later ones, written after the last translation was delivered, are not.
- The habits that helped Constance's letters (making a voice consistent across a whole book) are translation habits ([[translation-and-craft]]).

## What I Do Not Know

Whether seven weeks of translating after delivery, in April and May 2027, would affect a book that is by then delivered. Probably not. Whether translating during the third draft would: probably yes, which is why option 4 is rejected.
