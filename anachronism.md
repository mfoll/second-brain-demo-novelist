# Anachronism

## Summary

An anachronism is a chronological inconsistency: people, events, objects, words or customs placed in a period where they do not belong. The article says the commonest kind is an object out of its time, but that it can equally be an expression, a technology, an idea, a musical style, a material, a plant or animal, or a custom.

## Why It Matters

Read in January 2025 when I started the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]]. Its list of kinds became the log's checklist (objects, words, technology, music), and its omission, attitudes, is the gap I wrote up as [[anachronism-of-attitude]]. The music item caught a hymn tune that was too late for 1925.

## Key Details

- Object anachronisms are the commonest.
- Words, technologies, ideas, music and customs can all be out of time.
