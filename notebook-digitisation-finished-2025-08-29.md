# Notebook Digitisation Finished

On 2025-08-29 I finished notebook eleven, the last of the 2019 to 2025 notebooks, and closed [[craft-notebook-digitisation]] after twenty-one weeks.

## Numbers

- Eleven notebooks, about 1,900 pages.
- About 260 entries marked worth keeping, condensed into 23 vault notes rather than 260 fragments.
- Roughly 70 evenings of typing.

## What I decided at the end

- The paper is the record and stays on the shelf ([[craft-notebooks-2019-2025]]); the vault holds the index ([[craft-notebook-index]]) and the craft notes.
- From notebook twelve onwards, transcribe monthly rather than in a heap: [[transcribe-a-notebook-into-the-vault]].
- Personal entries are not transcribed.

The surprise was how much of the translation work generalised to the novel. The best craft notes I have on period voice, [[period-texture-vs-period-trivia]] among them, began as notes on translating someone else's 1950s.
