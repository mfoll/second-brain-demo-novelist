# Flashback (narrative)

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-07-11, between the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|draft two review]] and the third-draft planning day, while deciding how Tobias's war should appear.

## Summary

Defines the flashback, or analepsis, as a scene that takes the narrative back from its current point, usually to supply backstory; the flashforward is the opposite. Distinguishes internal analepsis (back to an earlier point inside the story) from external analepsis (to a time before the story began).

## Why It Matters

Tobias's two war scenes (chapters 7 and 17) are external analepses: the war is before 1924. Draft 2 mixed both kinds inside a frame that was itself a flashforward, which is why readers lost him. The [[storm-prologue]] is, in the article's terms, a flashforward, which helps explain why Callum feels it gives something away ([[should-the-salt-choir-keep-the-storm-prologue]]).

## Caveats

Terminology only.
