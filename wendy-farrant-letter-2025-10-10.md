# Wendy Farrant Letter 2025-10-10

A handwritten card from [[wendy-farrant|Wendy]] two days after our [[wendy-farrant-lunch-2025-10-08|lunch]], following up on what she had said about Nell.

## Summary

One side of a card. She lists the six scenes in chapters 1 to 20 where Nell is present only to report, and adds: finish it, then ask who else lives there. She also says she should not have said "keyholes" so bluntly, and that she would say it again.

## Why it matters

The first precise list of the scenes that later went ([[salt-choir-cut-list]]), four months before Imogen's letter.
