# Openings Handout 2025

Handout for the first session I taught, 16 September 2025: twelve first lines, six from public-domain novels and six invented by me, unlabelled.

## Contents

- Twelve first lines, numbered. The key is on the back: six from novels published before 1931 (including Mrs Dalloway and To the Lighthouse), six of mine.
- Three questions: what does this line promise; who is speaking; what would make you stop reading.
- Exercise: three openings for one story, starting with a person, a place, a problem.

## Why It Matters

Used in three terms. The class spots my invented lines too easily, which says something about my invented lines. Two examples need replacing before the spring 2027 term ([[teach-creative-writing]]).
