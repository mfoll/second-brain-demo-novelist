# Ashlar Sample Chapter

The French sample chapter (about 6,500 words) and two-page synopsis that [[celine-marchetti]] sent with the offer of 30 September 2026. Read on 1 October. Title and author withheld from the vault at Celine's request until a contract is signed.

## My Reading Notes

- Contemporary, close third, one point of view, a family and a harbour town in decline. Long sentences with many subordinate clauses, which in English will need breaking without losing the drift.
- Dialogue in a regional register with little dialect spelling. Needs an English register that is not borrowed from any real region; the same problem as Saltmarrow.
- Difficulty: medium. About 1,200 finished words a day at my old pace.
- Quality: good. I would be glad to have translated it.

## Why It Matters

Whether the book is worth doing is part of [[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]]. It is. That makes declining harder, not easier.
