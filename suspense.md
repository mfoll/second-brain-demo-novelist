# Suspense

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-07-06, preparing for the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|draft two review]].

## Summary

Defines suspense as anxiety or excitement produced by uncertainty, and in narrative as the audience's excited anticipation about the plot, especially where it affects a character the audience cares about.

## Why It Matters

The phrase about a character the audience cares about is the crux of the prologue argument. Callum says a storm prologue removes uncertainty about the outcome; I say it creates anticipation that attaches to every man in the crew once the reader cares about them. My working distinction is in [[suspense-versus-surprise]].
