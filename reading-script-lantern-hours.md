# Reading Script Lantern Hours

The passages I read at events, with timings. Revised after each reading.

## Standard set (about 11 minutes)

1. Chapter 3 opening, the mentor's desk: 4 minutes. Pages 31 to 34 of the paperback. Stop at "and the pencil was still sharp".
2. One-sentence setup: "Ines has been asked to find a word that does not exist in English."
3. The dictionary scene, chapter 7: 6 minutes. Pages 88 to 93, cutting the paragraph about the bus.
4. Last page, only if the room is warm and nobody has said they haven't finished the book: 1 minute.

## Variants

- Translators in the room: swap the last page for the proverb passage in chapter 12 (corrected in the paperback; see [[paperback-proof-corrections-2026]]).
- Library groups: they have read it. Read less, talk more; one passage, then questions.

## Rejected

- Anything from the middle third. The reviews were right about it ([[lantern-hours-hardback-reviews-2025]]).
