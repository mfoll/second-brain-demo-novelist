# Third Draft Chapter Map

The plan for the third draft of The Salt Choir as at 2026-10-21. Chronological, three parts, 33 chapters plus the prologue. N = Nell, T = Tobias, C = Constance (letter). The 66,900 words written so far include the prologue. Replaces the archived [[braided-chapter-map]].

## Prologue

- November 1927, the launch. Four pages, about 1,100 words. Stops as the boat clears the harbour mouth. Names nobody ([[storm-prologue]]).
