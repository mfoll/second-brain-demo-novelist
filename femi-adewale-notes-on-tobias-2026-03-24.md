# Femi Adewale Notes On Tobias 2026-03-24

Three pages of notes from [[femi-adewale|Femi Adewale]] on the seven Tobias chapters of draft 2, sent before our [[femi-adewale-reading-call-2026-03-26|call of 26 March]].

## Summary

- Symptoms plausible but too constant; episodes are more credible than a permanent state for a working coxswain.
- Interior vocabulary too modern in eleven marked places.
- Local language would be "nerves", "not himself", "not steady"; a doctor in a small town might say "neurasthenia" or nothing.
- The war memories are fragmentary in a way that reads as a modern convention.
- One factual error: a detail of his demobilisation that Femi corrected.

## Why it matters

Shaped Tobias's third-draft chapters and [[how-much-of-tobiass-war-goes-on-the-page]]. Background in the [[shell-shock]] article and [[femi-adewale-reading-list-2026-03]].

## Caveats

Femi asked not to be quoted as an authority. These are a reader's notes from a historian, not a published source.
