# How Do I Stop Research Showing On The Page?

[[teodora-vlasic]] said of the first draft that she could hear me "showing my homework" in every curing-yard chapter. She was right, and the archive visits have made it worse: the more I know, the more I want to use.

## What I do now

- The test in [[period-texture-vs-period-trivia]]: would this character notice this, now? If not, it goes.
- The [[research-iceberg]]: decide in advance that most of the research stays under the water.
- Cut every sentence that explains a process to the reader rather than shows a character doing it. Nell never explains gutting; she has been doing it since she was fourteen.
- Read the chapter aloud. Research sounds like a lecture.

## Why still open

These are cuts after the fact. I want a way of drafting that does not put it in first. In the third draft, part one is better (Teodora's notes in September said so), but the storm chapters are the danger, because I now know a great deal about pulling lifeboats and want the reader to know it too. I will judge this question by part two.
