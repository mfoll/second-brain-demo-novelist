# Writing Income Overview

Where the money comes from, as of October 2026. Written so that I stop thinking of "the advance" as a salary.

## Sources Of Income

| Source | Typical amount | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advance tranches | 8,000 GBP gross, 6,800 net | Three per book, over about three years | [[advance-tranches]] |
| Royalties | From nothing to about 1,000 GBP a statement so far | April and October | Lantern Hours earned out on the October 2026 statement ([[earning-out]]) |
| Rights income | Credited to the royalty account, less publisher's share | With statements | Italian rights 1,500 GBP gross, 1,200 credited |
| Teaching | 800 GBP a term | About three weeks after each term | [[evening-class-autumn-2026]] |
| Readings and festivals | 100 to 250 GBP an event | Within a month | Nine events in 2025, 1,150 GBP |
| Public Lending Right | About 200 to 300 GBP | February | [[plr-statement-2026]] |
| Grants | Up to 6,000 GBP | Irregular | [[vellacott-grant-application]] |
| Old translations | Reprint fees, a few hundred | Irregular | 320 GBP in 2025 |

## The Shape Of A Year

2025 gross was 14,464 GBP, of which 12,000 GBP was two advance tranches ([[accounts-2025]]). 2026 has no advance tranche at all, since delivery moved. Without the tranches, my writing and teaching income is about 4,000 GBP a year. That is the number to remember.

## Rules

- Forecast net of commission ([[halloran-pryce-agency-agreement]]).
- Teaching is the only income that arrives when expected.
- Grants and commissions are scenarios until signed ([[advances-arrive-late]]).
