# Transcribe A Notebook Into The Vault

The monthly pass over the live paper notebook, turning what survived a month into vault notes. Came out of [[craft-notebook-digitisation]]. About an evening a month.

## Steps

1. Read the month's pages with a pencil. Tick entries still alive; cross nothing out.
2. For each ticked entry, decide where it goes:
   - a new idea about technique: an existing Concept, or a new one if nothing fits;
   - a stance I hold: an Opinion;
   - something about The Salt Choir: the relevant chapter note, the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]] or the research project;
   - a reading note: the [[reading-log-2025-2026]] or a Source;
   - personal: stays on paper.
3. Type it in my own present words, not a copy of the notebook line. Link it to its Topic.
4. Write the page range done at the bottom of the notebook page and in the [[craft-notebook-index]].
5. Anything that is a task goes to the calendar, not the vault.

## Rules

- Prefer updating an existing note to creating a new one.
- No handwriting recognition: it mangles my hand and my French ([[craft-notebook-digitisation]]).
- If a month is missed, do two the next month; do not let it heap up.
