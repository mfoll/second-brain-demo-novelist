# Gwen Attah

Adult learning coordinator at the Carrowmede Adult Education Centre. She runs the centre's evening programme, hired me, and is the person I deal with for rooms, enrolment, timesheets and anything that goes wrong on a Tuesday.

## How We Started

Gwen came to the Lantern Hours launch at Quire & Candle in February 2025 and stayed for the questions. In June she emailed to ask whether I would take over the Tuesday fiction class from a tutor who had retired. The tutor agreement was signed on 2025-08-20 ([[carrowmede-tutor-agreement-2025]]) and the first session ran on [[first-class-session-2025-09-16]].

## How We Work Together

- Email for anything formal; she answers within a day. A ten-minute chat in her office before class if something needs discussing.
- Outlines go to her eight weeks before term for the brochure. She edits the blurb and has never changed the content.
- Timesheets at the end of each term, signed by her, paid by the centre about three weeks later ([[carrowmede-timesheets-2026]]).
- She enforces the centre's learner data rules firmly, which suits me ([[carrowmede-learner-data-policy]]). Student contact goes through the centre's system, not my email.
- She protected the class when enrolment for autumn 2026 looked thin in July, by moving it up the brochure. It filled.
