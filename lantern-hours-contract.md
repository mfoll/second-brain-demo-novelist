# Lantern Hours Contract

Single-book agreement with Larkspur & Venn for Lantern Hours, signed 2023-06-14. Captured into the vault on 2025-01-08 from the signed copy.

## Key Details

- Advance 12,000 GBP in thirds of 4,000 GBP: signature (2023), delivery and acceptance (2024), hardback publication (paid March 2025).
- Royalties: hardback 10% of RRP; paperback 7.5% of RRP; ebook 25% of net receipts.
- Rights: English language, world. Translation rights to the publisher, author's share 80%. Audio and dramatic rights reserved, which is why [[audiobook-edition]] is our deal to make.
- No option clause. Callum traded it for the translation share.
- Statements twice a year, to 30 June and 31 December, paid three months later; reserve against returns at the publisher's discretion for the first three statements after each edition.

## Why It Matters

The earn-out question ([[lantern-hours-earn-out-check]]) is measured against this 12,000 GBP. The Italian deal ([[casa-velluto-rights-agreement-2026-03]]) runs under its translation clause.
