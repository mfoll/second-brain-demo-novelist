# Should I Take The Ashlar Translation Commission?

Should I accept Ashlar Editions' offer to translate an 88,000-word French novel for 9,500 GBP, delivery 2027-06-30, while writing the third draft of The Salt Choir for delivery on 2027-03-31? Answer promised to [[celine-marchetti]] by 2026-11-06.

## Why It Matters

> [!question]
> The commission closes the November to April cash gap on its own, and it lands on exactly the months the novel needs most. Saying yes risks the 2027-03-31 delivery, which is the date the next 8,000 GBP depends on. Saying no leaves me relying on a grant decision I will not have until 11 December, five weeks after Celine needs an answer.

## The Offer

From [[ashlar-commission-offer-2026-09-30]] and [[ashlar-sample-chapter]]:

- French literary novel, about 88,000 words, contemporary, a family and a harbour town in decline. Good, and well within my range.
- Fee 9,500 GBP (about 108 GBP per 1,000 words, in line with the middle of [[translators-rates-survey-2025]]). Half on signature, half on delivery and acceptance. Royalty of 1% after earn-out, which in practice means none.
- Delivery 2027-06-30, fixed because of Ashlar's translation grant, which requires a contract by January.

## What Each Person Says

- **[[callum-pryce]]** against ([[callum-email-on-ashlar-2026-10-03]]): the third draft is the book's last chance to be the book, the delivery date has already moved once, and Larkspur & Venn will not move it again without asking why. He also points out that the option on book three runs from acceptance, so a late Salt Choir delays everything after it.
- **[[neil-gathercole]]** for, on cash grounds ([[cash-review-with-neil-2026-10-14]], [[neil-gathercole-cash-note-2026-10-14]]): with half the fee on signature the account stays positive through April even if the Vellacott award fails. Without either, I overdraw from February.
- **[[aurelio-santangelo]]** offered to co-translate, splitting the book and the fee by halves ([[aurelio-co-translation-proposal-2026-10-12]]). Depends on Ashlar accepting two translators ([[is-co-translation-credit-acceptable-to-ashlar]]).
- **[[wendy-farrant]]**, asked informally, said that she took a ghostwriting job during her second novel and does not regret the money but does regret the book.

## Time

My translating pace when I stopped was about 1,200 finished words a day over a full working week, so roughly 75 working days for 88,000 words including revision. Alone, that is three and a half months. Halved with Aurelio, about seven weeks. The third draft needs every weekday from now to 29 January and most of February and March for Imogen's notes.

## Options

Compared in [[ashlar-commission-options]]:

1. Decline.
2. Accept alone, start in April after delivery. 75 working days from 1 April does not fit 30 June. Only possible if Celine moves the date, which the grant prevents.
3. Accept with Aurelio, my half starting in April: about seven weeks, April to mid-May. Fits, if Ashlar agrees and if delivery is not late.
4. Accept alone and start now. Rejected: the novel would lose.

## Current Leaning

Option 3, conditional on two things: Ashlar accepting a co-translation, and the third draft reaching Imogen on 29 January. If either fails, decline. The Vellacott decision would make declining easy, but it comes too late to rely on.
