# Public Lending Right

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-07-24, read on 2026-08-05 while rebuilding the cash-flow forecast after the delivery date moved.

## Summary

A public lending right is a scheme that pays authors for library lending, either as compensation for sales they may lose when books are borrowed rather than bought, or as public support for the arts through works held in libraries. Such schemes can cover books, music and artwork.

## Why It Matters

I wanted to know whether PLR could be treated as a steady line in the forecast. In my case it is: small, annual and predictable ([[plr-statement-2026]]), which is why it is the only February 2027 income in [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]]. The article's two justifications also explain why my older translations still earn a little: libraries keep translated books for years.

## Caveats

A general article about schemes in many countries. It says nothing about rates or registration where I am registered, which come from my own statements.
