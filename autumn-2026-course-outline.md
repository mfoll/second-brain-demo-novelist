# Autumn 2026 Course Outline

"Revision", the outline for the autumn 2026 term, sent to [[gwen-attah]] on 30 July 2026, with an enrolment requirement.

## Summary

Ten sessions on revising an existing draft: reading as a stranger; the one-sentence test; cutting by ten per cent; the [[reverse-outline]]; point-of-view drift; openings revisited; scene order and time; sentences; feedback a writer can use; reading and revision plans. Students must bring a draft of at least 3,000 words.

## Why It Matters

The first term built around existing work rather than new writing, and the first with an entry requirement. It filled with 14, nine of them new ([[evening-class-autumn-2026]]).
