# How long should the third draft be?

## Question

Draft 1 was about 112,000 words and draft 2 about 118,000. The contract describes a novel of 100,000 to 110,000 words. What should the third draft aim for?

## Current Answer

105,000 words, give or take 3,000. Agreed with Imogen at the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|July review]].

## Why

- The braid's extra length was orientation, not story; chronology removes it.
- Two war scenes instead of fragments saves about 7,500 words.
- Larkspur & Venn's hardback format makes 105,000 words about 400 pages, which sales prefer to 450.

## Progress

66,900 words at 2026-10-21, prologue and chapters 1 to 19. Chapters 20 to 25 are planned at about 18,000 words, so part three has to come in at about 20,000 to land near 105,000 ([[salt-choir-word-count-log]]). Part three is the obvious place to be short: it is aftermath.
