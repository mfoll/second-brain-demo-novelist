# Oral history

## Summary

Oral history is the collection and study of historical information from people about events and everyday life, through planned interviews recorded on tape or video or transcribed. Interviewees took part in or witnessed what they describe, and the aim is to keep their memories and perceptions as a record.

## Why It Matters

Read a week before the [[oral-history-workshop-2025-03-29|Merrin workshop]] so that I would not arrive knowing nothing. It made two things clear: oral history is a discipline with ethics and methods of its own, and the people who practise it are wary of writers. Both shaped [[run-an-oral-history-interview]] and the [[dorothy-pengelly-consent-form]].

## Key Details

- Interviews preserve perspectives that written sources usually lack.
- The record is of memory and perception, which is a different kind of evidence from documents.
