# Marcus Ellery

Crime novelist, eight books in a series, and a member of the monthly writing group since before I joined.

## How we work together

He reads the one chapter a month the group sees and comments like a plotter: what the reader knows, when they learn it, and whether the chapter makes them turn the page. He is blunt and almost always right about pace, and not interested in sentences, which is a useful corrective.

## What he contributed

- At the [[writing-group-2026-04-08|April 2026 group]] he read the braided storm opening and liked it; his note ([[marcus-ellery-notes-on-storm-chapter-2026-04-08]]) said that once the reader knows who dies, every later chapter must make them want to know how. That was the first clear statement of the problem that sank the braid, though he meant it as encouragement.
- He is on Callum's side about the prologue, for crime-writer reasons: never give away the body count ([[should-the-salt-choir-keep-the-storm-prologue]]).
