## Working Context

The readings are the part of the campaign I would keep. Booksellers hand-sell afterwards, and two shops reordered within a fortnight. The cost is the evening plus the next morning, which in the third-draft months is real. The set is in [[reading-script-lantern-hours]]: the opening of chapter 3, the dictionary scene, and the last page only if the room is warm. Questions always come back to translation; I now prepare for that rather than for questions about the plot.
