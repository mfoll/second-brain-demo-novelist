# Write The Salt Choir

My second novel, from the first notebook page to an accepted manuscript and, after that, copy-edits and proofs. It is a Responsibility rather than a Project because the book keeps asking things of me after delivery: the edit, the title, the jacket copy, questions from rights buyers. The finite pieces are Projects under [[salt-choir-novel]].

## Scope

- The book itself: structure, drafts, chapters, characters on the page, the prologue, the letters, revision and cuts.
- Conversations about the book with [[imogen-lacey]] (editor) and [[callum-pryce]] (agent), and with first readers: [[teodora-vlasic]], the writing group and [[wendy-farrant]].
- Period research for the book, run as [[saltmarrow-research]]; the town itself lives in [[saltmarrow-town-bible]].
- Not here: the contract and its money ([[salt-choir-contract]], [[delivery-date-extension]], under the finance and publishing areas), publicity for [[lantern-hours-paperback]], or my teaching, even when a class gave me an idea (it did: [[constance-leal]]).
