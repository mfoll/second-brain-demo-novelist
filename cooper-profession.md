# Cooper (profession)

## Summary

A cooper makes casks, barrels, vats, buckets, tubs and troughs from timber staves, usually heated or steamed to make them pliable. The article is the general account of the trade.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after the [[merrin-first-visit-2025-06-10|first Merrin visit]], where the barrel book showed how many barrels a yard used in a week. Jem Garrick, Nell's younger brother, is a cooper's apprentice in the [[character-bible]], and the cooperage stands at the yard ends on the Strand in the [[saltmarrow-town-bible]]. The tools and the noise of it come from here; the scale comes from the ledger.

## Key Details

- Staves are heated or steamed so they can be bent into shape.
- Coopers made far more than barrels: vats, buckets, tubs and troughs too.

## Caveats

General trade history, not specific to herring barrels or any town.
