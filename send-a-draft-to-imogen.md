# Send A Draft To Imogen

How I send a full or partial draft to my editor, [[imogen-lacey|Imogen Lacey]], and my agent, [[callum-pryce|Callum Pryce]]. Written after draft 1 went out with the wrong chapter numbers and draft 2 went out before I had read it through.

## Inputs

- The Scrivener project ([[salt-choir-third-draft-scrivener-project]]) with every chapter's status set to "Done".
- A finished [[full-draft-read-through]], with its fix list empty or deliberately deferred.
- The current [[third-draft-chapter-map]].

## Steps

1. **Freeze.** Duplicate the Scrivener project as `salt-choir-draftN-sent-YYYY-MM-DD` and do not touch it again. Further work happens in the live project.
2. **Compile** to .docx with the "Manuscript (Times)" format: 12 point, double spaced, chapter breaks on new pages, page numbers, title and my name in the header. Check the Cuts and Research folders are excluded from compile ([[scrivener-software]]).
3. **Check the compiled file**: chapter numbers run in order; the prologue is unnumbered; Constance's letters keep their date lines; no comments or inline notes left; word count matches Scrivener within 1%.
4. **Write the cover note**, one page: what this draft is, what changed since the last, what I know is still wrong, and the two or three questions I most want answered. Never apologise for the draft in it.
5. **Send** to Imogen and Callum in one email, file attached and also shared from the cloud folder. Subject: "The Salt Choir: draft N, DD Month YYYY".
6. **Log** the date and word count in [[salt-choir-word-count-log]], and add a dated line to the draft's Project.
7. **Stop.** No revisions to the sent text until notes come back. I may write new material, read, or research.

## Verification

Imogen confirms receipt and that the file opens. Callum confirms the same. If either has a different word count, recompile and resend before they read.

## Pitfalls

- Sending on a Friday evening invites me to fiddle all weekend. Send Monday to Thursday.
- Draft 2 went out on 2026-06-12 without step 7 respected; I sent a "corrected" chapter a week later, which made Imogen read it twice.
