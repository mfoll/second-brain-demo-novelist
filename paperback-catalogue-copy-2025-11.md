## Why It Matters

My first sight of how the catalogue works: copy is fixed months before publication, and the catalogue is where the publisher announces titles. That is why The Salt Choir's title should not appear anywhere before its own catalogue entry ([[catalogue-announcement]], [[what-i-say-in-public-about-book-two]]).
