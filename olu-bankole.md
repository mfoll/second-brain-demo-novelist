# Olu Bankole

Student in the autumn 2026 revision term, his first at Carrowmede. The youngest in the group by some years.

## In Class

Olu is revising a novel-length fantasy draft, his second complete draft, written mostly on his phone. He met the enrolment rule (a draft of at least 3,000 words) about thirty times over. He was quiet for the first three weeks and then, at the [[reverse-outline]] session, asked the question that made the evening: what you do when the outline shows that the best chapter is in the wrong book. We spent twenty minutes on it. The answer we reached was to keep a file of cut material and to stop treating it as loss.

He reads the older students' work with real attention and his comments are short and kind. He finds the read-backs slow and has said so on the mid-term card, fairly.

## How We Work Together

Through the centre's system and in class. He would like reading suggestions outside fantasy; I gave him a list of four novels with strong structure, two of them public domain, on 2026-10-13.
