# Is The Reading Tour Worth The Travel Cost?

## What I know

- Net cash cost so far 87.60 GBP for four readings after reimbursed rail ([[reading-tour-expenses-2026]]). Small.
- Time cost: an evening and half the next morning each. In the third-draft months that is about 1,500 words a reading.
- Event sales 9 to 24 copies; royalty on those about 0.75 GBP a copy. Direct return is negligible.
- Indirect return: reorders and hand-selling ([[bookshop-readings-are-for-booksellers]]).

## Current leaning

Worth it for the booksellers, not for the money. Finish the six, take no more for Lantern Hours.

## What would answer it

Martin's sell-through for host shops against non-host shops at the end of the year.
