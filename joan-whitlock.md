# Joan Whitlock

Freelance copy-editor and proofreader. Copy-edited the Lantern Hours hardback and proofread the paperback. Larkspur & Venn book her; I ask for her.

## How we work together

- Works on paper and returns a scanned marked-up set plus a typed query list.
- Her queries are questions, not instructions. She will ask whether a repeated word is deliberate rather than change it, which suits a translator who repeats words on purpose.
- She knows Portuguese well enough to catch accents; two of her 23 paperback corrections were Portuguese.
