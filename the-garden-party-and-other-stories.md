# The Garden Party (short story collection)

## Summary

The Garden Party and Other Stories is Katherine Mansfield's 1922 collection of short stories. The article records the collection and its contents; I read the stories themselves in my own copy. Mansfield died in 1923 and the collection is in the public domain.

## Why It Matters

Mansfield enters a character's mind in a sentence and leaves it in another, with no announcement. I read the collection a few stories at a time in autumn 2025, between drafting mornings, for [[free-indirect-style]] and for something closer to Constance: a woman's private voice rubbing against public occasion. The next reread is planned before the third-draft revision of Constance's letters.

## Key Details

- First published 1922.
- Short stories; the title story is the best known.
