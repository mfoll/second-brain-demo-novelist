# Saltmarrow Map Sketch, Version 1

The first map of Saltmarrow, drawn on 2025-01-15. Superseded on 2025-03-14 by [[saltmarrow-map-sketch]]; kept because the first draft's early chapters were written against it.

## Summary

A harbour town with the lifeboat house inside the harbour, the chapel on the front, and one curing yard. No bar, no beach.

## Why It Was Replaced

The harbour mouth faces north-east, into the storm, so a boat kept in the harbour could not have got out on the night. Moving the lifeboat house to a beach slipway changed the storm chapters ([[saltmarrow-map-redrawn-2025-03-14]]).
