# Merrin Photograph Catalogue Notes

Notes on about 120 photographs seen with [[ifeoma-clarke]] on 2025-06-10, and twenty more of lifeboat crews seen on 2026-05-12. Descriptions in my words; no reproductions.

## Summary

- Curing yards: crews of three at the troughs, barrels stacked four high, coopers at the yard ends, children carrying.
- Harbours: drifters rafted three deep; the ice house; the women waiting on the quay.
- Lifeboat crews posed by their boats: oilskins, sou'westers, lifejackets worn over the oilskins and tied with tapes.

## Why It Matters

Settled the layout of the yards in the [[saltmarrow-town-bible]] and the crew's dress in [[what-did-a-1920s-lifeboat-crew-wear]].

## Caveats

Posed photographs show Sunday versions of working life. The museum's reproduction terms apply; I have not licensed any image.
