# Character Bible

A fixed reference for the fourteen principal characters of The Salt Choir: birth years, households, work, where they live in Saltmarrow and what each lost in the war. Written between March and June 2025 so that the first draft stopped contradicting itself about ages.

## Outcome

A character bible for the 14 principal characters, with ages, households, work and what each lost in the war, finished on 2025-06-27. Done.

## Current State

- Completed 2025-06-27. The working document lives in the Scrivener research folder: [[character-bible-document]].
- Still the reference for ages and households in the third draft. Viewpoint characters have their own notes: [[nell-garrick]], [[tobias-arden]] and [[constance-leal]].
- Amended twice since closing: Constance's sister named Agnes (2026-01-27, when the letters started), and the lifeboat crew fixed at thirteen with the five lost named ([[lifeboat-crew-roll]], 2026-05-14).
- No new principal characters since; part three may need the district inspector to grow, which would be the fifteenth.

## Scope and Boundaries

Characters of the novel only. They are Notes in this vault, never Person notes, and none is based on a real person. Dorothy Pengelly's mother is not in the book, though the gutting crews owe her a great deal ([[how-much-of-dorothys-story-can-the-novel-use]]).

## Working Context

The fourteen, in the order of the bible:

| Name | Born | Work in 1924 | War |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nell Garrick | 1905 | Herring gutter, then telegraph clerk | Lost her father's boat partner, not family |
| Tobias Arden | 1893 | Fisherman, lifeboat coxswain from April 1924 | Infantry 1915 to 1918; shell shock |
| Constance Leal | 1881 | Vicar's widow, chapel choir leader, piano teacher | Husband Edmund died 1921, not in the war |
| Ma Garrick (Bridget) | 1879 | Head of a gutting crew | Brother lost at sea 1917 |
| Jem Garrick | 1908 | Cooper's apprentice | Too young |
| Walter Pascoe | 1866 | Postmaster, Nell's employer | Son killed 1916 |
| Ivor Pascoe | 1903 | Postmaster's nephew, messenger | Too young |
| Hugh Rendell | 1870 | Curing-yard owner, lifeboat committee chairman | None; profited |
| Sarah Rendell | 1896 | His daughter, choir soprano | Fiance killed 1918 |
| Daniel Toms | 1890 | Second coxswain | Navy, came back whole |
| Minister Abel Crewe | 1874 | Bethel chapel minister | Preached recruitment, regrets it |
| Kit Harker | 1899 | Bowman, Tobias's friend | Gassed 1918 |
| Agnes (Constance's sister) | 1884 | Lives inland | Never appears |
| Dr Lomax | 1868 | Town doctor | Treats Tobias, says "nerves" |

The household map is in [[saltmarrow-town-bible]] (who lives on which street) and the timeline in [[saltmarrow-chronology]].
