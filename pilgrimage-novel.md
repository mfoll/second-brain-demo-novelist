# Pilgrimage (novel sequence)

## Summary

Pilgrimage is a novel sequence by Dorothy Richardson from the first half of the twentieth century, thirteen volumes including a final posthumous one, now regarded as a significant work of literary modernism. Richardson called the volumes "chapters".

## Why It Matters

The long one I have not finished: stalled in volume two since December 2025. I keep it in the [[modernist-women-writers]] cluster because Richardson's single consciousness, held over thousands of pages, is the opposite of what I am doing with three viewpoints, and seeing the opposite clearly helps. Not public domain everywhere, so cited here as an article, not as a text.

## Key Details

- Thirteen volumes; the last posthumous.
- Richardson's own term: "chapters".

## Caveats

My reading is partial; I have no view yet on the sequence as a whole.
