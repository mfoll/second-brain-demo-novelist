# Did The Tidewrack Panel Sell Books?

## Current Answer

Some. 38 copies signed at the festival shop on the day, from an order of 40, against about 140 in the tent. The best single event of the paperback campaign for copies, and the only one that paid (250 GBP).

## Evidence

- Festival shop count, passed on by [[orla-fenwick|Orla]] on 2026-06-22.
- No visible bump in the weekly figures afterwards; Martin Okello says one festival rarely shows ([[lantern-hours-paperback-sales-report-2026-09]]).

## Why it matters

Orla has asked about 2027. A panel is worth one weekend if the book is new; for The Salt Choir it will be worth more than readings. For Lantern Hours in 2027, probably not.
