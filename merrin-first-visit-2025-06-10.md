# First Merrin Visit, 10 June 2025

My first reading-room day at the [[merrin-maritime-collection]], arranged by [[ifeoma-clarke]] for the photograph collection and the curing-yard ledgers. The lifeboat records were not on my list; I did not yet know I needed them.

## What I saw

- About 120 photographs of curing yards and harbours from 1905 to 1935, from several towns. Women at the gutting troughs in crews of three, barrels stacked four high, coopers at the yard ends, children everywhere.
- One firm's wage book for 1926: pay by the barrel for each crew, with engagement money at the start, deductions for lodgings, and the season's total per crew. I copied the structure, not the names.
- A barrel book showing the coopers' output by week, which gave me the scale of a yard.

## What changed

- The [[saltmarrow-town-bible]] got three named curing yards on the Strand, the cooperage at the yard ends and the ice house.
- Ma Garrick's crew in the [[character-bible]] got its pay structure.
- Logged two fixes from the first draft: electric light in the yards (paraffin) and the gutters' gloves (none). See [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]].

## Note to self

I was so taken with the photographs that I spent four hours on them and forty minutes on the ledgers. Next time: documents first, pictures after lunch. That turned into the order in [[check-a-chapter-for-anachronisms]], evidence before atmosphere.
