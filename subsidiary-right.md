# Subsidiary Right

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2023-09-16), read on 2025-09-02.

## Summary

A subsidiary right is the right to produce the work in another form: other formats, translations, editions in other territories, audio, book-club editions, film and more. The author grants these to an agent, publisher or studio, and the holder can exploit them directly or license them on.

## Why It Matters

Read two days before the [[rights-strategy-call-2025-09-04|rights strategy call]], to get the vocabulary straight.
