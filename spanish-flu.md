# Spanish flu

## Summary

The article covers the 1918 to 1920 influenza pandemic caused by an H1N1 influenza A virus, misleadingly called Spanish flu. Within two years an estimated 500 million people, nearly a third of the world's population, had been infected; estimates of deaths run from 17 million to 50 million and possibly higher.

## Why It Matters

The book opens in 1924, but every adult in Saltmarrow lived through the war and the influenza years after it. In the [[character-bible]] two households lost someone in the winter of 1918 to 1919, and the [[saltmarrow-chronology]] records influenza in the town that winter. The novel mentions it twice, in passing, as people would have: a grave, an empty place at a table.

## Key Details

- 1918 to 1920; H1N1 influenza A.
- About 500 million infected; deaths estimated at 17 to 50 million or more.

## Caveats

Global figures. How the pandemic touched an invented town is my invention, kept deliberately small.
