# Autumn 2025 Course Outline

The outline for my first Carrowmede term, "Openings and Scene", sent to [[gwen-attah]] on 21 July 2025 for the brochure.

## Summary

Ten sessions: three on openings (first lines, first pages, what an opening promises), three on scene as a unit of change, two on dialogue, one on revising the opening in the light of the scene work, and an end-of-term reading. Each session one handout and one exercise. Brochure blurb of 80 words, edited by Gwen.

## Why It Matters

The plan I actually followed, mostly. The one change was the publishing Q&A squeezed into session 9, which the outline did not have and the students asked for ([[evening-class-autumn-2025]]).
