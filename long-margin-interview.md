# Long Margin Interview

A long interview with [[jasper-meade|Jasper Meade]] for The Long Margin, issue 31, timed for the paperback. It went to print with two errors about book two and needed a correction.

## Outcome

Interview published in issue 31 on 2026-07-15, with the online correction of 2026-07-22. Completed.

## Current State

- Recorded 2026-06-03 ([[long-margin-interview-recording-2026-06-03]]), published 2026-07-15 ([[long-margin-interview-2026-07-15]]).
- The printed text called The Salt Choir "a shipwreck novel" and printed its working title before Larkspur & Venn had announced it.
- Correction published online on 2026-07-22 after [[priyanka-desai|Priyanka Desai]] and [[callum-pryce|Callum Pryce]] asked for it ([[long-margin-correction-call-2026-07-17]], [[long-margin-correction-notice-2026-07-22]]). The print issue cannot be corrected.
- Rule adopted: [[what-i-say-in-public-about-book-two]].

## Scope and Boundaries

This interview only. General interview practice is in [[email-interviews-over-recorded-ones]] and [[on-the-record]].

## Working Context

Jasper's questions ([[long-margin-interview-questions-2026-05]]) were mostly about translation and Lantern Hours. Book two came up in the last ten minutes of a 90-minute conversation when I was tired and generous. I said the novel turns on a lifeboat lost in a storm and the town's choir. "Shipwreck" was his word, from his notes, not mine; the title I did say, after asking whether we were still recording, and he read the answer as yes. I did not ask for copy approval, which The Long Margin does not give, and did not ask to check quotes, which it does allow. That was my mistake.
