# Saltmarrow Anachronism Log

A running log of period errors found in The Salt Choir and how each was fixed. One line per error: date found, chapter (in the draft where it was found), what was wrong, how I know, and the fix. Checked with [[check-a-chapter-for-anachronisms]] after each chapter is rewritten. The project closes the log before delivery ([[saltmarrow-research]]).

> [!warning]
> Status on 2026-10-21: 41 entries since January 2025, 34 fixed, 7 open. All open entries are in parts two and three of the third draft, which are not yet rewritten.

## Fixed, 2025 (condensed)

Twenty-eight entries from the first draft, mostly language and objects: "teenager" (twice), "shell-shocked" in a local's mouth (now "nerves"), electric light in the curing yards (paraffin), a gutter's rubber gloves (bare hands and cotton strips, from Dorothy Pengelly), a bus to the next town in 1924 (carrier's cart), a 1930s hymn tune in 1925, a crew member calling the coxswain "skipper", and a maroon fired from the harbour wall rather than the lifeboat house. Full lines in the Scrivener research folder ([[salt-choir-research-dossier]]).

## Kinds of error

Objects are easy to catch; attitudes are not. The hardest entries are of the kind in [[anachronism-of-attitude]]: characters who think about mental health, or about women's work, in a vocabulary from my lifetime.
