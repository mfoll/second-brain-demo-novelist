# Femi Adewale

Medical historian who works on the treatment of war neuroses after the First World War. [[wendy-farrant]] introduced us in early 2026. He read the Tobias chapters of draft two as a favour, on the understanding that I would not quote him as an authority.

## Role

Expert reader for [[tobias-arden]]'s shell shock: symptoms, what a man in a small town in the 1920s would have been told about them, what treatment he could have had, and what words he and the people around him would have used.

## What he contributed

- Written notes on 2026-03-24 ([[femi-adewale-notes-on-tobias-2026-03-24]]) and a call on 2026-03-26 ([[femi-adewale-reading-call-2026-03-26]]). Main points: Tobias's symptoms were plausible but too continuous, a man running a lifeboat crew would have had good weeks; "PTSD" thinking had leaked into his interior voice; local people would more likely say "nerves" than "shell shock".
- A short reading list ([[femi-adewale-reading-list-2026-03]]).
- His point that the war should come back in a few whole scenes rather than constant flashes fed directly into [[how-much-of-tobiass-war-goes-on-the-page]].

## Boundaries

He is not paid and not credited as a consultant. An acknowledgement only, with his agreement, and he sees the Tobias chapters of the third draft before delivery.
