# What Did A 1920s Lifeboat Crew Wear?

Draft 3 chapter 5, the first service launch, had the crew "buckle on lifejackets". What would a pulling and sailing lifeboat crew have worn on a winter launch in the 1920s?

## Current Answer

Oilskins and sou'westers over their own wool, sea boots, and the station's lifejackets, which were bulky, worn over the oilskins and tied with tapes rather than buckled. Fixed on 2026-08-13 in the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]].

## Evidence

- [[souwester]] for the hat, which I had right.
- Photographs at the Merrin of crews posed by their boats, seen on the [[merrin-first-visit-2025-06-10|2025 visit]] and again in 2026: lifejackets over oilskins, tapes visible.
- [[ronan-maddox]]: men went out in what they were standing in if the maroon went at night, and dressed in the boathouse.

## Caveat

I have not settled what the lifejackets were filled with in 1927 and do not need to: the text says "the jackets" and describes their bulk, not their material.
