# Sou'wester

## Summary

A sou'wester is a traditional collapsible oilskin rain hat, longer at the back than the front to protect the neck; some have a gutter front brim.

## Why It Matters

Checked at Ferrow House on 2026-08-13 during the anachronism pass on chapter 5, the first service launch. The hat was right; the buckled lifejackets were not. See [[what-did-a-1920s-lifeboat-crew-wear]].

## Key Details

- Oilskin, collapsible, long at the back.

## Caveats

Says nothing about dates of use; the Merrin photographs confirm sou'westers on lifeboat crews of the period.
