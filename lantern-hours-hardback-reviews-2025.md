# Lantern Hours Hardback Reviews 2025

My log of reviews and notices of the hardback, closed on 2025-04-30 with the launch project. Clippings are in the publicity folder.

## Key Details

- Four print reviews: two national-paper short notices (about 150 words each), one literary weekly (900 words), one regional paper.
- One long blog review by [[maya-okonjo|Maya Okonjo]] ([[maya-okonjo-blog-review-2025-03]]), shared more widely than all four print reviews together.
- Common praise: the ear, the dictionary scene, the precision about translation work.
- Common complaint: the middle third is slow. Three of five said so. They are right; chapters 8 to 11 tread water.

## Why It Matters

The slow-middle note is the one I carried into The Salt Choir. It is part of why Imogen's 2026 letter about the storm arriving too late landed as hard as it did ([[editorial-call-2026-02-19]]).
