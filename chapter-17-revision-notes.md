# Chapter 17 Revision Notes

Working notes for chapter 17 of the third draft: Tobias, August 1927, the second of his two war scenes ([[third-draft-chapter-map]]).

## What the chapter has to do

- One remembered scene, complete, triggered by something in the dated present: the lifeboat exercise on the bar in August, the gun fired for the launch.
- No fragments. Draft 2 lost readers by scattering the war; this is the reason for two whole scenes only ([[how-much-of-tobiass-war-goes-on-the-page]]).
- End back in 1927 with the crew, not in 1917.

## Checks before I call it done

- The pension question: did a man with his record still have to appear before a board in the early 1920s, and what would he have been asked? Notes from my reading are in [[pension-board-hearing-1920]].
- [[femi-adewale]]'s correction still stands: good weeks and bad weeks, not a constant state.
- Length about 3,000 words; it is at 3,600.
