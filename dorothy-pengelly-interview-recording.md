# Dorothy Pengelly Interview Recording

Governed pointer to the audio recording of the interview with [[dorothy-pengelly]] on 2025-04-23, about an hour and fifty minutes. The recording is not transcribed in full and not stored in the vault.

## Summary

A timed summary log (topics by quarter hour, no direct quotation) sits with the recording. What I took from it, in my own words, is in [[dorothy-pengelly-interview-2025-04-23]].

## Why It Matters

It is the only primary memory source in the research. Its use is governed by [[dorothy-pengelly-consent-form]].

## Caveats

- Two passages were recorded before she asked me to stop; they are marked in the log as not for use.
- A copy went to Dorothy in May 2025 and, at her choice, to the Merrin's oral history collection via [[ifeoma-clarke]] in August 2025. The museum's copy is under its own access terms, not mine.

## Source Location

Encrypted external drive, folder "Oral history", with a second encrypted copy in the backup. Access: me only.
