# Hardback Launch 2025

Launch of Lantern Hours in hardback (Larkspur & Venn, 2025-02-27, RRP 16.99 GBP), with the first round of readings and reviews.

## Outcome

Lantern Hours launched in hardback on 2025-02-27 at Quire & Candle, with the first round of readings and reviews. Completed 2025-04-30.

## Current State

- Launch at Quire & Candle on 2025-02-27: about 70 people, 61 copies sold ([[lantern-hours-hardback-launch-2025-02-27]], [[quire-and-candle-event-sheet-2025-02]]).
- Five readings between March and April, two library talks, one regional radio interview.
- Reviews: four in print, one long blog review by [[maya-okonjo|Maya Okonjo]] ([[maya-okonjo-blog-review-2025-03]]); log in [[lantern-hours-hardback-reviews-2025]].

## Scope and Boundaries

The hardback launch window only (January to April 2025). Everything from 2026 is under [[lantern-hours-paperback]].

## Working Context

[[hardback-launch-plan-2025|Priyanka's plan]] was modest and right for a debut from a small house: one launch, a handful of readings, review copies to forty names. What I learned and kept: launch in a shop that will hand-sell for months, not the biggest room; never read for more than twelve minutes; bring a pen that works on coated endpapers. The reviews praised the translator's ear and found the middle slow, which is fair.
