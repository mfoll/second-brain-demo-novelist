# First Hour Rule

The first working hour of the day goes to the draft, before email, news, the class or the accounts. Not the best hour, the first one, because the first is the only one I can protect every day.

## Why the first

As a translator I worked to page quotas and could start anywhere. A novel has no quota from outside; whatever comes first eats the morning. The rule came from notebook eleven, early 2025, when the hardback publicity took three weeks of mornings and the first draft stalled at chapter 3.

## Practice

- 07:00 to 08:00 at the desk, phone in another room. If the hour goes well, it runs on to 11:00 ([[writing-routine]]).
- Counted in the [[daily-word-count-log]] as a yes or no, not in words.
- The hour can be revision, but not research and not reading.

## Record

In 2026 to date, the hour was kept on 181 of the 204 working days logged. The misses cluster around readings and the week of the Long Margin correction in July.
