# First Class Session 2025-09-16

My first evening teaching at Carrowmede. Twelve students, room 4, 19:00 to 21:00. Subject: first lines.

## What Happened

- [[gwen-attah]] introduced me and left. I talked for twenty-five minutes, which was fifteen too many.
- The [[openings-handout-2025]] had twelve first lines, half from public-domain novels and half invented by me without saying which. The class was better at spotting the invented ones than I liked.
- Exercise: write three first lines for the same story, one that begins with a person, one with a place, one with a problem. Everyone wrote; nine read.
- [[ronan-maddox]] began with a tide table. [[maureen-tighe]] knew the source of every public-domain line.

## What I Noted Afterwards

- Prepare half as much and leave room for the students.
- Two hours with a break at 20:00 is right; Kiran's group breaks at the same time and the corridor is the staffroom.
- I was more nervous than at the Lantern Hours launch. Teaching is not reading aloud; the room talks back.
