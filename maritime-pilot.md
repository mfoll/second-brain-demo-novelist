# Maritime pilot

## Summary

A maritime pilot is a mariner with specific knowledge of a dangerous or congested waterway, such as a harbour or river mouth: depths, currents and hazards. Pilots board and temporarily join a ship's crew to guide its passage, must handle ships of all types and sizes, and are licensed by a pilotage authority.

## Why It Matters

Looked up after the spring class reading of 31 March 2026, when [[ronan-maddox]], a retired harbour pilot, asked who had started the lifeboat's engine. Knowing what a pilot's knowledge is (local, exact, about water and boats) is why I took his question seriously before I had any evidence. Saltmarrow has no pilot station; boats come in over the bar on local knowledge, which the storm chapters use.

## Key Details

- Local knowledge of depth, current and hazard.
- Licensed; temporarily part of the ship's crew.

## Caveats

General and modern in emphasis; nothing about 1920s practice.
