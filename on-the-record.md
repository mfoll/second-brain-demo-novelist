# On The Record

The working convention that anything said to a journalist or interviewer may be printed and attributed, unless both sides have agreed otherwise before it is said.

## The parts I had wrong

- "Off the record" has to be agreed in advance and acknowledged. Saying it afterwards, or asking "are we still recording?" and not hearing the answer, does not count.
- An interviewer's paraphrase is not a quote, but readers cannot tell the difference. "A shipwreck novel" was Jasper's gloss; it read as mine ([[long-margin-interview-2026-07-15]]).
- Quote checking is a courtesy some magazines offer on request. It is not copy approval. The Long Margin offers it; I did not ask.

## What I do now

- Assume the recorder is on until it is visibly off.
- Ask at the start whether I may check quotes, and do.
- Prefer email interviews ([[email-interviews-over-recorded-ones]]).
- Use the one-sentence description of book two ([[what-i-say-in-public-about-book-two]]).
