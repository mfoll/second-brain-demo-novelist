# Publishing Economics

How money actually moves through a book: advances paid in thirds, royalties against the advance, reserves against returns, agency commission, the publisher's share of subsidiary rights, and what a reading or a festival fee is worth set against a day lost from the desk. A translator for twenty-two years, I knew the flat-fee side of this well and the royalty side hardly at all.
