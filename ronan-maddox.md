# Ronan Maddox

Student in the Tuesday class since autumn 2025, three terms running. Retired harbour pilot.

## In Class

Ronan is writing a sequence of linked stories about a working harbour across about forty years, told by the people who pass through the pilot station. Broad terms only, under [[student-writing-privacy-rules]]. He writes slowly and revises a great deal, and the revision term suits him better than either of the earlier ones.

His contribution to the room is precision about how things work. He will ask what a character is physically doing with their hands, which rope, which tide, and the answer is usually that the writer does not know. Younger students find this alarming for about two weeks and then start asking the same question of each other.

## The Lifeboat

At the [[spring-class-final-reading-2026-03-31|end-of-term reading on 31 March 2026]] I read one page of the new storm opening of The Salt Choir. Ronan asked afterwards, quite gently, who had started the engine. In draft 2 the Saltmarrow lifeboat had a motor. He said that a small station in the late 1920s would more likely have had a pulling and sailing boat, and that the crews of the bigger motor boats were still being trained in that decade.

I wrote it down and did not act on it until the archive visit in May confirmed it. The settled answer, a ten-oar pulling and sailing lifeboat, is in [[would-saltmarrows-lifeboat-have-had-an-engine-in-1927]] and the [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]]. Ronan is credited in my notes; he has asked not to be named in the acknowledgements, only "a harbour pilot", and I will respect that.

## How We Work Together

He prefers comments on paper. He does not use email much and the centre's system handles contact anyway. He has offered to read the storm chapters of the third draft for seamanship only, and I have accepted, once part two is stable.
