# Driftline Books Reading 2026-09-24

Fourth reading of the paperback tour, at Driftline Books. Thursday, 18:30.

- 27 people, 19 copies sold. [[malcolm-strachan|Malcolm]] reordered twelve copies the following week.
- An audience of translated-fiction readers. Questions were about translators' names on covers and about the Italian edition.
- Two people asked about "the shipwreck book". I said the next novel is set in a 1920s coastal town and left it there, as agreed after [[long-margin-interview-2026-07-15|the misquote]].
- Cost of the evening: rail 23.60 GBP (reimbursed), a taxi home 14.00 GBP (not), and the next morning at half speed. Logged in [[reading-tour-expenses-2026]].
