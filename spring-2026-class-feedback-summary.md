# Spring 2026 Class Feedback Summary

My summary of the centre's anonymous end-of-term feedback forms for the spring 2026 term, 12 of 14 returned. The forms themselves go back to [[gwen-attah]]; only this summary stays with me.

## Summary

- Overall: 11 of 12 would recommend the class.
- Most useful sessions, by mention: epistolary voice (7), close third and free indirect style (6), one scene in three viewpoints (5).
- Least useful: second person (6 mentions). Replace with frame narration.
- Pace: three forms said the reading-back took too long; two said the reading evening was too long.
- Requests: more time on dialogue (now the spring 2027 subject), fewer handouts with longer examples.

## Why It Matters

The evidence behind the changes in the autumn 2026 term and the plan for spring 2027 ([[teach-creative-writing]]). No comments are quoted and none are attributed.
