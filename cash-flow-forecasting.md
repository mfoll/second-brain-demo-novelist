# Cash Flow Forecasting

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-09-19, read on 2026-10-15, the day after the cash review with Neil.

## Summary

Cash-flow forecasting estimates a business's future cash position from expected payments and receipts. It is presented as a basic financial management tool for small businesses as much as large ones, with several possible methods.

## Why It Matters

My spreadsheet is the simplest kind: receipts and payments, month by month. Reading this confirmed that the method is fine and the weakness is the inputs, especially the dates of receipts. That is the whole of [[advances-arrive-late]], and why [[update-the-cash-flow-forecast]] moves every tranche a month late and keeps grants and commissions on scenario tabs.

## Caveats

Written for companies. Nothing on income as lumpy as advances and royalties.
