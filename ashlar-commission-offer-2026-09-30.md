# Ashlar Commission Offer 2026-09-30

[[celine-marchetti]] of Ashlar Editions emailed on Wednesday 30 September 2026, the day The Salt Choir would originally have been delivered, with an offer to translate a French novel.

## The Offer

- About 88,000 words, contemporary literary fiction.
- Fee 9,500 GBP, half on signature, half on delivery and acceptance.
- Delivery 2027-06-30.
- Ashlar's translation grant application requires the book to be under contract by January.
- Sample chapter and synopsis attached ([[ashlar-sample-chapter]]).

She asked for an answer within a month. On 2026-10-07 we spoke and agreed 2026-11-06.

## First Reaction

Flattered, then a spreadsheet. The fee would close the gap in [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]] by itself. The date overlaps the third draft and the 2027-03-31 delivery. I forwarded the email to [[callum-pryce]] the same evening, who replied on 3 October ([[callum-email-on-ashlar-2026-10-03]]).
