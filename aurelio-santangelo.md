# Aurelio Santangelo

Literary translator from French and Italian into English, a former colleague from my translating years and the person who runs a small translators' collective: eleven freelance translators who share contracts advice, a rates survey and sometimes work.

## How We Know Each Other

We co-translated a long French family saga in 2016 and 2017, splitting it by part and revising each other's halves. It was the most pleasant job of my translating life and the reason I trust him with the Ashlar question. When I stopped translating in 2022 he was the one who said, without fuss, that I could always come back.

## How We Work Together

- Phone calls, roughly monthly, about forty minutes, half work and half books.
- He sends me the collective's annual rates survey ([[translators-rates-survey-2025]]), though I am no longer a member.
- He reads my French when I doubt it, and I read his English when he doubts it.

## Current Threads

- **The Ashlar commission.** On 2026-10-12 he offered to co-translate the 88,000-word French novel [[celine-marchetti]] offered me, splitting it by halves as we did in 2016, with the fee split equally ([[aurelio-co-translation-proposal-2026-10-12]]). That would halve my share of the work and the fee. It depends on Ashlar accepting two translators ([[is-co-translation-credit-acceptable-to-ashlar]]). Decision in [[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]], due 2026-11-06.
- He thinks I should say yes in some form. He also thinks I should not take it alone, and he is probably right.
- He wants to read the Italian Lantern Hours when Casa Velluto publishes it, to report back on what [[giulia-rinaldi]]'s translator does with the mentor's voice.
