# Freelancer

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-09-05, read on 2026-10-13, the day after Aurelio's co-translation proposal.

## Summary

A freelancer is a self-employed worker not committed to one employer over the long term. Some work through a company or agency that resells their labour; others work independently or find work through professional associations and websites.

## Why It Matters

Twenty-two years of translation were freelance in exactly this sense, and the Ashlar offer is the old life asking to come back. The line about professional associations describes Aurelio's collective ([[translators-rates-survey-2025]]). It also clarified one thing for [[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]]: as a novelist I am represented by an agency; as a translator I would be on my own, with no agent and no commission taken ([[halloran-pryce-agency-agreement]]).
