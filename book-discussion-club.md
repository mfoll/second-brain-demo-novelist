# Book Discussion Club

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2026-07-15), read on 2026-02-03.

## Summary

Defines the book discussion club as a group that meets to talk about books its members have read, also called a reading group or book group, and notes the range of places they meet, from homes and libraries to bookshops, cafés and online forums. Covers their history and the role of publishers' discussion guides.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after starting the [[reading-group-guide]]. Two things shaped the guide: groups meet to talk, not to be taught, so the questions should start arguments rather than test comprehension; and many groups borrow sets from libraries, which is why the further reading is limited to books a library could supply ([[reading-group-guide-final-2026]]). [[rosa-mbatha|Rosa Mbatha]]'s groups bore both out.

## Caveats

General and descriptive. Nothing on what makes a good discussion question; that came from testing the draft on Rosa's group.
