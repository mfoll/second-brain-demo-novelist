# Advance Tranches

An advance against royalties is rarely paid in one sum. It is split into tranches, each triggered by an event in the book's life, and the headline figure says almost nothing about when money arrives.

## How My Two Contracts Split

| Book | Advance | Tranche 1 | Tranche 2 | Tranche 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lantern Hours | 12,000 GBP | Signature (2023-06-14) | Delivery and acceptance | Hardback publication (2025-02-27) |
| The Salt Choir | 24,000 GBP | Signature (2025-03-18) | Delivery and acceptance (now 2027-03-31 plus acceptance) | Hardback publication (spring 2028, expected) |

Each tranche is a third. Each reaches me less the agency's 15% ([[halloran-pryce-agency-agreement]], evidence in [[halloran-pryce-remittance-advices-2025]]).

## What Moves A Tranche

- **Signature**: the countersigned contract, then the publisher's payment run, then the agency's remittance. In practice four to six weeks.
- **Delivery and acceptance**: two triggers, not one. Delivery is mine; acceptance is the editor's, and can take weeks or a revision round. This is the tranche that moved with the [[delivery-date-extension]].
- **Publication**: fixed by the publisher's schedule, which moves when delivery moves, but not one-for-one.

## Why It Matters For Planning

A 24,000 GBP advance is three payments of 6,800 GBP net spread over about three years. Over the life of The Salt Choir that is less per year than the teaching and royalties combined in a good year. The cash-flow forecast plans by tranche and by trigger, never by headline ([[cash-flow-forecast-2026]]).
