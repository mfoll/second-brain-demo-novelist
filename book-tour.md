# Book Tour

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2025-03-07), read on 2026-05-15, the day after the paperback came out and a week before the first reading.

## Summary

Describes the book tour as a promotion in which an author travels to meet readers, booksellers and media around a new book. It names three aims for each appearance: entertain the audience, serve the host's interest and sell copies in person, and notes that authors disagree about whether tours are worth it.

## Why It Matters

The three aims are a fair checklist, and the second one, serving the host, is the one I had undervalued. It became the core of [[bookshop-readings-are-for-booksellers]]. The disagreement among authors is exactly my open question, [[is-the-reading-tour-worth-the-travel-cost]].

## Caveats

The article has big-publisher tours in mind: media, many cities, publisher-paid travel. Six readings in independent shops, reachable by train, is a different scale.
