# Maureen Tighe Letter Exercise

A half-page exercise card, written up on 3 February 2026 from what [[maureen-tighe]] described in class on 27 January, and checked with her. Credited to her on the card, with her agreement.

## The Exercise

1. Choose a character and the one person they do not perform for.
2. Write the character a letter to that person, about something small that happened this week.
3. Do not explain the relationship. The reader should work it out from the letter alone.
4. Optional: the addressee does not write back. Write the second letter, after the silence.

Twenty to twenty-five minutes for the first letter.

## Why It Matters

The exercise behind Constance Leal's letters ([[class-session-2026-01-27]], [[constance-leal]]). It is now a standing exercise in my handouts and will be used in the spring 2027 dialogue term as a contrast to dialogue.
