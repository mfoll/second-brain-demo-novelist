# Telegram

## Summary

A telegram is a written or printed message sent by telegraphy. The article says telegrams were popular for social and business correspondence from the second half of the nineteenth century to the first half of the twentieth, stayed in use into the telephone age, and produced a clipped style of writing of their own.

## Why It Matters

Nell's work at the counter is telegrams: prices for the curers, weather for the harbour master, deaths and births for the town. The telegram style, everything paid by the word, is a gift for a novelist: a whole life in eleven words. Nell's chapters use it sparingly, and the storm night (third draft chapter 22) is told partly through the messages she takes.

## Key Details

- Social and business use, roughly 1850 to 1950.
- A recognisable compressed style.
