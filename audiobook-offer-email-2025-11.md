# Audiobook Offer Email 2025-11

[[callum-pryce|Callum]]'s email of 2025-11-03 forwarding [[hana-kowal|Hana Kowal]]'s offer for the Lantern Hours audiobook, with his comments.

## Key Details

- Advance 2,000 GBP against royalties of 25% of net receipts.
- Licence seven years, English language, unabridged only.
- Narrator chosen by the producer in consultation with the author; author may read if her sample is good enough.
- Recording proposed for autumn 2026. (It slipped; see [[audiobook-pause-2026-04-14]].)

## Callum's comments

Fair terms for a small literary producer; the advance is modest because audio sales for literary debuts are modest. He would not push for more, but would ask for a five-year term. Not yet asked, since the project paused.
