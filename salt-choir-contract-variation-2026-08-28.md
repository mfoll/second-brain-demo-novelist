# Salt Choir Contract Variation 2026-08-28

Letter of variation to the publishing agreement for The Salt Choir of 2025-03-18 ([[salt-choir-contract]]), signed by me and countersigned by Larkspur & Venn on 28 August 2026. Drafted by [[bethan-okafor]] at the Halloran Pryce Agency.

## Key Details

- Clause amended: delivery date, from 2026-09-30 to 2027-03-31.
- Second tranche of the advance (8,000 GBP, one third of 24,000 GBP) remains payable on delivery and acceptance, and therefore follows the new date.
- Acceptance period unchanged: the publisher has 60 days from delivery to accept or request revisions.
- All other terms unchanged and confirmed: advance, royalty rates, rights, the option on the next novel, which runs from acceptance.
- Publication date not specified in the variation; Larkspur & Venn have said spring 2028.

## Why It Matters

The legal end of the [[delivery-date-extension]] and the cause of the cash gap in [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]]. The 60-day acceptance period means the tranche could legitimately arrive as late as June 2027, which is why Neil wanted the June case modelled.
