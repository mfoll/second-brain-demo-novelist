# Oral History Workshop At The Merrin

A Saturday workshop for volunteers and local historians run by [[ifeoma-clarke]] at the Merrin Maritime Collection, 10:00 to 15:30, 12 GBP including lunch. Eleven participants; I was the only novelist and said so at the start.

## What it covered

- Preparing: a short letter to the interviewee in advance, a list of themes rather than questions, and a consent form read aloud before the recorder goes on.
- During: open questions, silence as a tool, asking for the concrete ("what did the yard smell like in the morning?") rather than the general.
- After: a timed summary log within 48 hours, a thank-you, and offering the interviewee a copy.
- Rights and ethics: who owns the recording, what deposit with a museum means, and what "use" covers. Ifeoma was frank that writers are the users museums worry about most.

## What came of it

- My interview method, written up as [[run-an-oral-history-interview]].
- The consent form I adapted from the museum's template ([[dorothy-pengelly-consent-form]]).
- On the way out Ifeoma mentioned [[dorothy-pengelly]], interviewed by the museum in 2009, who might talk to me. Introduction by letter followed in early April; the interview took place on 2025-04-23.
- The idea of [[shared-authority]], which Ifeoma credited to the oral history literature and which made me uneasy in a useful way.
