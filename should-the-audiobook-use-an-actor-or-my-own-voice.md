# Should The Audiobook Use An Actor Or My Own Voice?

Deferred with the project on 2026-04-14 ([[audiobook-pause-2026-04-14]]). Revisit when Hana Kowal confirms the 2027 list.

## Considerations

- For me: the narrator is a translator speaking in the first person; I know that voice. Readings go well.
- Against: reading 82,000 words aloud takes four studio days plus retakes, and an eleven-minute reading is not an eight-hour one. [[hana-kowal|Hana]] says most authors who try are tired by chapter 5.
- The Portuguese and French fragments need correct pronunciation; I can do that, and an actor would need coaching.
- Hana's rule: she decides after hearing a 10-minute sample.

## Current leaning

Record the sample and let Hana decide. Do not record anything before the third draft is with Imogen on 2027-01-29.
