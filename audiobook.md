# Audiobook

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2026-08-28), read on 2025-11-05.

## Summary

An audiobook is a recording of a book read aloud; a reading of the full text is unabridged, a shortened one an abridgement. The article covers the format's history from recorded books for blind readers through cassettes and CDs to downloads and subscriptions, and the practice of authors narrating their own books.
