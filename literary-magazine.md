# Literary Magazine

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2026-10-23), read on 2026-05-05.

## Summary

A literary magazine or journal is a periodical devoted to literature: fiction, poetry and sometimes serialised novels, alongside essays, criticism, reviews and interviews or profiles. The article surveys their history and the small-press economics many of them run on.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after accepting The Long Margin's request ([[long-margin-interview]]), to calibrate what kind of piece to expect. A quarterly with a small staff, writing up from recordings and notes, is exactly the setting in which a gloss turns into a quote.
