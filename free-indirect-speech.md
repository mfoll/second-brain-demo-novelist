# Free indirect speech

Wikipedia article, English. Consulted 2026-01-10 while preparing the second session of the spring 2026 class.

## Summary

Defines free indirect speech as rendering a character's own thoughts in the grammar of third-person narration, so that the narrator's voice and the character's merge without quotation or a reporting verb. Lists the alternative names, including free indirect discourse, free indirect style and the French *discours indirect libre*.

## Why It Matters

The neutral definition I cite in the class handout and in my own craft note, [[free-indirect-style]], which records how I use the technique for Nell and Tobias in The Salt Choir and why Constance gets letters instead. Students like having a reference that is not me.

## Caveats

An encyclopedia summary, good for the definition and the names. The craft judgement (when to use it, what goes wrong) is mine and lives in the Concept note.
