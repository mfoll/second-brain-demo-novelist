# Artist-in-residence

## Summary

Artist residencies are programmes in which a host organisation, institution or community gives artists space and resources for their practice; participants are called artists-in-residence or writers-in-residence. The article notes that contemporary residencies are increasingly thematic, with artists working with the host towards a particular outcome.

## Why It Matters

Read before applying to Ferrow House for [[writing-retreat-2026]], to see how far a residency expects something back. Ferrow House is the plain kind, space and time with a report afterwards, which suited a narrow goal. The thematic kind the article describes would not have; see [[residencies-are-for-finishing-not-starting]].

## Key Details

- Residencies range widely; there is no single model.
- Thematic residencies expect an outcome tied to the host's theme.

## Caveats

General; says nothing about any particular house.
