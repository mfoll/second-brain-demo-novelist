# Tidewrack Panel Brief 2026

[[kwabena-osei|Kwabena Osei]]'s email to the three panellists on 2026-06-08 with his running order and seven questions.

## Key Details

- Running order: ten-minute openings, thirty minutes chaired, fifteen for the floor.
- His questions, paraphrased: does translation train the ear for period voice; is historical dialogue a translation from a lost language; when is archaism honest; who owns a dialect; what does a translator hide that a novelist must show; the translator's name on the cover; what each of us is reading.
- He asked us to bring one short passage each, under a minute.

## Why It Matters

My preparation in [[tidewrack-panel-notes]] answered four of these. On the day he asked mostly others, which was the better panel.
