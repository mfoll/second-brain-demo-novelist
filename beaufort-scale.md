# Beaufort scale

## Summary

The Beaufort scale relates wind speed to observed conditions at sea or on land. It was devised in 1805 by Francis Beaufort, a Royal Navy hydrographer, adopted by the Royal Navy and later spread internationally.

## Why It Matters

Read for the [[storm-chapter-research-pack]]. I describe the November 1927 gale to myself in Beaufort terms so that the sea in chapters 21 to 23 rises consistently through the evening. The characters never name a force number; they describe the sea, which is what the scale was built on.

## Key Details

- Wind force judged by observed effects, not instruments.
- In naval use from the nineteenth century.
