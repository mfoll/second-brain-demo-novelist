# Salt Choir Contract

The publishing agreement between me and Larkspur & Venn for The Salt Choir (working title), negotiated by [[callum-pryce|Callum Pryce]] and signed on 2025-03-18. This note holds the terms that drive decisions; the signed PDF and the variation stay in the contracts folder.

## Summary

> [!info]
> Advance 24,000 GBP in three tranches of 8,000 GBP: on signature, on delivery and acceptance, on hardback publication. Original delivery date 2026-09-30, moved to 2027-03-31 by the variation of 2026-08-28. Option on my next novel, running from acceptance.

## Key Details

- **Parties:** me as author; Larkspur & Venn as publisher. Agent: Halloran Pryce Agency, which receives all payments and deducts commission under the [[halloran-pryce-agency-agreement]].
- **Work:** a novel of about 100,000 to 110,000 words, provisionally titled The Salt Choir, described in the proposal as literary historical fiction set in a 1920s coastal town. Target in my own plan: 105,000 words.
- **Advance:** 24,000 GBP, double the [[lantern-hours-contract|Lantern Hours advance]].
  - Tranche 1, on signature: 8,000 GBP, paid through the agency in April 2025.
  - Tranche 2, on delivery and acceptance: 8,000 GBP. Was due after 2026-09-30; now after 2027-03-31.
  - Tranche 3, on first publication in hardback: 8,000 GBP. No publication date yet; realistically 2028.
- **Delivery:** complete manuscript by 2026-09-30 (original). Acceptance within 60 days of delivery, or a written editorial report. The variation signed 2026-08-28 moved delivery to 2027-03-31 and moved tranche 2 with it; all other terms unchanged ([[delivery-date-extension]]).
- **Royalties:** hardback 10% of RRP rising to 12.5% after 5,000 copies; paperback 7.5% of RRP; ebook 25% of net receipts. Same as Lantern Hours except the hardback escalator.
- **Rights granted:** English language, world. Translation rights to the publisher with the author taking 80%, as on Lantern Hours. Audio and dramatic rights reserved to me.
- **Option:** Larkspur & Venn have an exclusive option on my next novel. The window is 60 days from acceptance of The Salt Choir, on an outline and a sample chapter. See [[option-clause]], [[when-does-the-option-clause-start-and-end]] and [[third-book-option]].
- **Out of print:** reversion on written request if fewer than 150 copies sold in two consecutive statements.

## Why It Matters

This contract is the spine of the next two years. The braided rewrite after the [[editorial-call-2026-02-19|editorial call of February 2026]] cost about five months, so the original delivery date could not hold, and because tranche 2 is paid on acceptance, moving the date moved 8,000 GBP out of 2026. That is the gap in [[cash-flow-forecast-2026]] from November 2026 to April 2027, and the reason for the [[vellacott-grant-application]] and for the temptation in [[should-i-take-the-ashlar-translation-commission]].

The option clause matters more than I first understood. It starts on acceptance, not on publication, so the outline for book three has to exist by spring 2027.

## Caveats

- Tranche 2 depends on acceptance, not delivery. A long editorial report could push it later than April 2027; the forecast assumes payment within 30 days of acceptance and acceptance within 30 days of delivery, which is optimistic.
- Royalty rates above are from my notes of the signed copy, rechecked on 2026-08-28 when the variation arrived. Check the PDF before quoting them to anyone.
