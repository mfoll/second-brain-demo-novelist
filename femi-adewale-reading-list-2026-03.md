# Femi Adewale Reading List 2026-03

A short list [[femi-adewale|Femi]] sent after our call: six items on the experience of war neuroses after 1918, weighted towards men's own accounts and letters rather than medical texts, plus two novels of the period that he thinks handle it well.

## Summary

I have read four of the six. The most useful were the collections of first-hand accounts: they gave me the shape of a bad day, and the words men used for it, which is what Tobias's chapters needed. [[mrs-dalloway]] is on his list for Septimus Smith, which I had not thought of as research.

## Why it matters

Background for [[tobias-arden]]. Not cited in the book; nothing from it is quoted.
