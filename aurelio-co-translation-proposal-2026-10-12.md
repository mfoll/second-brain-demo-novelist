- Fee split equally: 4,750 GBP each, each half paid half on signature and half on delivery.
- Aurelio starts in January on the first half; I start my half on 1 April 2027, after delivery of The Salt Choir. He estimates seven weeks for my share at my old pace; I think eight.
- Joint revision in June; delivery 30 June 2027.
- Both names on the title page, alphabetical; he is happy to be second.
- A one-page agreement between us on credit, split and what happens if one drops out.

## Why It Matters

Turns the commission from a threat to the novel into a seven- or eight-week job after delivery: option 3 in [[ashlar-commission-options]]. It depends on Ashlar ([[is-co-translation-credit-acceptable-to-ashlar]]).

## Caveats
