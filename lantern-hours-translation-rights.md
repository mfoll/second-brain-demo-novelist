# Lantern Hours Translation Rights

Selling Lantern Hours into other languages. Larkspur & Venn hold translation rights under the [[lantern-hours-contract]] and [[sigrun-aas|Sigrun Aas]] sells them; I keep 80% of the income, credited to my royalty account.

## Outcome

At least two translation deals for Lantern Hours. Italian rights sold to Casa Velluto on 2026-03-09; a second language still open.

## Current State

- Italian: Casa Velluto, signed 2026-03-09, advance 1,500 GBP, Larkspur & Venn keep 20% ([[casa-velluto-italian-rights-deal-2026-03-09]], [[casa-velluto-rights-agreement-2026-03]]). The 1,200 GBP share is in [[lantern-hours-royalty-statement-2026-10]].
- [[giulia-rinaldi|Giulia Rinaldi]] is editing; translator appointed, publication planned for spring 2027.
- Second language: a German publisher has had the paperback since July; a Portuguese reader's report is due in November ([[sigrun-aas-rights-update-2026-09]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Translation rights only. Audio is [[audiobook-edition]]; English-language territories are covered by the main contract.

## Working Context

A former translator selling her own book into translation is an odd position. I can read the Portuguese and French reports myself, which helps, and I have to stop myself offering to vet the translator. The rule I agreed with Sigrun at the [[rights-strategy-call-2025-09-04|strategy call]]: I answer translators' questions when asked, and I never comment on a sample unless the publisher asks me to. The pitch that worked for Casa Velluto was the translation theme itself, set out in [[lantern-hours-rights-guide-2025]].
