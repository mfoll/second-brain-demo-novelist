# Option Clause

A clause giving a publisher the first right to consider, and usually to make an offer for, the author's next book, for a fixed period, before anyone else may see it.

## How it works

- **Trigger.** The window opens on an event named in the contract: delivery, acceptance or publication of the current book. Mine opens on acceptance ([[salt-choir-contract]]).
- **What the author submits.** Varies from "the next work" to a full manuscript. Mine is an outline and a sample chapter.
- **Exclusivity period.** Commonly 30 to 90 days. Mine is 60.
- **What happens next.** If the publisher offers and the author declines, a good clause frees the author; a bad one ("matching rights", or "on the same terms") ties her. Callum struck a matching right from the first draft of the Salt Choir contract.

## What I got wrong

I assumed an option was a promise of a contract. It is only a promise to look first. It protects the publisher, and it protects the author only if she arrives with something worth looking at, which is the point of [[third-book-option]].
