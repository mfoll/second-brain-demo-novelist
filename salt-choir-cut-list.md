# Salt Choir Cut List

What has been cut from The Salt Choir across drafts, with rough word counts and why. The cut text is in the Scrivener Cuts folder ([[salt-choir-cuts-folder]]); this list is the index, so I stop re-reading old material looking for something I half remember.

## From draft 1 (cut in the braid, February to March 2026)

- Nell at the lifeboat house during crew meetings, four scenes, about 9,000 words. She had no reason to be there.
- Nell at the inquiry, about 6,500 words. Replaced by Tobias in draft 2.
- Nell's walk-out with the postmaster's nephew, about 4,000 words. Charming, pointless.
- The 1926 regatta chapter, about 5,000 words.

## From draft 2 (cut in the third draft, July 2026 onwards)

- All six storm-night interludes, about 11,000 words, minus what went into the prologue.
- Tobias's war fragments outside chapters 7 and 17, about 7,500 words. Some lines moved into the two war scenes.
- Constance's letter about the vicarage furniture, about 2,000 words. Funny; did nothing.
- Orientation passages at the head of braided chapters, about 3,000 words.

## Things I keep wanting back

- The regatta. Probably as two paragraphs in chapter 11.
- One image from the war fragments: the tin of boiled sweets. Now in chapter 17.

## Running totals

About 24,500 words cut from draft 1 material, about 23,500 from draft 2. Third draft target 105,000 ([[how-long-should-the-third-draft-be]]).
