# How much of Tobias's war goes on the page?

## Question

Tobias's shell shock shapes his chapters. How much of the war itself should the reader see, and in what form?

## Current Answer

Two whole remembered scenes, in chapters 7 (December 1924) and 17 (August 1927), each triggered by something in the present and told complete. Elsewhere the war shows only in his behaviour and in the narration's distance from him ([[narrative-distance]]). Answered at the [[draft-two-review-2026-07-08|July review]].

## Why

- In draft 2 the war came in fragments across three timelines and readers lost track of it.
- [[femi-adewale]] advised that men in his position had good and bad periods, and that whole recurring memories are truer to accounts of the time than constant flashes ([[femi-adewale-notes-on-tobias-2026-03-24]]).
- Fewer, longer scenes cost fewer words: about 7,500 cut ([[salt-choir-cut-list]]).

## Caveats

Chapter 17 sits ten weeks before the storm. Imogen may find it too pointed. Watch it in the read-through.
