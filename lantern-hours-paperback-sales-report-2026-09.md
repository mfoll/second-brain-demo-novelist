# Lantern Hours Paperback Sales Report 2026-09

[[martin-okello|Martin Okello]]'s sales summary for the paperback to 2026-08-31, sent through Imogen on 2026-09-09.

## Key Details

- First printing 8,000; reprint of 3,000 ordered in July.
- Shipped net of returns to 2026-06-30: about 5,800 copies (4,620 at home discounts, 1,180 at high discount). To 2026-08-31: about 7,900.
- Through tills (sell-through panel) to 2026-08-31: about 6,300. The difference is stock on shelves and in warehouses, some of which will come back.
- Hardback still selling about 40 copies a month.
- Martin's comment: the reading group guide and the bookshop promotion did more than reviews, as Priyanka predicted.

## Why It Matters

These are the sales behind the earn-out on the statement to 2026-06-30 ([[lantern-hours-royalty-statement-2026-10]], [[lantern-hours-earn-out-check]]). The gap between shipments and tills is why the publisher holds a reserve against returns; see [[sell-through]].

## Caveats

Panel figures miss some independent shops and all events. The royalty statement, not this report, is the record for money.
