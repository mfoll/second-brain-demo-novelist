# How Big A Reserve Against Returns Is Reasonable?

The Lantern Hours statement to 2026-06-30 held back 781.99 GBP, 20% of the period's paperback royalties, as a reserve against returns. Is that normal, and when does it come back?

## Why It Mattered

It is the difference between 1,140 GBP and about 1,920 GBP payable in October, in the month the cash gap starts. If the reserve was unusual, the agency should challenge it.

## Current Answer

Normal. [[bethan-okafor]] compared it with three other Larkspur & Venn clients on 2026-10-09 ([[earn-out-call-with-bethan-2026-10-09]]): 20% on the first paperback period, 10% on the next, then released as returns are known. The Lantern Hours contract allows a "reasonable reserve" without a stated cap, which she says is common and which the agency now tries to cap at 25% in new contracts.

## What I Do With It

- Forecast nothing from the reserve until a statement shows it released ([[advances-arrive-late]] applies to reserves too).
- Check the release line on the statement to 2026-12-31, expected April 2027, using [[reconcile-a-royalty-statement]].
- For the Salt Choir contract, ask Callum whether the cap is in it. (He says it is: 25%.)
