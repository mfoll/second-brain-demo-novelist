# Copy Editing

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2026-08-28), read on 2026-02-16.

## Summary

Copy editing revises written material for accuracy, consistency, grammar and style. In print publishing it comes before typesetting, and proofreading follows on the typeset pages. The article quotes a style manual describing the range from mechanical correction to substantial work on clarity and style.

## Why It Matters

Looked up while answering [[joan-whitlock|Joan Whitlock]]'s paperback queries ([[paperback-proof-corrections-2026]]), because I wanted to know what a proofreader may reasonably change at proof stage and what belongs to the copy-edit. Proof stage is for errors, not style: that is why I declined her six queries on deliberate repetitions without guilt. For The Salt Choir the dialect style sheet has to exist at the copy-edit, not arrive at proof.
