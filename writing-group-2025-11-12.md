# Writing Group 2025-11-12

Monthly writing group, second Wednesday. The group read chapter 1 of the first draft (Nell's last day on the curing yards, March 1924, about 4,200 words).

## Responses

- [[teodora-vlasic|Teodora]]: the gutting scene is the best opening I have shown them; the second half of the chapter, where Nell walks home and introduces the town, is a guidebook.
- [[marcus-ellery|Marcus]]: nothing happens that makes him need chapter 2. He wanted a hook about the sea; there is none until chapter 5.
- [[sunniva-brekke|Sunniva]] read the first page aloud and cut it by a third in pencil as she went.

## What I took

- Cut the walk home to a page; the town comes in by being used, not described.
- Marcus's "nothing about the sea" was the first form of the problem the prologue now tries to solve ([[should-the-salt-choir-keep-the-storm-prologue]]). I did not see that then.
