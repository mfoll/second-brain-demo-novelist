# Saltmarrow Timeline Spreadsheet

Month-by-month timeline of The Salt Choir from January 1924 to December 1929: one row per month, columns for each viewpoint character, the town, the herring season, the choir, the lifeboat and chapters.

## Summary

Built in February 2025 for the first draft; rebuilt in July 2026 for the chronological third draft; last edited 2026-09-28 when chapter 19 became a Constance letter. Ages compute from birth years on a second tab, which is how age errors are caught.

## Why It Matters

The detail behind the year-level [[saltmarrow-chronology]]. When the draft and the bible disagree on a date, this decides.
