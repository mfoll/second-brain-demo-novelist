# Braided Structure Rewrite

The second draft of The Salt Choir, rebuilt as a nonlinear braid of three points of view that opened on the night of the November 1927 storm. Superseded by [[salt-choir-third-draft]] after the review of 2026-07-08.

## Outcome

Draft 2 rebuilt as a nonlinear braid of three points of view opening on the 1927 storm; superseded by the third draft after the review of 2026-07-08. The draft was finished and sent on 2026-06-12 (about 118,000 words), then partly reversed.

## Current State

> [!warning]
> Superseded. Do not use the braided chapter order or the storm-first opening as the plan. The three viewpoints and Constance's letter chapters survived; the chronology did not. Current plan: [[salt-choir-third-draft]].

- What came out of it and stayed: [[tobias-arden]] and [[constance-leal]] as viewpoint characters; Constance's chapters as letters ([[letter-chapters]]); the rewritten storm chapters; a four-page prologue cut down from the old storm opening ([[storm-prologue]]).
- What was dropped: the braid itself. Chapter order kept in [[braided-chapter-map]] (archived).

## Scope and Boundaries

This was a structural rewrite, not a line edit. Research corrections that happened during it (the motor lifeboat, see [[saltmarrow-anachronism-log]]) belong to [[saltmarrow-research]].

## Working Context

Why it started: [[imogen-lacey|Imogen]]'s [[imogen-editorial-letter-2026-01-30|editorial letter]] said the storm arrived too late and Nell alone could not carry the town. On the [[editorial-call-2026-02-19|editorial call of 19 February]] I proposed, and Imogen accepted, a braid: open on the storm night, then three strands moving outward in time from it, one per viewpoint, meeting again at the memorial.

How it went:

- 2026-02-23 to 2026-03-20: replanned in Scrivener; 44 chapters in a braid of three.
- 2026-03-26: [[femi-adewale]] read the Tobias chapters ([[femi-adewale-reading-call-2026-03-26]]).
- 2026-04-08: the writing group read the storm opening; [[marcus-ellery]] liked it, two people could not tell which year Tobias was in ([[writing-group-2026-04-08]]).
- 2026-05-12 to 2026-05-14: the motor lifeboat turned out to be wrong; storm chapters rewritten for a ten-oar pulling and sailing boat ([[would-saltmarrows-lifeboat-have-had-an-engine-in-1927]]).
- 2026-06-12: draft 2 sent, about 118,000 words ([[draft-two-sent-2026-06-12]]).
- 2026-07-08: review; chronological order restored ([[draft-two-review-2026-07-08]]).

Why it was reversed is the main thing worth keeping: telling the storm first drained its suspense, and readers lost track of Tobias's war when it arrived in pieces across three timelines. See [[salt-choir-structure-history]] and [[suspense-versus-surprise]].
