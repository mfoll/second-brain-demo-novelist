# Should The Delivery Tranche Move Or Be Split?

When delivery of The Salt Choir moved six months, should the 8,000 GBP delivery tranche move with it, or could part of it be paid on a partial delivery?

## Why It Mattered

A split would have put about 4,000 GBP (3,400 net) into the gap months and made the [[vellacott-grant-application]] and the Ashlar question far less pressing.

## Options

1. Move the whole tranche with the date.
2. Split: half on delivery of part one (about 40,000 words, forecast October 2026), half on full delivery and acceptance.
3. Keep the tranche on the old date regardless of delivery. Not seriously proposed; no publisher pays on acceptance of a book it has not received.

## Current Answer

Option 1, because option 2 was refused. [[callum-pryce]] asked on 2026-07-24; Larkspur & Venn declined on 2026-08-06. Their contracts pay on delivery and acceptance of a complete manuscript, and Imogen did not want to accept part one of a draft whose shape had already changed twice. Fair, if inconvenient. The variation moves the whole tranche ([[salt-choir-contract-variation-2026-08-28]]).

## For Next Time

Ask for a three-way split on delivery (half on delivery, half on acceptance) in the next contract, so a late acceptance does not hold the whole sum. Noted for [[third-book-option]].
