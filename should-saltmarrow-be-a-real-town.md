# Should Saltmarrow Be A Real Town?

Superseded on 2025-01-20 by the decision to invent the town, recorded in the [[saltmarrow-town-bible]] and argued in [[invented-towns-are-more-honest-than-real-ones]]. Kept for the reasoning.

## The question as it stood in January 2025

Set the novel in a real herring port, with real streets and a real station, or invent one?

## Arguments for a real town, now rejected

- Readers who know the place would trust the book.
- The archive would answer questions directly.

## Why it was rejected

- A real town has a real lifeboat history and real families. Inventing a loss in a real place would be an injury to people still living there.
- I would spend the book correcting myself against the place instead of writing.
- A translator's instinct: I am better at making a voice plausible than at being accurate about a street.
