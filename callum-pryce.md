# Callum Pryce

My literary agent at the Halloran Pryce Agency since 2022, when he took Lantern Hours on from a partial. He sold both novels to Larkspur & Venn and negotiated the [[salt-choir-contract]] and its variation.

## Role

Agent: sells and negotiates, reads every draft before or alongside my editor, and tells me when I am about to make a business mistake. Contracts and royalty statements go through [[bethan-okafor]] in his office.

## How we work together

- Video call roughly monthly, more around a draft. He prefers calls to email for anything with a judgement in it and follows up with a short email so there is a record.
- He reads as a reader, not an editor: fast, in one or two sittings, and reports what he felt, chapter by chapter. Useful precisely because he does not try to fix things.
- He disagrees openly and does not sulk when overruled. He also does not drop a point he believes in.
