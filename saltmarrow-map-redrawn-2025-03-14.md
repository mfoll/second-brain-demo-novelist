# Saltmarrow Map Redrawn

On 2025-03-14 I redrew the map of Saltmarrow because the first version put the lifeboat house inside the harbour, and the harbour mouth faces north-east, into the storm.

## Why

Reading the general background on [[lifeboat-rescue]] made me ask where a station would put its boat if the harbour could not be left in the worst weather. The answer, a beach launch from a slipway at the sheltered end of the bay, changes the storm: a slower launch, a horse team, the whole town on the beach. It also gives the lifeboat house its own spot at the west end of the Strand, away from the curing yards.

## What changed

- New map, version 2: [[saltmarrow-map-sketch]]. Version 1 archived: [[saltmarrow-map-sketch-v1]].
- [[saltmarrow-town-bible]] updated with the bar, the beach slipway and the west end of the Strand.
- The first-draft storm chapter rewritten around a beach launch; still with an engine, which took another fourteen months to remove ([[would-saltmarrows-lifeboat-have-had-an-engine-in-1927]]).
