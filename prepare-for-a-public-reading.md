# Prepare For A Public Reading

What I do before and after a reading, talk or panel. Written after the hardback launch, revised after each paperback reading.

## Inputs

- The booking: venue, date, time, host, format, expected audience, whether a bookseller is present ([[bookshop-reading-schedule-2026]]).
- The current [[reading-script-lantern-hours]].

## Steps

1. **A week before:** confirm stock with the host. Library events need a bookseller; ask which shop is supplying and how many copies.
2. **Choose the passages** for the audience: translators, general readers, or a group that has already read the book. Send the choice to the host if they introduce me.
3. **Time it aloud.** Eleven minutes maximum for a reading. Panels: eight minutes for an opening.
4. **Prepare the translation answer.** It always comes. One concrete story, not a theory.
5. **Re-read [[what-i-say-in-public-about-book-two]].** One sentence about the next book, no more.
6. **Pack:** marked-up reading copy, two pens that write on coated paper, receipts envelope, train times both ways.
7. **On the night:** arrive 40 minutes early; ask the bookseller what is selling and who is coming.
8. **After:** add a line to [[lantern-hours-publicity-ledger]] (attendance, copies), receipts into the envelope, a thank-you email to the bookseller the next day, and a change to the script if anything fell flat.

## Verification

The ledger has a line for every event, and every rail receipt has gone to Tariq within a month.
